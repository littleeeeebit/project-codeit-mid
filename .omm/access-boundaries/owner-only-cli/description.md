Settlement, reconciliation, adjustments, the paid switch, envelopes and the audit log are CLI-only (`unresolved`, `settle`, `reconcile`, `adjust`, `paid`, `audit`), each requiring `--actor` and a reason. Through the screens a member may change only the shared cap (`set_budget_limit`). Sealed test gold rows are reviewed only through the owner's `gold show/decide/second-review`; `service.gold_decide` refuses them, and also refuses approval by whoever requested the drafting run.
