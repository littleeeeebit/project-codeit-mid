`GatewayOwner` holds `pg_try_advisory_lock(GATEWAY_LOCK)` on a dedicated connection; the lock is database-wide, not per data directory. A second process gets `LockHeld` → `GatewayLockError` ('another process already owns the paid gateway'). `borrow_owner` lets more `Resources` in the same process share it with reference counting (`PinnedResources`, tests). `check()` is called by `OpenAITransport` before every call, and `owner_guard` inside every reserve and dispatch transaction. Losing the lock marks the owner lost, and every later dispatch is refused until restart and reconciliation.
