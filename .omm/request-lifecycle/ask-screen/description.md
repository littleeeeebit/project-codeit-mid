`useConversation.ask` in `ask-page.tsx` posts `{scope: [{doc_id, source_hash}], question, mode, previous_request_id}` through `postAsk`. While the latest turn is queued or running, the composer is disabled, so a conversation has at most one unfinished turn. Leaving the conversation (new documents, a new scope or 새 대화) or unmounting the page calls `release()`, which sends `POST /api/requests/{id}/abandon` with `keepalive`. `TurnView` polls its own request and opens the first citation of the latest answer in the evidence pane.
