When `_conversation` returns history, `_rewrite` sends `build_rewrite_messages(history, question, titles)` through `_metered_chat(stage='query_rewrite', max_output_tokens=1500)`. That is a separate paid attempt on the same request. `validate_rewrite` yields the standalone retrieval question, recorded as `trace.rewrite.query` and `result.standalone_question` (shown on screen as 검색에 쓴 질문). An unusable output ends the request `technical_error`; it is billed and never retried. The previous answer's cited units (up to `CARRIED_EVIDENCE` 8) are re-supplied after the fresh retrieval by `_with_carried` and renumbered. Units whose chunk left the serving index are dropped.
