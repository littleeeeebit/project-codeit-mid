`_execute` opens the trace, and `_paid_answer` does the work. A follow-up is first rewritten by `_rewrite` (stage `query_rewrite`, 1500 output tokens). `prepare_answer` or `_prepare_compare` retrieves and appends up to 8 carried evidence units from the previous answer (`_with_carried`). `_priced` builds messages with `generation.build_messages`, counts tokens (`count_request_tokens`) and estimates the cost, adding a query-embedding estimate on a cache miss. `_generate` streams the content, then `_validated` checks the citations.
