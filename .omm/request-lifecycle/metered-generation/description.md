`_generate` calls `_metered_chat(stage=generation, max_output_tokens=4000, on_delta=streamed)`, where `streamed` writes the accumulated content into `res.partials[request_id]`. The steps are `budget.reserve` (purpose `interactive`; a refusal becomes `budget_blocked` or `clarification_required`), `paid_refusal` (no key: release, then `technical_error`), `mark_dispatching` with `_dispatch_guard` (cancel or shutdown: `_Stop`; ledger refusal: `budget_blocked`), then `_call_and_settle` inside the `generate-answer` trace generation step. A provider error either releases (pre-execution) or marks unknown, and ends `technical_error` with the cost kept pending.
