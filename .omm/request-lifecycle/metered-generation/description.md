`_generate` calls `_metered_chat(stage='generation', step='generate-answer', on_delta=streamed)`. The steps are: `budget.reserve`, refused when it returns `budget_blocked` or `above_consented_maximum`; `paid_refusal()` (no key releases the reservation); `mark_dispatching` with `_dispatch_guard`; `transport.chat` streaming deltas into `res.partials[request_id]`; then `settle`, or `release` on a pre-execution failure, or `mark_unknown`. The model, key session and billing scope come from the contextvars bound when the member asked.
