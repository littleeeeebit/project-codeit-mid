`api.ask` passes the scope pairs, question, mode, today's date as `as_of` (the 기준일 control was removed) and `previous_request_id` to `service.ask`. That creates a fresh UUID used as both `generation_id` and `idempotency_key`, calls `submit_answer`, and returns `{request_id, generation_id, target}`, where `target` is `target_key` (sha256 of scope, question, mode, date and previous turn). The pydantic `AskIn` caps the scope at 2 documents and limits mode to `single|compare|corpus|metadata|inventory`.
