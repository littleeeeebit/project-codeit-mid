`_metered_chat` reserves the maximum (`budget.reserve`; refusal → `budget_blocked` or `clarification_required`), checks `res.paid_refusal()` (no key → release), and marks the attempt dispatching under `_dispatch_guard`. It then calls `transport.chat` with `on_delta` writing `res.partials[request_id]`, and settles from usage. Missing usage or a settlement failure leaves the attempt `unknown`, and nothing retries. `validate_answer` then checks citations against the stored quotes.
