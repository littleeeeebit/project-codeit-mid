`_finish`, in one immediate transaction, reads the request's attempts to derive `billing_state` (precedence unknown > pending > reconciled > settled > released). It keeps a status that is already `cancelled` or `interrupted`, and turns `completed` into `cancelled` if a cancel was requested. It writes `status`, `trace_json` and `result_json` (without the listing) and upserts `request_listings`, then drops `res.partials`. Readers: `GET /api/requests/{id}` (`request_status` → `_view`, owner or verifier only, plus `attachable`), the SSE stream (`answer_progress`: partial only while running, uncancelled and owned by this `generation_id`), evidence (`open_evidence`), and history (`list_requests`). `_trace_outcome` scores `insufficient_evidence` and `evidence_tokens` on the Langfuse trace.
