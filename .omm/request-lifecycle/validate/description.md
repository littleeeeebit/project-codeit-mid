`service._validated` wraps `generation.validate_answer` in the `validate-answer` guardrail step and passes the stored quotes. A `TechnicalError` keeps up to 4,000 characters of raw output in the trace and ends the request as `technical_error`: the cost is recorded and nothing is retried. On success, `_merged_missing` adds the server's missing fields (`ingestion_unavailable`, `not_found_in_context`) for documents the model did not mention.
