`prepare_answer` → `service.retrieve` (activated mode; a query-embedding miss may be paid here as its own `embedding` attempt) → `_with_carried` → `_priced`. `_priced` builds messages (`generation.build_messages` with conversation and limitations), counts tokens exactly (`count_request_tokens`: o200k_base, per-message overhead, schema, margin) and estimates the maximum reservation. The trace records the ranked chunks and the packed evidence.
