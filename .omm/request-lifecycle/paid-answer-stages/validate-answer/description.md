`generation.validate_answer` raises `TechnicalError` on refusal, `length` truncation or an incomplete finish, on schema-invalid JSON, on unknown evidence ids, on evidence from another document (inferences in compare mode may cite both sides but must cite their own), on a quote that no longer matches the stored chunk body (`_stored_quote`), on claims or conflicts without evidence, on absence claims not listed in `missing_fields`, on `source_absence_verified` (top-k cannot prove absence), on `answered` without claims or summary evidence, and on a missing comparison side. Inline `[E1]` ids are stripped from the text. A failure stores `raw_output` in the trace and finishes as technical_error ('비용은 기록되었으며 자동 재시도는 하지 않습니다'). A citation-class failure scores `citation_valid=0`.
