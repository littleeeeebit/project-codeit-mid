`_finish` writes status, `trace_json` and `result_json` under the immediate transaction. It derives `billing_state` and `attempt_ids` from the ledger, maps a 'completed' outcome on a cancel-requested row to 'cancelled', and keeps an 'interrupted' or 'cancelled' status written by shutdown or cancel. It then removes the streamed partial. `_trace_outcome` writes the answer and the free scores to Langfuse.
