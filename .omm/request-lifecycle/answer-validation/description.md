`generation.validate_answer(response, evidence, allowed_doc_ids, stored_quotes, required_doc_ids)` rejects a refusal, `output_truncated` (finish_reason length), incomplete output and schema-invalid JSON. It requires every claim's evidence ID to exist and belong to the claim's document; a compare inference may also cite the other required side but must cite its own. Every cited quote must equal the stored chunk body (`_stored_quote`). `absence` claims are dropped only when they restate a listed missing field. Missing fields must stay in scope, `source_absence_verified` is refused because top-k misses cannot establish absence, `answered` needs at least one claim, and compare needs both sides covered. A failure scores `citation_valid=0` for citation errors and returns technical_error with the raw output kept in the trace (the cost stays recorded). On success, server-detected missing fields (`ingestion_unavailable`, `not_found_in_context`) are merged in and `done(payload.status, …)` persists the result.
