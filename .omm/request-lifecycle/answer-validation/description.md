`generation.validate_answer` rejects any of the following: refusal; truncated output (`finish_reason=length`); schema errors; unknown evidence IDs; evidence from another document (except comparison inferences that also cite their own side); quotes that differ from the stored chunk (`_stored_quote`); claims, conflicts or an `answered` summary without evidence; out-of-scope documents; `source_absence_verified` (top-k misses cannot prove absence); and a comparison that omits a side. Absence claims are kept only when they restate a listed missing field. Inline `[E1]` markers are stripped from the text. The Langfuse score `citation_valid` is recorded. A failure becomes `technical_error` with `raw_output` kept in the trace; the call was billed and is not retried.
