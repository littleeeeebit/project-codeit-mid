`generation.validate_answer` (generation.py:506) parses the structured output and checks every evidence ID against the packed units, every claim's document against the allowed scope, quotes against the stored chunk text (`_stored_quote`), and that every claim, conflict and summary cites evidence. In compare mode both represented documents must appear. On failure it raises TechnicalError with one of CITATION_ERRORS. The request then fails as `technical_error`; the cost is already recorded and nothing is retried, and the raw output (first 4,000 characters) goes into the trace. On success, server-side missing fields (`not_found_in_context`, `ingestion_unavailable`) are merged, and `_finish` stores `result_json` and `trace_json`. `_finish` derives `billing_state` from the attempts with the precedence unknown > pending > reconciled > settled > released.
