generation.validate_answer rejects refusal, truncation, schema errors, unknown evidence IDs, citations to another document, quote mismatches against the stored chunk body (_stored_quote), uncited claims or conflicts, retrieval-claimed absence, answered-without-claims and a summary without evidence. For compare it also rejects a missing side. Inline [E#] markers are stripped. Failure stores technical_error with raw output in trace_json. Success merges server-side missing fields and calls done(), and _finish writes status, trace_json and result_json (including the evidence map) and pops the partial.
