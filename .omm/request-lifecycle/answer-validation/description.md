`_validated` loads the stored quote for every evidence unit (`_stored_quote`, from the serving index's chunk body) and calls `generation.validate_answer`. The payload must parse against the strict schema. Every claim, summary and conflict must cite allowed evidence IDs whose quotes match their scope. Compare answers must represent every side that had evidence (`required_doc_ids`). Citation failures (`CITATION_ERRORS`) score `citation_valid=0` in the trace. A validation failure keeps the usage billed, stores up to 4000 characters of raw output in the trace and returns `technical_error` without retrying.
