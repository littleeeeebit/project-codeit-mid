`_validated` maps each citable evidence ID to its text as stored now: the chunk in the serving index (`_stored_quote`) or the metadata statement (`current_statements`). It then runs `generation.validate_answer`. A refusal, truncation or schema violation is a `TechnicalError`. Absence claims become `missing_fields`. Further checks: status consistency (`status_problem`), every claim in scope with evidence of its own document (comparison inferences may also cite the other side), quotes that must still match, conflicts with two or more alternatives and a next action, no `source_absence_verified` from retrieval, an answered status needing claims and summary evidence, and both comparison sides covered. Inline `[E1]` markers are stripped. The Langfuse step `validate-answer` scores `citation_valid`. On failure the raw output (up to 4,000 characters) goes into `trace_json` and the request fails `technical_error`, with the cost recorded and no retry.
