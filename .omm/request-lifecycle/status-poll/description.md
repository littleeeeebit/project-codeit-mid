`usePoll` keyed `${request_id}/${generation_id}` calls `GET /api/requests/{id}?generation_id&target` every 1,000 ms and stops once the status is terminal. The route returns `RequestOut{view, attachable}`. `service.request_status` refuses members other than the owner unless the principal has `verifier`. `OwnedAnswer` shows the reserved maximum while queued or running. A non-attachable result is pointed to the history list instead of being rendered.
