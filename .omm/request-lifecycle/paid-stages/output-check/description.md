`generation.validate_answer` checks the output against the retrieved evidence, the allowed document IDs and the stored chunk bodies (`_stored_quote`), plus the represented documents in compare mode. A `TechnicalError` stores up to 4,000 characters of raw output in the trace and ends `technical_error` ('cost recorded, no automatic retry'). On success, model `missing_fields` are merged with server-detected ones, and the payload status, summary, claims, conflicts and `next_action` are stored with the evidence map.
