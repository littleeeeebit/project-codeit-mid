When `_conversation` returns history (up to `CONVERSATION_TURNS` earlier turns), `_rewrite` makes a paid `query_rewrite` call through `_metered_chat` (`REWRITE_PROMPT_VERSION`, at most 1500 output tokens). `validate_rewrite` checks the result, which then replaces the question for retrieval and is returned as `standalone_question`. A refused or unusable rewrite finishes the request as `budget_blocked` / `technical_error`; it is billed and never retried. The previous answer's cited evidence (`carried`, capped at `CARRIED_EVIDENCE`) is later appended after fresh retrieval by `_with_carried`, unless its chunk left the serving index.
