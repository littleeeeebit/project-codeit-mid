`_validated` calls `generation.validate_answer` inside a `validate-answer` guardrail span. It checks evidence IDs, scope, quotes against `_stored_quote` and per-document coverage for compare, and scores `citation_valid`. A validation `TechnicalError` ends as `technical_error`, with the raw output kept in the trace and the cost recorded. `_finish` reads the attempts (`_billing`: unknown > pending > reconciled > settled > released) and keeps an already interrupted or cancelled status. It turns `completed` into `cancelled` when `cancel_requested` is set, writes `trace_json` and `result_json`, and drops `res.partials[request_id]`.
