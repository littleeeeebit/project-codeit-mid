`generation.validate_answer` (in a `validate-answer` guardrail span) refuses a refusal, a truncated output (`finish_reason=length`), invalid JSON, unknown evidence IDs, citations of another document's evidence, a quote that differs from the stored chunk body (`_stored_quote`), claims without evidence, claims or missing fields outside the scope, `source_absence_verified` claims (top-k cannot prove absence), `answered` with no claims, and in compare mode a missing side. Absence claims are dropped only when they restate a listed missing field. Failure → `technical_error` (cost recorded, no retry), with `raw_output` kept in the trace. The Langfuse scores `citation_valid`, `insufficient_evidence` and `evidence_tokens` are recorded.
