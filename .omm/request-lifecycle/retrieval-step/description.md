`_paid_answer` resolves the scope and refuses documents that are not ingested. A follow-up first goes through `_rewrite`: a paid `query_rewrite` call produces the standalone question and is recorded in `trace.rewrite`. `_prepare_paid` picks `prepare_answer` (single/corpus), `_prepare_compare` (one scoped retrieval per document) or `_frozen_prep` (a verifier run's frozen evidence). `_with_carried` appends the previous answer's cited evidence. `_priced` builds the messages, counts tokens with tiktoken and estimates the maximum cost. No evidence ends the request as `insufficient_evidence` without a generation call.
