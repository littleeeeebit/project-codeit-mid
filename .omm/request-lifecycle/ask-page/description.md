`AskPage` posts `/api/ask` and stores the returned ownership. A turn is rendered by a component that polls `GET /api/requests/{id}?generation_id&target`. While the turn is live and not revoked it shows `LiveTurn` with the streamed provisional text (`Provisional`). Once finished it shows the validated `result` only when `attachable`. Otherwise the outcome stays in history. Leaving the conversation abandons the latest turn.
