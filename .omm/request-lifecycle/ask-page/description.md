`AskPage` holds `selected` (at most two `Doc`s from `DocumentSearch` → `GET /api/documents`), `mode`, `question` and the owned request. Any change of documents, mode or question calls `release()`. That invalidates a POST still in flight (`pending.current.valid=false`), clears ownership, and calls `POST /api/requests/{id}/abandon` with `keepalive: true` under the captured member name. If an ownership record arrives after the screen moved on, it is abandoned at once. While the request is queued or running, the textarea and submit button are disabled. 'Recent requests' (`GET /api/requests?limit=20`) reloads when the owned request finishes.
