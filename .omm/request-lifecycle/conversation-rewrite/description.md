For a follow-up, `_rewrite` builds `generation.build_rewrite_messages(history, question, titles)` and makes one paid `_metered_chat` call (stage `query_rewrite`, at most 1,500 output tokens). `validate_rewrite` checks the output. The standalone question then drives retrieval and the answer, and is stored as `standalone_question`. An unusable rewrite ends the request as `technical_error`; it is billed and never retried. The answer prompt also receives the conversation, and evidence the previous answer cited is appended after the fresh retrieval and renumbered (`_with_carried`).
