`_validated` runs `generation.validate_answer` against the retrieved evidence and stored quotes. A pass yields the payload's status, summary, claims, conflicts, next action, and missing fields merged with the server's (`_merged_missing`). `_finish` rolls the request's attempts up into `billing_state` (precedence unknown > pending > reconciled > settled > released) and keeps an existing `interrupted`/`cancelled` status rather than overwriting it. It also turns completed-with-`cancel_requested` into `cancelled`, writes `result_json` and `trace_json`, and drops `res.partials[request_id]`. `_trace_outcome` scores `insufficient_evidence` and `evidence_tokens`.
