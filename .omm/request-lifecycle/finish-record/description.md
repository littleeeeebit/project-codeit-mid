`_validated` checks citations and scope, then `done(...)` → `_finish` writes the final `status`, `trace_json` (config, retrieval, rewrite, admission, usage, settlement, billing attempts) and `result_json` (an `AnswerResult` with summary, claims, conflicts, missing fields, evidence map, coverage, limitations, `billing_state`, `attempt_ids`). It pops the partial. `_trace_outcome` writes the Langfuse output and scores.
