`_finish` derives `billing_state` from the request's attempts by precedence `unknown > pending > reconciled > settled > released`, where `reserved`/`dispatching` count as pending. It honours a `cancel_requested` flag that arrived during a completed run by storing `cancelled`, and writes `trace_json` (config snapshot, retrieval, admission, usage, settlement, billing) and `result_json`. `_view` exposes `reserved_micro_usd` (open attempts) and `settled_micro_usd` for the screen.
