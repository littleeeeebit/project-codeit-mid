`execute` runs `sys.executable -I -B worker.py <worktree> <stage> <inputs> <config> <output> <mode>` with cwd at the worktree and a 3,600 s timeout. The environment drops OPENAI_, GEMINI_, GOOGLE_API_, LANGFUSE_, TYPESAFE_ and HF_TOKEN variables and sets HF_HUB_OFFLINE / TRANSFORMERS_OFFLINE. PGOPTIONS forces read-only transactions for retriever, chunking and OCR estimate passes. Missing output becomes a failed candidate with the stderr tail.
