For generation and OCR, each candidate prices its own work without a key. `reprice` builds `estimate.json` at current ledger rates from recorded token bounds. `approve` records the approver after expiry (24 h) and envelope checks. `resume` re-checks the approval, inputs, worker, rates and budget, and that each ref has not moved since pricing, before `paid.approved_env` hands the key to that worker. Answers use idempotency keys `<run>:<cand>:<question>:<n>` in the `gold_eval` envelope, so billed items are never paid twice. A run stays `stopped` until every approved candidate completes.
