`evaluate-retrieval` and `compare` score frozen retrieval runs (K0, K1, D, H, HR, plus matrix rows) on the reviewed dev rows and the needle set against source spans. The results go to `.runtime/runs/<run_id>/` and `compare/tables`. `run-answers` scores paid answers per finalist. A person picks a row, and `activate_decision` switches serving. Nothing activates automatically or on a schedule.
