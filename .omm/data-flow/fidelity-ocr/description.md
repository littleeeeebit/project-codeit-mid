`fidelity run` has Hancom Viewer print each HWP to PDF (Windows, with 'Microsoft Print to PDF' as the default printer), then compares extraction and print both ways: 6-character shingles, ruled-table cells, and exact runs of 3 or more digits. The verdict is `auto_verified` or `auto_flagged`, with locations; a human `sample_checked` or `reviewed` status is never overwritten. The verify screen's fidelity tab shows the flagged spots (GET /api/verify/fidelity, rendered page PNGs) and records confirmations. `ocr` reads image regions with PaddleOCR-VL on the GPU and re-reads only flagged regions with gemini-3.5-flash-lite under a $0.50 cap in its own ledger. The cache is per region under `.runtime/ocr/<hash>/`, and the next `ingest` merges it.
