OCR (`cli ocr`, corpus/ocr.py) reads embedded HWP pictures and PDF images with PaddleOCR-VL on the GPU. Flagged regions are re-read by gpt-5-mini from the `ocr` envelope and cached per region, and the next ingest merges the text as `image_text` elements. Fidelity (corpus/fidelity.py) has Hancom Viewer print each HWP to PDF as an independent witness, compares 6-character shingles and digit runs in both directions, and sets `auto_verified` or `auto_flagged`. A person's `sample_checked` (confirm_fidelity) is never overwritten by an automatic verdict.
