`answer` converts a stored `result_json` and `trace_json` into the generation view's answer shape. For a `technical_error` whose error prefix is a validation code, it shows the refused answer parsed from `trace.raw_output`; since that is capped at 4,000 chars, it may be partial-parsed via `pydantic_core.from_json` and flagged `rejected_cut`. Every field is treated as untrusted. `checked` (an answer call was sent: a generation attempt in dispatching, settled, unknown or reconciled) decides `passed` versus `not_validated`. Cost is the sum of settled micro-USD from the trace's billing list.
