`cli manifest` → `ingestion.import_manifest` reads `RFP_SOURCE_DIR/data_list.csv` (default `<repo>/원본 데이터`). It normalizes typed metadata with explicit unknown, zero and conflict flags, hashes each original in `files/`, and writes the `documents` (one per CSV association, `doc_id_for(filename)`) and `sources` (one per unique SHA-256) rows. Byte-identical associations share one source and extraction but keep their own metadata. `resolve-metadata` records a canonical value with rationale and evidence; `_doc_rows` exposes it as `effective`, while `meta` keeps the CSV value.
