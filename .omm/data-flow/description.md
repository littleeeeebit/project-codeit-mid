This is how corpus bytes become cited evidence. Originals in `RFP_SOURCE_DIR` (read-only) are hashed into `sources` and `documents`. They are parsed into versioned extractions (JSONL artifacts plus `elements` rows), OCR text is merged in, and fidelity and reviews are recorded. They are chunked into a keyword index, embedded into pgvector sets, scored in frozen runs, and activated by a person. Serving then retrieves evidence units whose quotes are re-checked against stored element text before an answer is accepted.
