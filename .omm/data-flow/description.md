Corpus data moves from the original HWP/PDF files and `data_list.csv` to the evidence the model sees, as follows. Originals are hashed into `sources`. Parsing produces revisioned extractions and elements. Chunk profiles produce versioned keyword indexes whose files are hash-locked by a manifest. Unique chunk payloads are embedded into pgvector sets. Retrieval runs are frozen against an index, embedding and evaluated population. A person's activation points serving at one run. Gold datasets supply the evaluated populations. Every step is content-addressed or revisioned, so issued citations and pinned gold rows keep resolving after rebuilds.
