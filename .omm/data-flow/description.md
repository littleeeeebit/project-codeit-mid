How the corpus becomes something that can serve. Originals and `data_list.csv` under RFP_SOURCE_DIR are hashed into documents and sources. Each unique original is parsed into an extraction revision of elements. Fidelity checks and OCR add review verdicts and image text. Chunking builds an immutable keyword index; embedding sets are built for it. Frozen evaluation runs and axis matrices score candidates on reviewed gold. A person activates one row. Every stage reuses unchanged inputs, so a rerun with nothing changed makes no provider call.
