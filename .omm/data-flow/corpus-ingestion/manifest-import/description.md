`read_manifest_csv` and `normalize_row` parse amounts and dates with precision flags. `import_manifest` binds each CSV row to a `doc_id` (`doc_id_for(filename)`) and to its source by SHA-256, recording duplicates and metadata conflicts. `identity` compares CSV institution and title with the original's own cues, and `resolve-metadata` records a canonical value with rationale and evidence in `metadata_resolutions`. Search, filters and display then use the effective value; the next `build-keyword` freezes it into scope terms.
