The serving process reads only `app_settings.active_run` and `active_index` (`service.active_serving`). `Resources.index()` reloads the `KeywordIndex` when the active version changes, `dense()` loads the activated pgvector set against that index, and `run_settings()` applies the run's limits. A new activation is picked up on the next request without a restart. Dense load failures are retried after a new activation or a restart.
