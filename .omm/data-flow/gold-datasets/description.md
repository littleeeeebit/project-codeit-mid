`gold_candidates` rows are the source of truth. `.runtime/datasets/<name>.jsonl` (dev, dev-pilot) and the sealed `.runtime/sealed/test.jsonl` are rendered from them. Labels are evidence groups of source spans (hash, extraction, element, offsets, quote), never chunk IDs. Drafting runs are stored under the drafts directory (`service._drafts_dir`). The Phase-4 pilot runtime lives in the separate database `bidmate_pilot_archive` with paid admission disabled.
