`plan-run` (`answers.plan_run`) prices every remaining attempt and stores an `eval_estimates` row that binds the configuration, prices and token counts. `run-answers` and the UI's `start_answer_evaluation` run `answers.run_answers`: each row goes through `service.answer` with a `PinnedResources` that serves the finalist's recorded configuration and charges `gold_eval`. It stops at the first budget refusal or unknown billing, and resuming never re-sends a finished or unknown row. `export-review` and `import-review` collect blind human verdicts. There is no paid judge in the scores. The Luna versus Jev judge comparison is a separate study (`judges.py`, `judge_eval` envelope).
