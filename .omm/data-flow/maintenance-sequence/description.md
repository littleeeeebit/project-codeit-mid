`maintenance.run` (CLI `maintain`, or `service.start_maintenance` from the verify screen) runs `STEPS` in order: backup, restore_check (scratch database `rfp_mrestore_<uuid>`), ingest, fidelity, keyword, embedding, regression, report. Every step reuses unchanged inputs, so a no-change rerun makes no provider call. A paid embedding raises `NeedsApproval` and stops at its estimate. A failed step stops the sequence with its name and reason, and a rebuild that lost rows for any question ends `unverified`. State is written to `.runtime/maintenance/state.json` and reports to `maintenance/reports/`. Nothing is activated; the report checks that the activated run still serves.
