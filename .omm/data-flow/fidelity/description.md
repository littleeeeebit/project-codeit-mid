`fidelity run` compares each HWP extraction with Hancom's printed PDF (6-character shingles both ways, exact runs of 3+ digits) and returns `auto_verified` or `auto_flagged` with its findings. It updates `sources.review_status` only for the active extraction, and never over a human status (`HUMAN_STATUSES`). `import-reviews` and `review record` add human statuses such as `sample_checked` and `reviewed`.
