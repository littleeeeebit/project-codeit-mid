`fidelity.verify_source` compares an HWP extraction with Hancom Viewer's own printed PDF in both directions: 6-character shingles, ruled-table cells, and exact matches for runs of 3 or more digits. It records `fidelity_checks` and `auto_verified` or `auto_flagged`, with the exact element, cell and page of each difference. `GET /api/verify/fidelity/{hash}/pages/{n}` renders the printed page, and `/confirm` records `sample_checked`. `ocr` reads raster regions with PaddleOCR-VL on the GPU and re-reads only flagged regions with Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite, under its own $0.50 ledger. The next `ingest` merges the results.
