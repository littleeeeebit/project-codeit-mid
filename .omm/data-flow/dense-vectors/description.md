`plan-embeddings` prices uncached payloads into an estimate bound to the index, model, dimensions and rates (24 h). `build-dense` (OpenAI, paid in the `embedding` envelope), `build_local_dense` (GPU) and `build_gemini_dense` (own cap) fill `embedding_payloads` through `cache_put_many`, which verifies unit norm and checksum and refuses to overwrite a different vector. `publish` writes `embedding_sets` and ordered `embedding_set_rows` as `building`, verifies with `PgDenseIndex.load`, then marks the set `ready`. An unknown embedding attempt blocks `build-dense` and paid evaluation queries until reconciled.
