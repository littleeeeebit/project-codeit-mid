`fidelity.verify_source` has Hancom Viewer print the HWP to PDF (an independent renderer) and compares both directions with 6-character shingles plus exact runs of 3 or more digits. It writes `fidelity_checks` (per extraction and method) and sets `auto_verified` or `auto_flagged` without overwriting a human status. On 검증 → 원문 대조, `confirm_fidelity` records `sample_checked` after a person has looked at every reported place (`rendered_page_png`).
