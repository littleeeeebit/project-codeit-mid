`cli ocr` → `ocr.run` reads every raster region of the HWP print or PDF original with local PaddleOCR-VL. Regions that fail `fallback_reasons` (looping, low confidence, length far from the ink estimate) are re-read by `gemini-3.5-flash-lite`. Region results are cached under `.runtime/ocr/<hash>/`. The next `ingest` places them as `image_text` elements with `image_ocr` locations; the fidelity check skips them, citations are warned as `ocr_text`, and the system prompt tells the model to flag OCR-based claims.
