`dense.plan_embeddings` prices the unique uncached payloads (with an estimate ID and a 24 h expiry). `build_dense` reserves and settles bounded batches in the `embedding` envelope, refuses while any embedding attempt is unknown, and publishes only after every row verifies. Local models (`models.EMBEDDINGS`, GPU only) embed for free (`build_local_dense`); Gemini runs under its own cap (`build_gemini_dense`). `vector_store.cache_put_many` stores verified unit vectors in `embedding_payloads` (refusing to overwrite a different vector), and `publish` writes `embedding_sets` plus `embedding_set_rows` bound to the keyword index's chunk order, then flips the state to `ready` after `PgDenseIndex.load` verifies it.
