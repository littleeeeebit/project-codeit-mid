`plan-embeddings` counts unique payload tokens, subtracts verified cache hits and stores a 24-hour estimate bound to index, model, dimensions, payloads and rates. `build-dense` refuses a stale or over-envelope estimate, then reserves and settles each batch in the `embedding` envelope through `metered_embed`. It refuses while any embedding attempt is unknown and publishes only after every row verifies (`vector_store.publish` → `embedding_sets` building → ready). Local models (`models.EMBEDDINGS`) embed every active chunk on the GPU for free (`build_local_dense`). Gemini builds use their own cap. Vectors are deduplicated by payload hash in `embedding_payloads` and reused across indexes.
