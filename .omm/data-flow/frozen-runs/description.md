`.runtime/runs/<run_id>/` records the dataset hash, the population SHA, the index version and manifest, the analyzer, the dense version, limits including `corpus_route`, the reranker and `eval_version`, plus the per-question traces and scores. Activation copies the serving-relevant subset (`serving_config`) into `app_settings.active_run`. Answer-run folders `A-*` and the sealed freeze files reference these runs.
