`evaluate-retrieval` writes frozen runs K0, K1, D, H and HR under `.runtime/runs/<run_id>/` (config, traces, scores, report). A run is bound to the dataset hash, the evaluated population, the index manifest, analyzer, dense version and limits, and is scored against source spans (hit@k, nDCG@5, MRR, packed completeness, critical failures, Wilson intervals). `compare --matrix lexical|chunking|embedding|reranker` (compare.Runner, compare.run_matrix) varies one declared axis over three populations: development rows in their own document, development rows over the whole corpus, and the needle set. Finished cells are cached under `.runtime/compare/cells/`. Uncached paid rows stop as `needs_approval` with a priced estimate until `compare --approve`. Tables go to `.runtime/compare/tables/<matrix>.json|.md`, and the 실험 비교 tab reads them through GET /api/verify/experiments.
