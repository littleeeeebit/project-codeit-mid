`RFP_SOURCE_DIR` holds `data_list.csv` and `files/`. `import_manifest` records the 100 CSV associations in `documents` (`normalized_metadata_json`) and the unique originals in `sources`, keyed by SHA-256. Byte-identical files share one source, one extraction and one vector per payload, but keep their own metadata and scope. `resolve-metadata` records canonical values in `metadata_resolutions`.
