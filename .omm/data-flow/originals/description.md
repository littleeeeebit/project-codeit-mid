`import_manifest` reads `원본 데이터/data_list.csv` and `files/`, hashes every original (sha256 = source_hash) and creates `documents` (doc_id from the filename, CSV metadata) and `sources`. Byte-identical originals share one source and extraction but keep their own document metadata and scope. `original_download` serves only managed `(doc_id, source_hash)` pairs whose file sits directly in `files_dir` and still hashes to the recorded value.
