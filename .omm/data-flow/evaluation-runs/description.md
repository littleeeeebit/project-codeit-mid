`evaluate-retrieval` and the `compare` matrices (lexical, chunking, embedding, reranker) score frozen retrieval runs against gold source spans. The metrics are hit@k, nDCG@5, packed completeness, critical/code/scope failures, latency and Wilson intervals. Runs are frozen by dataset hash, population, index manifest, analyzer and limits, and stored under `.runtime/runs/<run_id>/`. Comparison tables go to `.runtime/compare/tables/<matrix>.json|md`, which `/api/verify/experiments` reads. Paid rows stop at `needs_approval` estimates until `compare --approve`. Runs over different populations are never compared.
