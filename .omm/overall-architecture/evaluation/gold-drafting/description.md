`service.plan_drafting` prices the slots, and `start_drafting` checks the consented maximum and `fits`. One thread then runs `drafting._generate` with gpt-6-luna, charged to `gold_eval`, with a guard that interrupts the run when the service closes. Output goes to `datasets drafts/<run_id>/output` (`candidates.jsonl`, `invalid.json`, `receipt.json`). `submit_drafts` sends a completed run's valid drafts to `gold.submit` as one batch, after `evaluation.assign_families`.
