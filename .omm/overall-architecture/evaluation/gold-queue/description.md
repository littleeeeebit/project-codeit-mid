`gold_candidates` rows wait for review. `service.gold_decide` requires a note and refuses approval by whoever requested the drafting run. It then calls `gold.decide` with an `expected_sha` that guards against concurrent edits. A rejection goes to the rejection wiki with categories. Sealed `test` candidates are reviewed only through the owner CLI (`gold show/decide/second-review`). `validate-gold` and `freeze-dataset` produce the frozen dev and test manifests.
