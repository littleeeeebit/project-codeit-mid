gold.decide changes a pending candidate to approved or rejected inside one mutex transaction. It requires a matching row_sha256, and approving a gold-2 row also requires original_inspected. The drafter, the drafting model, and (enforced in service.gold_decide) the member who started the drafting run cannot approve. An approval rewrites the dataset JSONL. A rejection writes a page to the rejection wiki. Sealed candidates are refused unless the owner CLI passes include_sealed.
