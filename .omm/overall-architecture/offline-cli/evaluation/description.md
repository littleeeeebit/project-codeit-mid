Free and paid retrieval runs (`evaluate-retrieval`, `trial-reranker`, `compare --matrix …`) are frozen by dataset, population, index manifest, analyzer and limits under `.runtime/runs/<run_id>/`. `activate-run` or the Verify experiments action → `evaluation.activate_decision` validates the decision (`decision_errors`: current policy, verified index and matrix, pinned embedding revision) and writes `app_settings.active_run` in one transaction, keeping the history in `activations`. Only this step changes what serves. Answer evaluation, sealed runs, judges and `release-report` (which now adds `error_analysis.release_summary` as a non-gating section) live in the same package and were traced only at their entry points.
