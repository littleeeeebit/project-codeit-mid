src/rfp_assistant/cli.py is an argparse dispatcher with roughly 60 subcommands. Every command except `check`, `load-check` and `restore-check` runs against `RFP_DATABASE_DSN` and refuses an unvalidated database. The traced groups are the corpus pipeline, index and dense builds, evaluation and activation, and stage and traffic review. Paid offline work goes through the same gateway functions under an envelope purpose (`embedding`, `gold_eval`, `ocr`), and stops at a priced estimate until a named person approves it.
