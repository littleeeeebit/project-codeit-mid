Long paid or heavy jobs run as daemon threads started inside `contextvars.copy_context()`, so they pay with the starting browser's key. Each is registered in `res._jobs` so that `close()` joins it. They are: `start_drafting` (`_DRAFT_JOBS`, one at a time, refused above the consented maximum or when `plan_drafting` says it does not fit); `start_maintenance` (`_MAINTENANCE_JOB`, one at a time, under `_EVAL_LOCK`); `start_answer_evaluation`; and `start_judges`. The last two are at service.py:2321 and :2403; I did not read them line by line. Their progress lives in files under .runtime (drafts/<run>/, maintenance/state.json, judge and answer-run directories), and the screens poll it. A thread found dead while its state still says running is reported as interrupted (`maintenance_status`).
