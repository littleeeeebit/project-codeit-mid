`Resources(settings, transport=None, recover=False, dispatch=True, tracer=None)` is the process-wide owner. `_initialize` runs `postgres.require_imported_database`, `init_schema` and `budget.ensure_budget_row`. With `recover=True`, or whenever a transport exists, it claims gateway ownership through `_own`, which borrows this process's live `GatewayOwner` or takes a new advisory lock. A new owner runs `recover_requests` and `budget.recover`. `provider='fake'` builds `FakeTransport`. Otherwise `OPENAI_API_KEY` builds `OpenAITransport(owner_check=owner.check)` plus a `Tracing` from settings. A missing key leaves `provider_note` set and paid stages answer 'provider unavailable'. `dispatch=False` gives a ledger-only owner that the CLI's `reconcile`, `unresolved` and `settle` use beside the serving app. Loaded lazily, under `_index_lock`: `index()` (the `KeywordIndex` for `active_serving().index_version`), `dense()` (`PgDenseIndex.load`; a failure is cached per version pair in `_dense_failed` and recorded in `stage_errors`) and `reranker()`. `run_settings(cfg)` overlays the activated run's limits, embedding identity and reranker settings on the process settings, so serving reproduces what was measured. `close()` is the controlled stop.
