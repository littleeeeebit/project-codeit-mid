`Resources(settings, transport=None, recover=False, dispatch=True, tracer=None)`. `__init__` enters `store.database_lifecycle` (a reference-counted psycopg pool per DSN), then runs `_initialize`. That step calls `postgres.require_imported_database`, `init_schema` and `budget.ensure_budget_row`. With `recover=True`, `_own()` borrows this process's live `GatewayOwner` or takes `postgres.gateway_lock`. Only a fresh owner runs `recover_requests` (queued/running -> interrupted) and `budget.recover` (dispatching -> unknown, reserved -> released). `provider='fake'` builds `FakeTransport`. Otherwise `OPENAI_API_KEY` builds `OpenAITransport(owner_check=owner.check)` and `tracing.Tracing.from_settings`; without a key, `provider_note` explains why paid generation is unavailable. Lazy, lock-guarded loaders: `index()` (a `KeywordIndex` for `active_serving()['index_version']`, reloaded when the version changes), `dense()` (a `PgDenseIndex`; a verification failure is cached in `_dense_failed` and surfaced through `stage_errors['dense']`), and `reranker()` (reloaded when model, revision, max length, concurrency or precision change, after `free_gpu()`). `retire_inactive(cfg)` unloads GPU embedders and the reranker the activated configuration no longer uses. `run_settings(cfg)` overlays the activated run's measured limits, embedding model/dims and reranker settings on the process settings. `close()` stops admission, runs `runner.shutdown`, joins jobs until `shutdown_wait_seconds`, re-runs recovery if a job is still alive, closes the transport and then tracing (flush), and finally releases the gateway owner and pool.
