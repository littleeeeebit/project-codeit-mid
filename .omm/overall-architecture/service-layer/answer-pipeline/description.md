run_queued claims queued→running (only if cancel_requested = 0), rebuilds AnswerRequest and Principal from request_json, and calls _execute. _execute opens one Langfuse trace `answer-question` seeded by request_id. _execute_traced dispatches metadata/inventory to free deterministic answers (_metadata_answer from CSV facts, _inventory_answer from the requirements table) and everything else to _paid_answer. _paid_answer: _checkpoint → _conversation → optional _rewrite (paid `query_rewrite` stage) → retrieval and pricing (prepare_answer, _prepare_compare or _frozen_prep) → empty evidence ends `insufficient_evidence` without paying → _checkpoint → _metered_chat streaming into res.partials → generation.validate_answer against stored chunk quotes → done(...). Every exit goes through _finish, which derives billing_state from the request's attempts and keeps a cancelled or interrupted status.
