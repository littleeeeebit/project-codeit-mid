src/rfp_assistant/service.py holds the public service functions (each authorizes a capability through auth.require_any), the Resources owner and the request orchestration. It is the only module api.py imports. It coordinates retrieval (service.retrieve, prepare_answer, _prepare_compare), paid stages (_metered_chat, dense.query_vector), persistence of requests and results (_create, _finish), evidence and original access, verifier traces, and starting background jobs for drafting, answer evaluation, judges and maintenance.
