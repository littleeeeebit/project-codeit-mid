`src/rfp_assistant/service.py` holds every non-rendering decision: capability checks, scope resolution against the active source hash, request persistence and idempotency, the paid answer state machine, verifier runs, drafting and evaluation jobs, and screen-entry helpers (`ask`, `find_documents`, `target_key`, `may_attach`). The other subsystems (`retrieval`, `dense`, `budget`, `generation`, `gold`, `drafting`, `answers`) are reached through it.
