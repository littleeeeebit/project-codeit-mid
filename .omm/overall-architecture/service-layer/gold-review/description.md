`gold.py` keeps candidate questions in `gold_candidates`; `.runtime/datasets/*.jsonl` and the rejection wiki pages are projections re-rendered from the database. `gold_queue`, `candidate_review` and `gold_decide` serve the dataset page with development candidates only; sealed rows are reviewed through owner CLI `gold show/decide/second-review`. `gold.check` verifies that every projection and batch file equals its database rendering and hash. See dataset-and-release-flow for the review rules.
