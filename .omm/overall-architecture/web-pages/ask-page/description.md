AskPage manages scope (선택한 문서 or 전체 문서), mode, the conversation's turns and request ownership. submit() POSTs /api/ask with scope, question, mode and previous_request_id (the last turn's request_id) under the member captured at submit time. It keeps the returned Owned {request_id, generation_id, target}. Each TurnView polls GET /api/requests/{id}?generation_id&target every 1000 ms until the status leaves queued/running. It renders the answer only when the response says `attachable` (service.may_attach). Cancel POSTs /api/requests/{id}/cancel. release() runs on a document or scope change, 새 대화 or unmount; it invalidates a pending POST and POSTs /abandon with keepalive, which cancels only a still-queued request. History lists GET /api/requests. Citation markers open GET /api/requests/{id}/evidence/{eid} in the evidence pane (answer-parts.tsx).
