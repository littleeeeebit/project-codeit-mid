`fidelity run` has Hancom Viewer print each HWP to PDF as an independent witness. It compares 6-character shingles both ways and requires runs of three or more digits to match exactly, then sets `auto_verified` or `auto_flagged` and records the exact differing locations. A person can confirm a document as `sample_checked` through `POST /api/verify/fidelity/{source_hash}/confirm`. An automatic status never overwrites a human one.
