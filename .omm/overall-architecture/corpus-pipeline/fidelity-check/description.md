The README describes the flow: `fidelity run` has Hancom Viewer print each HWP to PDF as an independent witness, then compares the extraction both ways with 6-character shingles, rendered-line and table-cell coverage, and exact runs of 3+ digits. The verdict and its findings land in `fidelity_checks` for the active extraction under `FIDELITY_VERSION`. The 검증 screen reads them through `service.fidelity_overview` and `rendered_page_png`, and `confirm_fidelity` records a human `sample_checked` review over the reported locations. Maintenance's `step_fidelity` checks only extractions without a current verdict. The internals of `fidelity.verify_source` were not read.
