`ocr.merge_hwp` places the cached OCR text of embedded HWP pictures as `image_text` elements at the picture markers pyhwp's walk left. `ocr.merge` does the same for PDF raster images, next to the surrounding text. The `ocr` CLI fills the cache: PaddleOCR-VL reads locally on the GPU, and only regions that fail the fallback test are re-read by gpt-5-mini from the `ocr` envelope. Documents parsed by the HWP loader get no image text.
