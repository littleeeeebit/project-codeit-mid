Inside `_parse_source`, `ocr.merge_hwp` and its PDF counterpart merge cached OCR reads from `.runtime/ocr/<hash>/` as `image_text` elements: at the picture markers pyhwp left for HWP, or next to the surrounding text for PDF. The reads come from PaddleOCR-VL on the GPU. Only flagged regions are re-read by gpt-5-mini, which is paid from the `ocr` envelope through the gateway. OCR text is chunked separately, cited as OCR, and skipped by the fidelity check.
