`cli ocr` reads embedded HWP pictures (WMF/EMF rendered on Windows) and PDF raster images with PaddleOCR-VL on the GPU. Only regions failing the fallback test are re-read by `gpt-5-mini` through the budget gateway from the `ocr` envelope, paid with the owner's personal key. Results are cached per region under `.runtime/ocr/<hash>/`. The next `ingest` merges them as `image_text` elements, which are chunked apart, cited as OCR and skipped by fidelity.
