`assign_revision` binds the extraction ID to the actual output. Identical output keeps its revision and its review. Different output under the same fingerprint gets a content-hashed revision beside the old one, whose artifact and element rows stay for pinned gold rows and citations. `ingest_source` writes `extractions`, replaces `elements`, records `extraction_inputs`, and resets `review_status` to `unreviewed` when the active extraction changes.
