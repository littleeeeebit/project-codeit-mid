`parse_pdf` reads PyMuPDF text with page labels and columns. It serves PDF originals, and HWP originals that pyhwp cannot read but Hancom printed (`printed_pdf_path`). In that case elements are tagged `format: hwp_print`, a `recovered_from_native_print` warning records the rendering hash, and the fingerprint becomes `<pdf fp>-hancom-print`. Such sources keep no independent witness, so they stay unreviewed.
