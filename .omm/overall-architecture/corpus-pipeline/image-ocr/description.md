`ocr.run` (CLI `ocr`) holds `ProcessLock(ocr/run.lock)`. For each parsed source it takes regions from the HWP BinData pictures (`hwp_regions`, with WMF/EMF rendered to PNG through `_placeable` and `to_png`) or from the PDF raster images (`regions`). `LocalOCR` reads them with PaddleOCR-VL 1.6 on the GPU. Regions that fail `fallback_reasons` (a loop, mean probability below 0.90, or output far longer than the ink estimate) are re-read by `RemoteReader` with gpt-5-mini through the budget gateway under the `ocr` envelope, which starts at zero. Results are cached per region in `ocr/<hash>/<version-hash>.jsonl`, keyed by place and image digest. After every region the cache is checkpointed, including rows not yet reached, so an interrupted run keeps its paid reads, and a picture already read elsewhere is reused by digest. `--ledger-dsn-env` points paid reads at another database's ledger, for example codeit's through a tunnel. `OcrStop` (budget refusal, unknown billing, overrun) ends paid reads for the run, and other failures leave the region `unresolved`.
