From the README and the `ingest_source` call sites (`fidelity.py` and `ocr.py` internals were not read in detail): `fidelity run` has Hancom Viewer print each HWP to PDF. It compares 6-character shingles and digit runs both ways between extraction and print, then records `auto_verified` or `auto_flagged` without ever overwriting a human status. `ocr` reads raster regions with PaddleOCR-VL and re-reads flagged regions with Gemini under its own $0.50 cap and ledger. The next ingest merges the results as OCR-labelled elements.
