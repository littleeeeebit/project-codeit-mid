assign_revision content-addresses the extraction. extraction_id is sha256(source_hash:fingerprint)[:24] when the output matches the stored artifact; otherwise a content-hashed ID is used and the old artifact and elements are kept for pinned gold rows and issued citations. It writes .runtime/extracted/<hash>/<fp>/elements.jsonl atomically. ingest_source then replaces the elements rows, inserts the extraction and sets sources.active_extraction_id. A new revision resets review_status to unreviewed. Per-run results and diagnostics go to .runtime/reports/ingest-*.json.
