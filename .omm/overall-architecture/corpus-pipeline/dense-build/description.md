`plan_embeddings` counts unique payloads, subtracts verified cache hits, and stores an estimate bound to the index, model, dimensions, rates and batch limits for 24 h. `build_dense` refuses a stale or over-envelope estimate. It reserves and settles each batch in the `embedding` envelope through `metered_embed` and refuses while any embedding attempt is unknown. `publish_dense` publishes the pgvector set only after every row verifies. Local models (`build_local_dense`) embed on the GPU for free; Gemini goes through `build_gemini_dense` under its own cap.
