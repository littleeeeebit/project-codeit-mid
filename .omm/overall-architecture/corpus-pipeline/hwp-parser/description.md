`parse_hwp` runs `run_hwp_converter`: the `hwp5proc xml` subprocess, with no shell, a timeout and stderr capped with xmllint noise filtered out. It maps failures to `hwp_converter_missing`, `hwp_converter_timeout`, `hwp_converter_decode_error` or `hwp_converter_failed`, empty output to `hwp_empty_output` and broken XML to `hwp_xml_malformed`. It fills `EqEdit` equation slots from `equation_scripts` when the counts match, then `walk_hwp` produces elements in native order, with tables as cells and section paths.
