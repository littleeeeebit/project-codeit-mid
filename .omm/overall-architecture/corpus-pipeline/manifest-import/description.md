`import_manifest` reads `data_list.csv` (`read_manifest_csv`, `normalize_row` with typed dates and amounts plus flags), hashes every original (`sha256_file`) and writes `documents` (`doc_id_for(filename)`) and `sources` keyed by source hash. Byte-identical files share one source and one extraction but keep their own metadata. `identity_report` and `resolve-metadata` record canonical values with a rationale, used by filters and ranking.
