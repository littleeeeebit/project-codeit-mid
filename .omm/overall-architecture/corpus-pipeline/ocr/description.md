`ocr.run` reads every embedded HWP picture (BinData; WMF/EMF rendered on Windows) and every PDF raster image with local PaddleOCR-VL 1.6 on CUDA. Only regions that fail the fallback test (loop, low confidence, implausible length) are re-read by `gpt-5-mini` through the budget gateway, from the `ocr` envelope that the owner funds. Results are cached per region under `.runtime/ocr/<hash>/`, so a rerun resumes. The next `ingest` merges them as `image_text` elements; they are cited as OCR and skipped by fidelity.
