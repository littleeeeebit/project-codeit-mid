`sources.review_status` moves among unreviewed, auto_verified, auto_flagged, sample_checked, reviewed and needs_recovery. `record_review` and `import_reviews` append `reviews` rows, and a human status is never overwritten by an automatic one (README). `build_keyword_index` admits auto_verified, sample_checked and reviewed sources, plus unreviewed and auto_flagged ones with `--include-unreviewed` (the operating index). Answers and evidence views label sources whose review is not human.
