`corpus/ingestion.py` turns the originals listed in `data_list.csv` into extractions. `import_manifest` hashes and deduplicates. `ingest` parses each unique original once through `_parse_source` and reuses unchanged inputs (same bytes, parser revision, OCR cache and converter, unless `--force`). It writes `sources`, `extractions` and `.runtime/reports/ingest-*.json` with diagnostics. Fidelity, OCR and the keyword build are separate CLI commands that the maintenance sequence also runs.
