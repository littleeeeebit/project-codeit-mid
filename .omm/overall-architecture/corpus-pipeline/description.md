These are offline CLI commands (`manifest`, `ingest`, `ocr`, `fidelity run`, `import-reviews`, `recover-source`, `resolve-metadata`) and the `ingest`/`fidelity` steps of `maintenance.run`. `import_manifest` hashes every original listed in `data_list.csv`. `ingest` parses each unique source once (`ingest_source`), reusing it when `input_key` (original, parser, print, OCR cache, converter) is unchanged.
