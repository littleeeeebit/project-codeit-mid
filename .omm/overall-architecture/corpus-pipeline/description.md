The free-to-paid path from originals to what serves, driven from the CLI (`manifest`, `ingest`, `ocr`, `fidelity run`, `build-keyword`, `plan-embeddings`/`build-dense`, `evaluate-retrieval`, `compare`, `activate-run`) or by the maintenance sequence.
