`tracing.run` opens one Langfuse trace per executed request. Its id is derived from `seed=request_id`, and it is tagged with the member, the environment (server or local) and the release (`<branch>@<commit>`). Inside it, `tracing.step` records retrieve-evidence, assemble-evidence, rewrite-question, generate-answer (usage and ledger cost) and validate-answer. The trace carries three scores: `citation_valid`, `insufficient_evidence` and `evidence_tokens`. Tracing is observability only: admission and settlement never read it, and with the LANGFUSE_* keys missing no client is created.
