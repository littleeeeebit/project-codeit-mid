Resources._initialize enters a bounded database lifecycle, requires a validated import, initializes schema and budget, and selects fake or OpenAI transport. _own acquires or borrows the database gateway owner and recovers unfinished work. shared_analyzer() constructs one Kiwi analyzer per process; index(), dense() and reranker() lazily load serving stages. close() rejects new work, waits a bounded interval, closes transports and tracing, and releases ownership.
