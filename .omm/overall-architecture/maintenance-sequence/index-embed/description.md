`step_keyword` requires an activated `index_version`. It builds (or reuses) a keyword index with the served index's profile and review scope and `activate=False`, and records `rebuilt_index` and `same_as_served`. `step_embedding` is skipped for keyword-only serving. Otherwise `compare.ensure_dense` embeds the rebuilt index's uncached chunks with the activated model: free for local GPU models. An uncached paid model raises `NeedsApproval` with a priced estimate until someone runs `compare --approve` or uses 실험 비교.
