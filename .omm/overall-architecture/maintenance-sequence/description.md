src/rfp_assistant/storage/maintenance.py `run` executes STEPS in order: backup, restore_check, ingest, fidelity, keyword, embedding, regression, report. Callers are the 검증 button (`service.start_maintenance` → daemon thread on the app's analyzer and transport) and CLI `maintain` (`_paid_resources` → `Resources(settings)`). Progress and the outcome go to `<data>/maintenance/state.json` after every step and every file. The final copy goes to `reports/<run_id>.json` and `.md`. A step result of `reused` marks no change, so a rerun with no changed input makes no provider call. Nothing is activated: serving before and after is recorded and compared (`serving.unchanged`).
