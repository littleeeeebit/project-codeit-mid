`maintenance.run` executes `STEPS` in order: backup, restore_check (into a scratch database `rfp_mrestore_<uuid>`), ingest, fidelity (changed HWP only), keyword, embedding, regression and report. Every step reuses unchanged work. `NeedsApproval` stops the sequence at a priced embedding estimate, and any exception stops it as `failed` with the step name. The service's `closing()` also stops it between steps. State is persisted to `maintenance/state.json` after every step. The run compares `_serving()` before and after and ends as `unverified` when the regression lost evidence-pinned questions. It never activates anything.
