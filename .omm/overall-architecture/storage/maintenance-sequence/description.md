`maintenance.run` executes `STEPS` in order: backup, restore_check, ingest, fidelity, keyword, embedding, regression, report. It writes `maintenance/state.json`, reuses unchanged outputs (no provider call on a no-change rerun), stops at a paid embedding estimate (`NeedsApproval`) or at the first `StepFailed`, and never activates anything. `maintenance_status` reports a run recorded as `running` with no live thread as `interrupted`.
