PostgreSQL is accessed through a thin adapter, `storage/postgres.Connection`, which turns `?` placeholders into psycopg parameters (`bind_sql`). `store.open_db` / `tx` are the only entry points. `init_schema` applies the schema, the pgvector schema (`vector_store.SCHEMA`) and the external-ledger schema under an advisory schema lock, and refuses a database whose schema is newer than the code. Large immutable artifacts live under `RFP_DATA_DIR` (`.runtime`), and their hashes are recorded in the database.
