The corpus catalog and review records:
- `sources`: format, original_path, active_extraction_id, parse_status, review_status.
- `documents`: CSV row, filename, active source, raw and normalized metadata, quality conflicts.
- `extractions`, `elements`, `indexes`, `chunks`, `requirements`, `reviews`, `fidelity_checks`, `extraction_inputs`, `metadata_resolutions`.
- Verification and evaluation records: `verifier_runs`, `corrections`, `gold_candidates`, `gold_reviews`, `eval_estimates`, `embedding_estimates`, `activations`.
