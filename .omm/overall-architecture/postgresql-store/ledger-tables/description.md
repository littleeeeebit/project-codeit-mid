`attempts` holds one row per paid stage with `stage` (query_rewrite, generation, embedding), `purpose` envelope, model, state, reserved and settled micro-USD, token estimates, `price_json`, `raw_usage_json`, `response_id`, `error_json` and timestamps. `adjustments` are append-only owner or reconciliation corrections, idempotent by `correction_key`. `budget_settings` (single row) holds cap, allowance, envelopes, rates, `paid_enabled`, `frozen_reason`, a revision and history. `external_attempts` (models.EXTERNAL_SCHEMA) is the separate Gemini ledger.
