`app_settings` holds the keys `active_run` (the serving config JSON) and `active_index`. `activations` is the append-only activation history with the previous config. `database_control.paid_admission` is the database-side paid switch that `paid on` sets together with the ledger flag. `migration_import`/`migration_validation` hold the validated-import marker plus artifact references whose hashes `require_imported_database` re-checks at startup. `bidmate_recovery.control`/`receipt`, when present, block startup and dispatch until a verified restore check.
