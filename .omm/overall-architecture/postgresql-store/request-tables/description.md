`requests` holds `request_id`, `member_id`, `idempotency_key` (unique per member), `generation_id`, `input_hash`, `config_hash`, `scope_json`, `status`, `request_json` (the input snapshot, including `previous_request_id` and capabilities), `trace_json` (config, retrieval, rewrite record, billing attempts, raw output on failure), `result_json` (the `AnswerResult`), `mode` and `cancel_requested`. `verifier_runs` stores frozen retrieval-only runs. `corrections` and `audit_events` record reviewer and owner actions.
