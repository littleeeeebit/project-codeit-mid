`storage/postgres.py` holds the pinned server and extension checks (`require_server`: 18.6; `_configure`: pgvector 0.8.6, lock_timeout 5 s, statement_timeout 60 s), a reference-counted `psycopg_pool.ConnectionPool` per target (`lifecycle`, entered by `store.database_lifecycle`), `open_db`, which refuses when no lifecycle owner exists, and `tx`. `bind_sql` translates `?` placeholders. `init_schema` runs under a schema advisory lock and creates the store tables, the vector schema, the external ledger, `application_mutex` and `database_control`. `storage/store.py` declares the tables: sources, documents, extractions, elements, indexes, chunks, requirements, reviews, fidelity_checks, requests, attempts, adjustments, budget_settings, gold_candidates, gold_reviews, verifier_runs, corrections, audit_events, activations, app_settings and others. It also holds the atomic file writers (`write_text_atomic`, `write_jsonl_atomic`) used for every `.runtime` artifact. `postgres_backup.py`, `release.backup` and `restore_check` produce and verify custom-format dumps; a restore leaves paid admission off.
