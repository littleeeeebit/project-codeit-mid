`postgres.py` and `store.py`. `lifecycle(target)` creates one bounded `psycopg_pool.ConnectionPool` per DSN, reference-counted by owners, after probing the server version. `_configure` sets `lock_timeout` 5 s and `statement_timeout` 60 s, requires pgvector 0.8.6 and registers the vector type. `open_db` refuses when no owner entered the lifecycle. `tx(conn, immediate=True)` locks the single `application_mutex` row `FOR UPDATE`, serializing every admission and idempotency write. `require_imported_database` checks `migration_import` complete, `migration_validation` passed with a matching snapshot, every referenced artifact hash (index manifests and their files included), and no blocked `bidmate_recovery.control`. `init_schema` applies DDL under an advisory transaction lock and seeds `database_control.paid_admission = false`. Main tables: documents, sources, extractions, elements, extraction_inputs, indexes, chunks, requirements, requests, attempts, adjustments, budget_settings, app_settings (`active_index`, `active_run`), activations, fidelity_checks, gold_candidates, embedding_payloads, embedding_sets, embedding_set_rows, external_attempts.
