`budget.reserve` returns `{admitted, attempt_id, reserved_micro_usd, reason}`. Refusal reasons are `paid_disabled`, `postgresql_maintenance`, `frozen`, `unknown_rate`, `unknown_purpose`, `above_consented_maximum`, `cap_exhausted`, `envelope_exhausted` and `ledger_unavailable`, the last on lock timeout or a database error. `_metered_chat` maps these to a `budget_blocked` or `clarification_required` outcome. The attempt's `price_json` freezes the rates, the rate version and the member's `billing_scope` from the contextvar.
