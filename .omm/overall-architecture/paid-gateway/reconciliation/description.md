`budget.recover` runs at ownership claim: dispatching → unknown, reserved → released, and the same for the Gemini `external_attempts`. `budget.reconcile` imports a provider total for a closed interval and scope as an adjustment, and moves the explicitly covered unknown attempts to `reconciled`. A late `settle` of a reconciled attempt adds a negative `late-settlement:` adjustment so the cost is not counted twice. These are exposed through the CLI `reconcile`, `unresolved`, `settle` and `adjust`.
