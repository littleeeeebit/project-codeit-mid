These are owner CLI commands that open a ledger-only `Resources(dispatch=False)` where applicable. `unresolved` lists attempts in reserved, dispatching, unknown or reconciled state. `settle` (`service.settle_from_evidence`) settles an unknown or reconciled attempt from dated provider usage, with evidence and a reason. `reconcile` (`budget.reconcile`) imports a closed provider interval: the covered unknown attempts must have been dispatched inside it, intervals must not overlap, and the adjustment is provider total minus locally settled cost. Covered attempts become reconciled. `adjust` records external usage as an idempotent `correction_key`. `paid on|off` (`set_paid_enabled`) flips the ledger flag and `database_control.paid_admission` together. `configure-budget` records dates, prior use, rates, allowance and cap. `set-envelopes` and `set-limit` preserve spending and reservations. Every action writes to `audit_events`.
