`budget.recover(db)` runs under the newly acquired owner lock (from `Resources._own`) and again in `close()` when job threads outlived the shutdown wait. It moves `dispatching` to `unknown` (the process may have sent the call) and `reserved` to `released` (never sent), and applies the same rule to the Gemini `external_attempts` ledger. `service.recover_requests` marks queued and running requests `interrupted`. Nothing is replayed automatically.
