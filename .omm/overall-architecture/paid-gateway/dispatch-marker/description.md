`budget.mark_dispatching(db, attempt_id, guard)` runs `guard(conn)`, which is `service._stop_reason`, in the same transaction that would move `reserved` to `dispatching`. It also refuses while the database is in maintenance and while any `unknown` attempt exists on PostgreSQL. On refusal it moves the attempt to `released` (`stopped_before_dispatch:<reason>`) and raises `DispatchRefused`. `_metered_chat` turns cancelled or interrupted into `_Stop` and any other refusal into `budget_blocked`.
