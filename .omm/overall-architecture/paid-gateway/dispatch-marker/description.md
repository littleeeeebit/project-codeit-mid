`mark_dispatching(db, attempt_id, guard)` runs the caller's guard (the request's `_stop_reason`: cancelled, or interrupted / closing) and `owner_guard` in the same transaction. It also checks that paid_admission is on and that no attempt is 'unknown'; any unknown attempt blocks all dispatch until it is reconciled. On refusal the reservation becomes 'released' with `stopped_before_dispatch:<reason>` and `DispatchRefused` is raised. Otherwise the row becomes 'dispatching' with `dispatched_at` before any network call.
