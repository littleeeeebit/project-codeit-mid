One immediate transaction that evaluates the caller's guard (service._stop_reason: cancelled or interrupted), owner_guard, paid_admission and the existence of any `unknown` attempt. On a refusal it moves reserved -> released with stopped_before_dispatch:<reason> and raises DispatchRefused. Otherwise it moves reserved -> dispatching with dispatched_at. Because the stop check and the marker share one transaction, a controlled shutdown or cancel cannot slip in between check and dispatch.
