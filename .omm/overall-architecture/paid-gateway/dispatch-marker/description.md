budget.mark_dispatching(attempt_id, guard) runs the caller's guard (service._stop_reason: cancelled, interrupted or closing) and owner_guard. It also checks paid_admission and the absence of any `unknown` attempt in the same transaction. Then it either releases the attempt with `stopped_before_dispatch:<reason>` and raises DispatchRefused, or moves it to `dispatching`. Because the check and the marker share one transaction, a shutdown or cancellation can never slip between them.
