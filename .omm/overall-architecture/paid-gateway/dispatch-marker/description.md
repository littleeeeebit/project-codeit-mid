`mark_dispatching(db, attempt_id, guard)` reruns the caller's stop check, `owner_guard`, the maintenance flag and the any-unknown check in the same immediate transaction. On a refusal it moves the attempt from `reserved` to `released` with `stopped_before_dispatch:<reason>` and raises `DispatchRefused`. Otherwise it moves the attempt from `reserved` to `dispatching` with `dispatched_at`. This prevents a shutdown or cancel from slipping in between the check and the call.
