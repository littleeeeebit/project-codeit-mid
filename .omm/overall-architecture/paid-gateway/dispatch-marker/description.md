`budget.mark_dispatching(db, attempt_id, guard)` runs the caller's guard (the request's `_stop_reason`, or a job's closing check), the owner guard, the `paid_admission` check and an unknown-attempt check in one transaction. On any reason it releases the reservation (`stopped_before_dispatch:<reason>`) and raises `DispatchRefused`; otherwise it moves the attempt to `dispatching` with `dispatched_at`.
