`budget.mark_dispatching` checks, in one transaction, the request's guard (`_stop_reason`: cancelled, interrupted or closing), `owner_guard`, `paid_admission`, and the rule that no attempt may be `unknown`. On refusal it releases the reservation with reason `stopped_before_dispatch:<reason>` and raises `DispatchRefused`. Otherwise it sets `dispatching` and `dispatched_at` before any network call, which gives a durable marker that restart recovery can act on.
