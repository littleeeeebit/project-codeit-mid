`postgres.GatewayOwner` holds `pg_try_advisory_lock(GATEWAY_LOCK)` on one dedicated autocommit connection for the life of the process. Other `Resources` in the same process share it through `borrow_owner`, counting users, and the last `release()` closes the session and with it the lock. `check()` (called by `OpenAITransport._check_owner` before each call, and by `require_owner` before admission) verifies the lock is still granted and that recovery and validation are ready. Once ownership is lost it is permanent for the process: no reconnect, and the operator must restart and reconcile. `owner_guard(conn)` repeats the check inside the admission and dispatch transactions.
