`postgres.GatewayOwner` takes `pg_try_advisory_lock(GATEWAY_LOCK)` on a dedicated autocommit connection and registers itself in the `_owners` map. Other `Resources` in the same process borrow it through `borrow_owner`, which counts users. `check()` (called by `OpenAITransport._check_owner` before every call) and `owner_guard(conn)` (called inside the reserve and dispatch transactions) verify that the lock is still granted and that recovery and validation are ready. If the lock is lost, the owner is marked `lost` and never reconnects; a restart plus reconciliation is required.
