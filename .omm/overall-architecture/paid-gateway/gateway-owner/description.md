`GatewayOwner` keeps one dedicated autocommit psycopg connection that holds `pg_try_advisory_lock(GATEWAY_LOCK)` until shutdown. Other `Resources` objects in the same process share it through `borrow_owner`, and it is released with its last user. `check()` and `owner_guard(conn)` verify through `pg_locks` that the lock is still granted and that recovery and validation are ready. If ownership is lost, it is never reconnected: every later dispatch is refused, and the next owner recovers dispatched work as unknown.
