`GatewayOwner` holds `pg_try_advisory_lock(GATEWAY_LOCK)` on one dedicated autocommit connection for the life of the process. Several `Resources` in one process share it (`borrow_owner`), and the lock is released with the last user. `check()` verifies through `pg_locks` that the lock is still granted and that recovery and validation are ready. Losing the lock is permanent for the process: no reconnect, and every further dispatch is refused. `owner_guard(conn)` repeats the check inside admission and dispatch transactions.
