`postgres.GatewayOwner` holds `pg_try_advisory_lock(GATEWAY_LOCK)` on one dedicated connection until shutdown and never reconnects after losing it. `borrow_owner` lets another `Resources` in the same process share it. `require_owner` and `owner_guard` are checked at admission and dispatch, and refuse if the owner was lost, a recovery fence is open or validation is not ready. `postgres_backup` takes the same lock on a restore target.
