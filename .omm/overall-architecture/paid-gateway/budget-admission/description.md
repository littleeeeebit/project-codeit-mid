`budget.reserve` runs in one `tx(immediate=True)` and refuses with a reason in this order: `require_owner`/`owner_guard` (lost ownership or recovery not ready), `paid_disabled`, `postgresql_maintenance` (`database_control.paid_admission` false), `frozen`, `unknown_rate`, `unknown_purpose`, `above_consented_maximum`, `cap_exhausted` (cap − spent − pending < amount), `envelope_exhausted` (the purpose's envelope: interactive, gold_eval, embedding, judge_eval). Lock or connection errors fail closed as `ledger_unavailable`. Admission inserts an `attempts` row in `reserved`.
