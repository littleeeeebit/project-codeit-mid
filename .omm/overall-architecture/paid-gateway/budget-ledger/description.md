`budget.reserve` inserts an `attempts` row in state `reserved` for `max_cost(rates, input_tokens, max_output_tokens)`, pricing every input token at the cache-write rate, together with the price snapshot and the `BILLING_SCOPE` contextvar. The cap check uses `cap - spent - pending`, the envelope check uses `envelopes[purpose] - _purpose_used`, and `ceiling_micro_usd` is checked when given. Refusal reasons are returned rather than raised: `paid_disabled`, `postgresql_maintenance`, `frozen`, `unknown_rate`, `unknown_purpose`, `above_consented_maximum`, `cap_exhausted`, `envelope_exhausted` and `ledger_unavailable` (fail-closed on operational DB errors). `service._metered_chat` turns them into `budget_blocked` or `clarification_required` answers.
