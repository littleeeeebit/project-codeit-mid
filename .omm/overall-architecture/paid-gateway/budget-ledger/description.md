`budget.reserve` runs in one immediate transaction. It calls `require_owner` and `owner_guard`, then refuses with the first matching reason: paid_disabled, postgresql_maintenance (`database_control.paid_admission` is false), frozen, unknown_rate, unknown_purpose, above_consented_maximum, cap_exhausted (cap − spent − pending) or envelope_exhausted. Otherwise it inserts an `attempts` row in state `reserved`, with price_json holding the rates, the rate_version and the billing_scope of the member's key. `settle` prices uncached, cached, cache-write and output tokens exactly once: a duplicate returns the earlier result, and a late settlement of a reconciled attempt adds a negative adjustment. `snapshot` and `report` derive the totals, the cap warnings (50/75/90 %) and pacing for /api/budget. `_call_and_settle` in service.py reports provider usage back to this ledger.
