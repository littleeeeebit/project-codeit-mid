`reserve` checks, inside one `tx(immediate=True)`: gateway ownership, `paid_enabled`, `database_control.paid_admission`, `frozen_reason`, a known rate for the model, the purpose envelope (`interactive`, `embedding`, `gold_eval`, `judge_eval`, `ocr`), the consented ceiling, the global cap, and the envelope balance. It then inserts a `reserved` attempt priced by `max_cost`, which bills every input token at the cache-write rate, and stamps the `BILLING_SCOPE` context variable into `price_json`. `mark_dispatching` refuses and releases when the caller's guard (`service._stop_reason`) says cancelled or interrupted, when ownership is lost, during maintenance, or when any attempt is `unknown`. `settle` computes `micro_cost` from the reported usage and freezes the ledger (`frozen_reason`) when the cost exceeds the reservation. `reconcile`, `add_adjustment`, `set_envelopes`, `set_limit` and `set_paid_enabled` are owner operations, reached from the CLI or `service.set_budget_limit`.
