`reserve` makes an atomic admission in one transaction. It checks `owner_guard`, `paid_enabled`, `database_control.paid_admission`, `frozen_reason`, a known rate and purpose envelope, the consented ceiling, the cap minus spent and pending, and the remaining envelope, then inserts an `attempts` row in state `reserved` at `max_cost`, which prices all input at the cache-write rate. `mark_dispatching` refuses while any attempt is `unknown`. `settle` happens exactly once and freezes the ledger (`frozen_reason`) if the settled cost exceeds the reservation. `reconcile` and `add_adjustment` are owner CLI paths. `snapshot` feeds `/api/budget` and `open_paid_work`.
