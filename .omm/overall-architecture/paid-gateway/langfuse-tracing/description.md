`Tracing.from_settings` returns None (no client) unless the provider is `openai` and all three `LANGFUSE_*` values exist. The client has its own `TracerProvider` and a mask for secret-shaped values. `tracing.run` opens the root `answer-question` observation with `trace_id = create_trace_id(seed=request_id)`; `tracing.step` nests `retrieve-evidence`, `assemble-evidence`, `generate-answer` (model, usage and ledger cost through `usage_and_cost`) and `validate-answer`, and records nothing outside a run. Scores: `citation_valid`, `insufficient_evidence`, `evidence_tokens`. Drafting runs receive `res.tracing` too. Every `Step` method is guarded and never raises.
