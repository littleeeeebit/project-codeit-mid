`generation.validate_answer` parses the strict JSON payload (`answer_json_schema`), drops listed absences and applies `status_problem`. It then requires every claim, conflict alternative and summary evidence ID to exist, to belong to the cited document (comparison inferences may also cite the other side but must cite their own), and to have a quote equal to the stored chunk body (`evidence_quote_mismatch`). It refuses `source_absence_verified` from retrieval, an `answered` status without claims or summary evidence, and a comparison side missing from claims and missing fields. Inline `[E1]` markers are stripped from the text. `validate_rewrite` checks the rewritten standalone question's length. Failures raise `TechnicalError`, which becomes `technical_error`; the call stays billed and is not retried.
