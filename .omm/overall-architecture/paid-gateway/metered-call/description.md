`service._metered_chat` (and `dense.metered_embed` for embeddings) runs the steps in order. It calls `budget.reserve` with stage, purpose (`res.paid_purpose`: interactive, or gold_eval for pinned evaluation), model, exact input tokens, max output tokens and an optional consented ceiling. A refusal maps to `budget_blocked`, or to `clarification_required` for `above_consented_maximum`. `res.paid_refusal()` with no transport or key releases the attempt as `provider_unavailable`. `budget.mark_dispatching(attempt_id, _dispatch_guard)` raises `DispatchRefused`. For cancelled or interrupted that becomes a `_Stop`; any other ledger refusal becomes `budget_blocked`. `transport.chat` follows. On `ProviderError(pre_execution=True)` the attempt is released; on any other exception it is marked unknown. Missing usage marks it unknown. Otherwise `budget.settle` runs; a settlement exception marks the attempt unknown with `settlement_failed`. Every outcome is recorded into the request trace (`record["admission"|"usage"|"settlement"]`).
