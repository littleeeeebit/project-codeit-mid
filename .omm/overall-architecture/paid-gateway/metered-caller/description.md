The call sites that follow the reserve → dispatch → call → settle protocol: `service._metered_chat` + `_call_and_settle` (stages `query_rewrite` and `generation`), `dense.metered_embed` (stage `embedding`, from `query_vector` and `build_dense`), and, at entry-point level only, drafting, judges and answer runs, which charge `gold_eval`/`judge_eval`. `_metered_chat` maps admission refusals to `budget_blocked` (or `clarification_required` for `above_consented_maximum`). A missing key releases with `provider_unavailable`. `DispatchRefused` with a stop reason raises `_Stop`.
