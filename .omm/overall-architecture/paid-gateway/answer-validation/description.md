`generation.validate_answer(response, evidence, allowed_doc_ids, stored_quotes, required_doc_ids)` parses the structured `AnswerPayload`. It rejects unknown evidence IDs, scope mismatches, quote mismatches against the stored chunk text (`_stored_quote`), and claims, conflicts or summaries without evidence (`CITATION_ERRORS`). In compare mode it also rejects an answer that drops a side. `service._validated` wraps it in a `validate-answer` guardrail span and scores `citation_valid`. A failure becomes `technical_error` with the raw output kept in the trace.
