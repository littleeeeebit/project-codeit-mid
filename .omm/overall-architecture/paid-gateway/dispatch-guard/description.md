`mark_dispatching(db, attempt_id, guard)` runs the request's `_stop_reason` (cancelled, interrupted, closing service), the owner guard, paid admission, and a database-wide check for any `unknown` attempt, all in one transaction. On refusal the reservation becomes `released` with `stopped_before_dispatch:<reason>` and `DispatchRefused` is raised. Cancel and interrupt become `_Stop`, and ledger reasons become `budget_blocked`. On success the state becomes `dispatching` with `dispatched_at`, a durable marker that a call may have been sent.
