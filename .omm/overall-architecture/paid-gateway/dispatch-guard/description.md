`budget.mark_dispatching(db, attempt_id, guard)` runs `service._dispatch_guard` (the request's `_stop_reason`) in the same transaction as the state change. A refused guard releases the reservation with `stopped_before_dispatch:<reason>` and raises `DispatchRefused`. The service turns `cancelled` or `interrupted` into `_Stop` and other reasons into `budget_blocked`. Before calling, `_metered_chat` also releases with `provider_unavailable` when the member has no key (`paid_refusal`).
