`budget.mark_dispatching(attempt_id, guard)` runs the caller's guard in the same transaction (`service._stop_reason`: cancelled, interrupted, or service closing), then checks `owner_guard`, `paid_admission`, and whether any `unknown` attempt exists. A refusal moves the attempt to `released` with `stopped_before_dispatch:<reason>` and raises `DispatchRefused`. The service turns `cancelled` or `interrupted` into `_Stop` and anything else into `budget_blocked`. On success the attempt becomes `dispatching` with `dispatched_at`. This is the durable marker a restart turns into `unknown`.
