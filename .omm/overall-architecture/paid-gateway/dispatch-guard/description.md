`budget.mark_dispatching(attempt_id, guard)` runs in the same transaction as the request's `_stop_reason`: cancelled, interrupted, not running, or the service is closing. It also re-checks ownership and paid admission, and refuses whenever any attempt in the database is `unknown`. On refusal it releases the reservation and raises `DispatchRefused`; otherwise it moves the attempt to `dispatching` with `dispatched_at`. A shutdown or cancel therefore cannot slip in between the check and the network call.
