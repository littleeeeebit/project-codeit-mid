`budget.mark_dispatching(db, attempt_id, guard)` runs several checks in the same transaction that flips the attempt from `reserved` to `dispatching`: the request's `_stop_reason` (cancelled, interrupted or a closing service), `owner_guard`, the paid-admission flag, and the unknown-attempt check. On refusal it flips the attempt to `released` with `stopped_before_dispatch:<reason>` and raises `DispatchRefused`. A cancelled or shut-down request therefore cannot send a call after its check.
