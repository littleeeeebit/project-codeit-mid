`gateway/budget.py` maintains the `attempts`, `adjustments` and `budget_settings` tables. `reserve()` refuses with a reason and never raises. Reasons, checked in order: `gateway_ownership_lost` or recovery (`owner_guard`), `paid_disabled`, `postgresql_maintenance` (`database_control.paid_admission`), `frozen`, `unknown_rate`, `unknown_purpose`, `above_consented_maximum`, `cap_exhausted` (cap − spent − pending), `envelope_exhausted` (purposes `interactive`, `gold_eval`, `embedding` and `judge_eval`, shared 12:3:1:0), and `ledger_unavailable` on database errors. `mark_dispatching` repeats the stop and ownership checks in its own transaction. It also refuses while any attempt is `unknown`. A refusal releases the reservation and raises `DispatchRefused`. The owner CLI resolves billing through `reconcile` (provider totals per closed interval and billing scope), `settle` and `adjust`.
