Every paid provider call made by serving, CLI maintenance or verifier jobs goes through the shared PostgreSQL ledger in `gateway/budget.py` and the one SDK call site in `gateway/generation.py`. A call reserves its maximum cost, marks itself dispatching, then settles, releases or stays unknown. Langfuse tracing (`gateway/tracing.py`) only observes. Admission, reservation and settlement never read it, and a missing or failing Langfuse changes nothing.
