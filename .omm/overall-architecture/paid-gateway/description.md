Every OpenAI call in the package (answers, rewrites, query and corpus embeddings, OCR re-reads, drafting, judges, stage review candidates) is admitted by budget.reserve and dispatched only after budget.mark_dispatching. generation.py is the only SDK call site. The ledger is the attempts, adjustments and budget_settings tables, with per-purpose envelopes (embedding, gold_eval, interactive, judge_eval, ocr) under one cumulative cap.
