Every paid provider call follows the same sequence. `budget.reserve` admits the maximum cost atomically. `budget.mark_dispatching` writes a durable marker under the request's stop check, then the transport calls OpenAI. Afterwards `budget.settle` records the usage, `release` applies when the call provably never executed, and `mark_unknown` applies otherwise. Nothing is retried: the SDK runs with `max_retries=0` and the application never retries.
