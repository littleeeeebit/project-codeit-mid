Every paid provider call follows the same protocol. Admission (`budget.reserve`) reserves the maximum cost against the cap and the purpose envelope (`interactive`, `gold_eval`, `embedding`, `judge_eval`). `budget.mark_dispatching` writes a durable marker under the owning request's stop guard. Then the transport call runs, and the attempt ends as exactly one of `settle`, `release` (confirmed pre-execution) or `mark_unknown`. Nothing retries: SDK `max_retries=0`.
