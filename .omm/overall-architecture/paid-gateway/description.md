src/rfp_assistant/gateway: `budget.py` (ledger and admission), `generation.py` (prompts, JSON schemas, `OpenAITransport` / `FakeTransport`, answer validation) and `tracing.py` (Langfuse). It is the only OpenAI call site for answers, rewrites, drafting, judges, OCR fallback and embeddings. All cost accounting is in micro-USD on `attempts`, `adjustments` and `budget_settings`. Admission requires paid_enabled, `database_control.paid_admission`, no `frozen_reason`, a known rate, envelope room and cap room.
