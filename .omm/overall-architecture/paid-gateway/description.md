Every provider call goes through `_metered_chat` (chat) or `dense.query_vector` and `build_dense` (embeddings), and through the ledger in `gateway/budget.py`. `gateway/generation.py` is the only OpenAI SDK call site. Its messages, schema and validation are `build_messages`, `answer_json_schema`, `validate_answer`, `build_rewrite_messages` and `validate_rewrite`. `gateway/tracing.py` sends Langfuse traces that are observational only.
