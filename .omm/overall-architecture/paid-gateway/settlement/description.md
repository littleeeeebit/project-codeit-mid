`settle` runs exactly once and costs the provider usage with the reservation's stored rates. A settle on a 'reconciled' attempt inserts a negative late-settlement adjustment. A cost above the reservation sets `budget_settings.frozen_reason`, stopping new paid work until someone inspects it. `release(confirmed_pre_execution=True)` frees a call that never ran. `mark_unknown` keeps the reservation counted as pending when execution may have happened (transport error, no usage, failed settlement). Unknown attempts are resolved only by owner `reconcile` (closed interval, per billing scope) or `settle` from evidence.
