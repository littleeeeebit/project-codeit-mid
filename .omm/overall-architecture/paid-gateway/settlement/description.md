budget.settle computes micro-USD from the reported usage (prompt, cached, completion, cache-write) exactly once (a duplicate returns the stored value). If the cost exceeds the reservation, it sets budget_settings.frozen_reason, stopping all paid admission until inspection. A late settle of a `reconciled` attempt inserts a compensating adjustment. release(confirmed_pre_execution) is used for 4xx. mark_unknown covers a response without usage, transport errors after dispatch and settle failures. Unknown attempts keep their reservation as pending cost. reconcile (owner CLI) turns covered unknown attempts into `reconciled` against a provider total for one billing scope and interval.
