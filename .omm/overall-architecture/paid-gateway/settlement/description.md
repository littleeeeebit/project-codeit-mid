`budget.settle` runs exactly once per attempt from `dispatching`, `unknown` or `reconciled`. It prices prompt, cached, cache-write and completion tokens from the attempt's frozen `price_json`. A late settlement of a reconciled attempt adds a compensating adjustment. An overrun freezes the budget. `release` frees a reservation (from `dispatching` only when the provider confirmed pre-execution). `mark_unknown` keeps the reserved amount pending until owner reconciliation. `attempts_response` is a unique index on `(stage, model, response_id)`, which rejects a reused response ID.
