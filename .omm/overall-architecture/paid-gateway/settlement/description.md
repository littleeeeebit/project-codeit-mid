`service._call_and_settle` and `drafting._dispatch_and_settle`. A pre-execution `ProviderError` leads to `budget.release(confirmed_pre_execution=True)`. Any other error, or a response without usage, leads to `mark_unknown`. Usage leads to `budget.settle`, which runs exactly once (a duplicate returns the stored cost), prices uncached, cached and cache-write tokens from the reservation's frozen rates, and accepts states dispatching, unknown or reconciled. Settling a reconciled attempt adds a negative `late-settlement:` adjustment. A settled cost above the reservation sets `budget_settings.frozen_reason`, which stops all new paid work until inspected. A failed settlement write keeps the attempt unknown.
