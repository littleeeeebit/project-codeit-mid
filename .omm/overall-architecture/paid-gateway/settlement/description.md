`settle(db, attempt_id, usage, response_id)` settles exactly once (a duplicate returns `duplicate: True`), from dispatching, unknown or reconciled, at the rates frozen in the attempt's `price_json`. Settling a reconciled attempt adds a compensating negative adjustment (`late-settlement:<id>`). A settled cost above the reservation sets `budget_settings.frozen_reason`, which stops new admissions until the owner inspects and re-enables. `mark_unknown` moves dispatching → unknown. `release(confirmed_pre_execution=True)` moves dispatching → released for 4xx rejections. In `_paid_answer`, a response without usage, a non-ProviderError exception or a failed settlement all become unknown, so the cost stays conservatively pending.
