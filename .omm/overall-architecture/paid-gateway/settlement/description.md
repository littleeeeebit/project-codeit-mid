`_call_and_settle` releases with `confirmed_pre_execution` on a pre-execution error. Any other error, and a response without usage, becomes `mark_unknown`. Otherwise `budget.settle` computes the cost from the recorded rates exactly once (a duplicate returns the stored value). A cost above the reservation sets `budget_settings.frozen_reason`, which stops all new paid work until someone inspects it. A late settlement of an already reconciled attempt adds a negative `late-settlement:` adjustment. If settlement itself fails, the attempt is kept `unknown` and the request fails `technical_error`. Operators resolve unknowns with `settle_from_evidence`, or `reconcile` over a closed interval against a provider total for one billing scope.
