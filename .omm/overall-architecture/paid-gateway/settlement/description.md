settle() runs exactly once from dispatching, unknown or reconciled. It prices uncached, cached and cache-write tokens at the reservation's stored rates and stores raw usage and response_id. A late settle of a reconciled attempt adds a negative 'late-settlement' adjustment so the provider total is not double counted. A settlement above the reservation freezes the ledger. release() frees reserved (or dispatching, when confirmed pre-execution) attempts. mark_unknown() moves dispatching -> unknown with the error text.
