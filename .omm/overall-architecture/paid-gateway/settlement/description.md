`settle` runs exactly once. It prices the reported prompt, cached, completion and cache-write tokens at the reserved rates, and a duplicate call returns the earlier settlement. A late settlement of a `reconciled` attempt inserts a negative adjustment. A cost above the reservation sets `budget_settings.frozen_reason`, which stops all new paid work until someone inspects it. `release(confirmed_pre_execution=True)` handles provable 4xx failures. `mark_unknown` handles timeouts, missing usage and settlement failures. `recover` (on a new owner or an unclean close) moves `dispatching` to `unknown` and `reserved` to `released`, for both `attempts` and `external_attempts`.
