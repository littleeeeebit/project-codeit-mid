`postgres.GatewayOwner` holds `pg_try_advisory_lock(GATEWAY_LOCK)` on one dedicated autocommit connection. Several `Resources` in one process share it through `borrow_owner`, and the last user's `release()` closes it. `check()` and `owner_guard(conn)` confirm the lock is still granted and that recovery and validation are ready. They mark the owner lost on any psycopg error, and it never reconnects.
