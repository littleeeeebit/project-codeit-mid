`attempts` holds one row per paid stage call, with its state, reserved and settled micro-USD, token bounds, count method, `price_json` (rates, rate version, billing scope), raw usage, response ID and timestamps. `adjustments` holds append-only owner corrections, reconciliations and late-settlement offsets. `budget_settings` holds the cap, envelopes, rates, `paid_enabled`, `frozen_reason` and the project dates. `database_control.paid_admission` is the PostgreSQL-side switch. `external_attempts` is the separate Gemini ledger. `budget.snapshot` derives totals, pacing and the cap warnings (50/75/90%).
