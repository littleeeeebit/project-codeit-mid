src/rfp_assistant/corpus/ has three modules. ingestion.py validates data_list.csv (read_manifest_csv, import_manifest), parses each unique original once (ingest, then ingest_source) and writes extraction revisions. ocr.py's merge adds cached image-region text as image_text elements. fidelity.py compares an HWP extraction with a PDF printed by Hancom and records auto_verified or auto_flagged. The per-source path is under data-flow/ingest.
