`judges.plan` prices translations and Luna judge calls for a part (calibration, held_out, judge_set) and counts unpriced Jev calls. `judges.begin` publishes the run. `judges.run(settings, transport, estimate_id, actor, closing)` executes under the `judge_eval` envelope, then fits thresholds or scores agreement, kappa and the replacement verdict. `overview`, `results` and `disagreements` back the 검증 judge tab.
