`judge_set` builds a free judge golden set from mutated held-out positives (README). `judges.plan`, `begin`, `run`, `results` and `disagreements` compare a Luna judge with a Jev judge (English ladder), priced and run through the gateway under the `judge_eval` envelope. They are reached from `/api/verify/judges/*` and `cli run-judges`. Only the service-level entry points were traced.
