`gold.submit` inserts drafted candidates (from `service.submit_drafts`, after `evaluation.assign_families`, or from `cli gold submit`) as `gold_candidates` in state pending. `gold.decide` records the reviewer's decision with a required note. Inside the deciding transaction it renders approved rows into the dataset file (for example `.runtime/datasets/dev.jsonl`) and rejected rows into the rejection wiki (`rejections/*.md` plus `index.md`). `service.gold_decide` refuses approval by the person who started the drafting run (`requested_by` in `drafts/<run>/request.json`). Sealed test candidates are reviewed only through the owner CLI. `gold.check` verifies that the files equal their database rendering.
