`gold.submit` loads drafted candidates (from `service.submit_drafts` or the CLI `gold submit`) as `pending` rows in `gold_candidates`. `gold.decide` approves or rejects with a note. The reviewer must differ from the drafter and from whoever started the drafting run. A rejection is rendered into the rejection wiki, and `gold.infer` later adds an inferred reason. `second_review` records agreement. `repin` moves pending rows to a new extraction by element path after a parser revision. `gold.check` and `sync` keep the dataset file and wiki pages equal to their database rendering. Sealed `test` rows are reviewed only through the owner CLI.
