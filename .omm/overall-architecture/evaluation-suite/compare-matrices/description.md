`compare.MATRICES` (lexical, chunking, embedding, reranker) declare axes and fixed values. Each row is scored on three populations (dev in-document, dev whole corpus, needle set) and keeps the serving run's limits for axes it does not vary (`serving_base`, `row_settings`). Cached cells are reused. Paid corpus builds stop as `needs_approval` with an estimate until `compare --approve`. `ensure_dense` reuses a set, builds it locally, or builds it under an approved estimate. Tables go to `.runtime/compare/tables/<matrix>.json|md` and are listed by `service.experiments`. `compare.regression` is the maintenance table. Row execution internals were read only at signature level.
