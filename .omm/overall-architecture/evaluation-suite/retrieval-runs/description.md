`evaluate_retrieval` (CLI) runs K0/K1/D/H/HR retrieval-only over reviewed dev rows through `evaluation._execute`. It writes `.runtime/runs/<run_id>/` (`config.json`, `traces.jsonl`, `scores.json`, `report.md`), frozen by dataset hash, evaluated population, index manifest, analyzer, dense version and limits, and scored against source spans (hit@k, nDCG@5, MRR, packed completeness, critical failures, Wilson intervals). `run_errors` lists why a run cannot serve: incomplete, superseded `EVAL_VERSION`, gate, artifacts or corpus route. Verifier traces (`service.verifier_trace`) are separate frozen single-question runs that a member can turn into one paid answer.
