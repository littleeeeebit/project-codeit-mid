`compare --matrix lexical|chunking|embedding|reranker` (compare.MATRICES) varies one axis at a time on three populations: dev in-document, dev corpus-wide, and the needle set. It caches keyword indexes, vectors, reranker scores and finished cells. An uncached paid corpus build stops as a `needs_approval` row with a priced estimate until `compare --approve`. Tables are written to `compare/tables/<matrix>.{json,md}`, and `service.experiments` serves them to the verify screen.
