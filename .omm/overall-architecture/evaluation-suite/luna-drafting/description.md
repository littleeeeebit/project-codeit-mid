`service.start_drafting` re-prices the slots (`plan_drafting`). It refuses when the maximum exceeds the consented amount or no longer fits the `gold_eval` envelope or cap, writes `drafts/<run_id>/request.json` with `requested_by`, and runs `drafting._generate(..., guard, tracer)` on a background thread. Every call is reserved and settled in the shared ledger. `drafting_runs` derives the status from files (receipt → completed, error.txt → failed, interrupted.txt, live thread → running). `submit_drafts` refuses incomplete, empty or already-submitted runs, calls `evaluation.assign_families`, and then `gold.submit(... 'dev', drafting.DRAFTER)`. Drafting internals were not read in detail.
