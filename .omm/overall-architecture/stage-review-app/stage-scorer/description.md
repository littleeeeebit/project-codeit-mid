Every candidate is graded once, in the runner process, by the runner's own code. That guarantees one scorer for all refs. `score_retriever` computes nDCG@5 over the development questions on their own documents and needle top-5 hits over the whole corpus, against the evaluation's gold groups (`ev.load_eval_rows`, `_score_question`). `score_chunking` reports chunk-size distribution and table-title retention (`table_titles`, the `TITLE_LINES`/`TITLE_CHARS` rule fixed in the runner). `run.json` records the scorer's code fingerprint and `metric_code_sha256`.
