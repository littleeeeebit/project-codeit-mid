The Next.js static export in `review/src` has per-stage views (`retriever-view.tsx`, `chunking-view.tsx`, `generation-view.tsx`, `ocr-view.tsx`), span annotations (`annotations.tsx`, `note-field.tsx`), the decision panel, progress and the failure taxonomy (`taxonomy-view.tsx`). Taxonomy and suggestion replies carry `seq`, the count of committed history records, and the `newest()` reducer keeps the newest of overlapping replies. The '프롬프트 복사' actions call the export endpoints and copy the returned one-line prompt for the coding agent.
