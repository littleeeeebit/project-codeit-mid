For the paid stages, generation and OCR, every candidate prices its own work without a key, and the run stops at `needs_approval` with `estimate.json`. `approve` records the approver, refusing estimates older than 24 hours or larger than the envelope. `resume` re-checks inputs, worker, rates, refs and remaining budget, and only then hands `OPENAI_API_KEY` to the workers. Spending goes into the shared ledger with idempotency key `<run-id>:<candidate>:<question>:<n>`, so a billed item is never paid twice. `reprice` and `rescore` redo pricing or scoring without making calls. Per the README, `bidmate.service` holds the gateway lock and must be stopped for `resume` against codeit's database.
