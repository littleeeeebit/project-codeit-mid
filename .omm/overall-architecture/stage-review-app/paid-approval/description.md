`estimate` prices each candidate's recorded token bounds at the ledger's current rates and writes `estimate.json` with an expiry and a `fits` flag. `approve` requires a name, an unexpired estimate and `fits`, and writes `approved_by`/`approved_at` plus an audit record. `resume` calls `require_approved`, then `recheck` (input and worker hashes unchanged, rate version unchanged, the remaining outstanding maximum fits the envelope and cap, paid admission on), and refuses a ref that moved since pricing. `approved_env` is the only path by which `OPENAI_API_KEY` (and the Langfuse credentials for generation) reaches a worker, and only while the on-disk estimate is still the approved one.
