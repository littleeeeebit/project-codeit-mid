`_verify_routes` and `_dataset_routes` expose the verifier and dataset functions: traces (`service.run_trace`, `generate_from_run_id`, `compare_verifier_runs`), answer evaluation and judge plans and starts, maintenance status and start, experiments and `activate_experiment`, fidelity overview, page PNG and confirmation, ingestion overview, corrections and history, the gold queue, decide and second review, and drafting documents, slots, plan, start, runs and submit. `_dataset_routes` is registered after `/api/gold/recent` and similar routes, because `/api/gold/{candidate_id}` would otherwise shadow them.
