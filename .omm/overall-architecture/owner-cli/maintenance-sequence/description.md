maintenance.run executes STEPS in order: backup, restore_check (scratch database), ingest, fidelity (only HWP extractions without a verdict), keyword (rebuild without activating), embedding (free for a local model; a paid one raises NeedsApproval), regression (compare.regression: K1 and serving on the served and the rebuilt index) and report. It writes maintenance/state.json after every step and a report under maintenance/reports. It stops at the first failure or estimate. It ends `unverified` when a re-parse dropped questions from the rebuilt rows. It records serving before and after and never activates. Triggered by CLI `maintain` or POST /api/verify/maintenance/start.
