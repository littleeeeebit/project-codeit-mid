`maintenance.run` executes `STEPS` in order (backup, restore_check, ingest, fidelity, keyword, embedding, regression, report), writing state after each step. It stops at the first failure (`StepFailed`) or paid estimate (`NeedsApproval`), and ends `unverified` when the regression step finds questions whose pinned evidence was replaced by a reparse. It records serving before and after (it must stay unchanged) and the number of provider calls made, then writes `.runtime/maintenance/reports/<run_id>.json|md`. It is started by `cli maintain` or by `service.start_maintenance` (검증 → 유지보수) as a background job.
