`compare.py` runs declared axis matrices (`MATRICES`: lexical, chunking, embedding, reranker) over three populations (dev questions in their own document, dev over the whole corpus, the needle set). Only the declared axis varies; the other limits come from `serving_base`. Cells are cached (`.runtime/compare/cells/`) and every measured row is written as an activatable run. An uncached paid corpus build stops as a `needs_approval` row with a priced estimate until `compare --approve` records the person. OpenAI spend goes through the shared ledger, Gemini through its own cap. Tables are written to `.runtime/compare/tables/<matrix>.json|md` and shown on 검증 → 실험 비교.
