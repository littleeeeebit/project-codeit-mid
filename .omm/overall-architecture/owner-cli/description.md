`python -m rfp_assistant.cli <command>` is the owner's entry point. Commands that dispatch paid work use `service.Resources(settings)` (full owner) and refuse while the serving app holds the gateway lock. Budget administration (`unresolved`, `settle`, `reconcile`, `adjust`, `paid`, `audit`, `configure-budget`) uses `Resources(settings, dispatch=False)` and runs beside the serving app, recording the typed `--actor`. The CLI also exposes the offline pipelines (`ingest`, `build-keyword`, `build-dense`, `evaluate-retrieval`, `compare`, `activate-run`, `maintain`, `backup`, `restore-check`, `release-report`) and the checks (`check --phase`, `load-check`). On `codeit` it runs through the `tools/infra/bidmate-cli.sh` wrapper.
