RFP_DATA_DIR holds extracted/<hash>/<fingerprint>/elements.jsonl, indexes/<version>/, reviews/printed/<hash16>.pdf, ocr/<hash>/, runs/<run_id>/ (config, traces, scores), compare/ tables and estimates, datasets/, sealed/, releases/, maintenance/ and drafts. Originals are under RFP_SOURCE_DIR/files. Artifacts are written atomically (write_text_atomic, write_jsonl_atomic) and are never mutated in place, so issued citations and pinned gold rows keep resolving.
