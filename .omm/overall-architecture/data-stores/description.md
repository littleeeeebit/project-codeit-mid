PostgreSQL (bidmate_app) is the only database: documents, sources, extractions, elements, indexes, chunks, requirements, requests, attempts, adjustments, budget_settings, audit_events, verifier_runs, gold_candidates and reviews, activations, app_settings, fidelity_checks and the pgvector tables. Access goes through store.open_db/tx wrappers over postgres.Connection; `?` placeholders are rewritten by bind_sql. Immutable artifacts (extraction elements.jsonl, keyword index directories, printed PDFs, OCR caches, run directories, datasets) live under RFP_DATA_DIR (`.runtime`). Recorded Windows paths are mapped on the VM through postgres.host_path and RFP_PATH_MAP.
