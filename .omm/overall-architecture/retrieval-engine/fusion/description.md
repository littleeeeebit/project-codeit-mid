`fuse` computes weighted RRF (`rrf_fuse`, k from `rrf_k`, dense weight `dense_weight`). With `fusion='keyword_first'`, the first `keyword_head` BM25 rows stay in BM25 order and the rest follow in RRF order, truncated to `fused_top_k`. HR applies `LocalReranker.rerank` to the top `rerank_depth` of the fused pool. An inference error serves the H order, marked as fallback `hybrid_rerank->hybrid:reranker_error`.
