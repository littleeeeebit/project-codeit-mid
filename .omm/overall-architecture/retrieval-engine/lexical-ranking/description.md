Inside `retrieval.retrieve`: an All-documents scope (more than 2 pairs) is first narrowed by `route_corpus` (rule `greedy-rare-term-1`). Exact requirement codes (`CODE_RE`) are pulled ahead of every ranker. Query terms that cannot discriminate in the scope are dropped by `scope_redundant_terms` and listed as `scope_redundant_terms:`. `rank_lexical` then scores the allowed rows. A question that only names its project (`name_only`) skips lexical ranking and ranks the named documents by dense similarity.
