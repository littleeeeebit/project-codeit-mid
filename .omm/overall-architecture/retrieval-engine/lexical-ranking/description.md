Exact requirement codes (`CODE_RE`) are matched first and stay ahead of every ranker. These matches can record `code_not_found`, `code_detail_unavailable` or `code_ambiguous`. `scope_redundant_terms` drops non-discriminating query terms inside the scope: terms present in more than half of the scope's chunks, and the selected project's restated name terms. Codes, digits and negations are never dropped, and nothing is dropped unless a kept term still matches the scope. `rank_lexical` runs BM25 `get_batch_scores` over the allowed rows only, keeps scores above zero and breaks ties by chunk ID.
