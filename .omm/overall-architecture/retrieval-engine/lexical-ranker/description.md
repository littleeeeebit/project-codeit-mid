`rank_lexical` runs BM25 over the allowed rows only and drops zero scores; ties break by chunk_id. For K1 with one or two selected documents, `scope_redundant_terms` drops query terms present in more than half of the scope's chunks, plus the restated project-name terms. Codes, digits and negations are never dropped, and nothing is dropped unless a remaining term still matches the scope. Dropped terms are recorded as `scope_redundant_terms:`. Requirement codes found by `CODE_RE` become an `exact` channel that is always ranked first. Unmatched or ambiguous codes are reported as limitations (`code_not_found`, `code_ambiguous`).
