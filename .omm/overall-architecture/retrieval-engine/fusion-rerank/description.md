`_candidates` records every channel's ranked candidates. For H and HR it fuses the lexical and dense IDs with `fuse` (weighted RRF, keyword-first head per the run's fusion setting). `_rerank` keeps the protected BM25 head in place and reranks the pool below it to `rerank_depth` with `LocalReranker`, a GPU model loaded under a model lock. An inference exception serves the H order with a `hybrid_rerank->hybrid:reranker_error` fallback.
