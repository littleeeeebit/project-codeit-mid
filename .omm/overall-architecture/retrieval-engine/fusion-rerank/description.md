`_candidates` emits candidates per channel. For hybrid modes, `fuse` applies weighted RRF (`rrf_fuse`, k 60) with the run's keyword-first head; per the README, the BM25 top 6 keep BM25 order. For hybrid_rerank, `_rerank` keeps up to `rerank_protect` leading BM25 rows in place and reranks the pool below them to `depth` with `dense.LocalReranker`, which runs under a model lock with concurrency 1. An inference error serves the H order, marked `hybrid_rerank->hybrid:reranker_error`.
