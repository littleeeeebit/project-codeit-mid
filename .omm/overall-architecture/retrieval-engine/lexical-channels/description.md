`_exact_matches` finds code identifiers (`CODE_RE`), and those rows stay ahead of every ranker. `_lexical_matches` runs Kiwi BM25 (K1) or whitespace BM25 (K0) over the allowed rows. It drops scope-redundant query terms (non-positive scope-local IDF, plus the project's own title terms when the question restates them) and records `scope_redundant_terms:`. Codes, digits and negations are never dropped.
