`fuse` computes weighted RRF (`rrf_k`, `dense_weight`). With `fusion == 'keyword_first'` the first `keyword_head` BM25 rows stay in BM25 order and the rest follow in RRF order, truncated to `fused_top_k`. In `hybrid_rerank`, `reranker.rerank(question, pool)` reorders rows below a protected BM25 head (`rerank_protect`) up to `rerank_depth`. An inference error falls back to the H order with a `reranker_error` fallback string. Rerank queue and inference milliseconds and truncation counts go into the timings and limitations.
