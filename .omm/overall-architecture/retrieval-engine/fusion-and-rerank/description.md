`fuse` applies weighted RRF (`rrf_k` 60, `dense_weight`). With `fusion == keyword_first`, the leading `keyword_head` BM25 rows (6) stay in BM25 order ahead of the RRF tail, and the result is cut at `fused_top_k`. `hybrid_rerank` reranks the pool below a protected BM25 head with the loaded local reranker. An inference exception falls back to the hybrid order, and the trace records the reason.
