`_candidates` fuses the lexical and dense rankings. `rrf_fuse` and `fuse` implement weighted RRF, `1/(k+rank)`. Per the README, the serving geometry `keyword_first:60:1.0:6` keeps the BM25 top 6 in order. In `hybrid_rerank`, `_rerank` scores the fused head with the local reranker (`dense.load_reranker`, which `Resources.reranker()` caches by model, revision, length, concurrency and precision). `rerank_protect` keeps the leading BM25 rows in place. A reranker error falls back to `hybrid`.
