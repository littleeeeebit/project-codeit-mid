`vector_store.PgDenseIndex.load` performs the identity checks over `embedding_sets`, `embedding_set_rows` and `embedding_payloads` (checksums, model/dimension/policy homogeneity, extraction scope), and explicitly refuses NumPy as a serving fallback. Query vectors are cached by normalized text, model and dimensions (`cache_get`/`cache_put` over `embedding_payloads` rows). A corrupt entry counts as a miss.
