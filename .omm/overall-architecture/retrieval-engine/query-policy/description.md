`scope_redundant_terms` (policy `scope-redundant-2`) drops query terms present in more than half of the scope's chunks (when the scope has at least 8 rows), and drops the selected document's own title/institution terms from the frozen `scope-terms.json` when the question restates two or more of them. Codes, digits and negations are protected, and nothing is dropped unless a kept term still matches the scope. Dropped terms are reported as `scope_redundant_terms:`. K0 (whitespace) skips this policy and stays the plain baseline.
