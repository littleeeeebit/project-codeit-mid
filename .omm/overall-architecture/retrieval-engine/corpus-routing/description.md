`_routed_scope`: for the all-documents scope (more than two pairs from `corpus_scope`), `route_corpus` narrows to the documents whose frozen title and institution terms the question names. Generic title words like 대학교 or 사업 narrow nothing. The function notes `index_includes_unreviewed_sources` and `scope_not_indexed:`. `_name_only` and `_named_rows` detect a question that only names a project; it is then ranked by dense similarity within the named documents (`name_only_question:dense_within_named_documents`).
