For the all-documents scope (more than two pairs), `route_corpus` keeps only the documents whose frozen title and institution terms the question restates: at least `METADATA_RESTATEMENT_MIN` terms, weighted by IDF, greedily on terms no better match already explains, and at most `ROUTE_MAX` documents. Otherwise it returns `[]` and the whole corpus stays in scope. Routing adds the limitation `corpus_routed:`. A question that only names the project (`name_only_question`) drops lexical ranking and ranks the named documents by dense similarity.
