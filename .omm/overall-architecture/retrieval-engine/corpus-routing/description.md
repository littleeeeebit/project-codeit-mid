`_routed_scope` handles the all-documents scope (more than 2 pairs). There, `route_corpus` narrows the scope to at most ROUTE_MAX = 4 documents whose frozen title and institution terms the question names. The rule, greedy-rare-term-1, is recorded in run configurations, and generic project words narrow nothing. `scope_rows` turns the scope into allowed row ids, so nothing outside the scope can be scored. `_name_only` detects a question that only names its project; then BM25 is skipped and dense ranks within the named documents (`name_only_question:dense_within_named_documents`).
