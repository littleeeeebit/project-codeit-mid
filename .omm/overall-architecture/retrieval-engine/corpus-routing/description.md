For the all-documents scope (more than two pairs), `route_corpus` keeps the documents whose own title and institution terms the question restates: at least two terms, at least one of them rarer than `ROUTE_MAX`, weighted by IDF, greedy over unexplained terms, with share 0.5. It applies no routing when nothing matches or more than 4 documents match. Requirement codes (`CODE_RE`) found in the question become exact-match candidates that rank ahead of every ranker. Their absence or ambiguity is recorded as `code_not_found:`, `code_detail_unavailable:` or `code_ambiguous:`.
