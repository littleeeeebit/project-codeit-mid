For the all-documents scope (`corpus_scope`: every indexed original), `route_corpus` narrows the search to the documents a question names, using the frozen title and institution terms. Generic title words such as 대학교 or 사업 narrow nothing. The limitation records `corpus_routed:<doc_ids>`. A name-only question (`_name_only`: it names its documents and asks nothing else) skips lexical scoring; `_named_rows` ranks those documents' rows by meaning only.
