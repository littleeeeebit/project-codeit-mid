`route_corpus` applies only when the scope has more than two documents (corpus mode). It keeps documents whose frozen title and institution terms the question restates: at least `METADATA_RESTATEMENT_MIN` = 2 terms, at least one of them rare (df < `ROUTE_MAX` = 4), weighted by IDF and taken greedily best first. A document needs `ROUTE_SHARE` = 0.5 of the top weight on terms not already explained. It returns no routing when nothing, or more than four documents, match. The rule is recorded as `corpus_route_record()`. `active_serving` stops serving an activated run measured under another rule (`activated_corpus_route_requires_rerun`), and `run_errors` refuses to activate one.
