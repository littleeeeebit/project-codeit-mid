The packer walks the final ranking (exact matches first, deduplicated). `admit` excludes chunks for `other_requirement_code`, `unit_limit` (`evidence_max_units`), `duplicate_span` (`_overlaps` on element offsets or table rows; a shared header row alone does not count) and `token_budget`. The first unit may use `evidence_max_tokens`; later units fit within `evidence_target_tokens`. After each admitted chunk, up to `LINKED_EXTRA_UNITS=2` sibling pieces of a split element or row follow, nearest first. A sibling left out is reported as `linked_evidence_missing:`. Each unit is an `EvidenceUnit` (E1..En) with doc_id, source_hash, extraction_id, chunk_id, element_ids, quote (the chunk body), location and token_count. Out-of-scope rows are excluded as `outside_scope` as a defensive check.
