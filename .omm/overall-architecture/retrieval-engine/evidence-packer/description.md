The packing loop walks the final ranking and admits chunks as `EvidenceUnit(E1..)`. A chunk is excluded with a stated reason: `other_requirement_code`, `unit_limit`, `duplicate_span` (overlapping spans or table rows), `token_budget` (the first unit may use `evidence_max_tokens`, later ones `evidence_target_tokens`) or `outside_scope`. Linked sibling pieces of a split element or row are pulled in nearest-first, up to `LINKED_EXTRA_UNITS`. A piece left out is reported as `linked_evidence_missing:`. The result carries candidates, exclusions, limitations, per-stage timings and `index_version`/`dense_version` for the trace.
