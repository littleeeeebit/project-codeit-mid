The packer walks the final ranking (exact matches first) and admits chunks as `EvidenceUnit(E1..En)` carrying doc_id, source_hash, extraction_id, chunk_id, element_ids, quote (the chunk body), location and token_count. It stops a chunk at `evidence_max_units`, rejects duplicate spans, enforces the token budget (`evidence_max_tokens` for the first unit, `evidence_target_tokens` after it), and rejects other requirement codes. Up to `LINKED_EXTRA_UNITS = 2` sibling pieces of a split element are packed next to their chunk; when a sibling is left out, `linked_evidence_missing:*` is reported. Excluded chunks are recorded with a reason, and per-stage timings go into `timings_ms`.
