Exact requirement-code matches (CODE_RE in the question against chunk requirement_key) lead the ranking. admit() rejects chunks with another requirement code, past evidence_max_units, overlapping spans already picked (_overlaps), or over the token budget (evidence_max_tokens for the first unit, evidence_target_tokens after). It then adds up to LINKED_EXTRA_UNITS sibling pieces of a split element, recording linked_evidence_missing when one is left out. Output is EvidenceUnit E1..En with doc_id, source_hash, extraction_id, element_ids, quote (chunk body) and location, plus excluded reasons, timings and limitations.
