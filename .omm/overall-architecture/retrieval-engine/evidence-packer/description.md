`_pack_evidence` walks the ranking (exact matches first) and admits chunks as `EvidenceUnit(E1..En)` with doc, source hash, extraction, chunk, element IDs, quote and location. It enforces `evidence_max_units`, the token budgets (`evidence_max_tokens` for the first unit, `evidence_target_tokens` after), overlapping-span deduplication and other-requirement-code exclusion. It adds up to `LINKED_EXTRA_UNITS` sibling pieces of a split element and records `linked_evidence_missing` when one is left out. Exclusions and their reasons go to `excluded`.
