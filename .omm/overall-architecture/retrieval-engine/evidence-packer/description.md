Walks the ranking and admits chunks as EvidenceUnit E1..En carrying doc_id, source_hash, extraction_id, chunk_id, element_ids, quote (the chunk body), location and token_count. Exclusions are other_requirement_code, unit_limit (evidence_max_units), duplicate_span, token_budget (evidence_max_tokens for the first unit, evidence_target_tokens afterwards) and outside_scope. Linked split siblings are added nearest first up to LINKED_EXTRA_UNITS; a missed sibling is reported as linked_evidence_missing.
