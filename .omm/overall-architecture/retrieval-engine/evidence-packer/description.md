`_pack_evidence` walks the final ranking, with exact matches first, and admits chunks as `EvidenceUnit(E1…)`. A unit has doc/source/extraction/chunk IDs, element IDs, the chunk body as the quote, the location, and a token count. Admission is limited by `evidence_max_units`, `evidence_max_tokens` for the first unit and `evidence_target_tokens` after it. It excludes other requirement codes, overlapping spans (`duplicate_span`) and out-of-scope rows. Linked split pieces of an oversized element are added nearest first up to `LINKED_EXTRA_UNITS`. A piece left out is reported as `linked_evidence_missing:`, never silently dropped.
