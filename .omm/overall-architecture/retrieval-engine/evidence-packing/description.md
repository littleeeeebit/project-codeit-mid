`_pack_evidence` walks the ranking (exact matches first) and admits chunks within `evidence_target_tokens` / `evidence_max_tokens`. It adds the linked pieces of a split element or row, nearest first, so a condition stays next to its fact. A missing linked piece records `linked_evidence_missing:`. The result is a `RetrievalResult`: evidence units numbered E1…, excluded candidates, limitations, timings and the ranking.
