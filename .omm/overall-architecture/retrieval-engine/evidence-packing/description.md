`_pack_evidence` walks `exact + ordered` and packs chunks into `EvidenceUnit`s (E1, E2, ...) within `evidence_target_tokens` and `evidence_max_tokens`. Linked split pieces stay together or produce `linked_evidence_missing`. The output is `RetrievalResult` (evidence, excluded, candidates, ranking, timings_ms, index_version, dense_version). For a follow-up, `service._with_carried` appends the previous answer's cited units that the new retrieval lacks, renumbered after it, and drops any whose stored quote can no longer be checked.
