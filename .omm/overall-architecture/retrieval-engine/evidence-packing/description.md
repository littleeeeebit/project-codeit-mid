Walks the ranking (exact matches first). `admit` refuses `other_requirement_code`, `unit_limit` (`evidence_max_units`), `duplicate_span` (overlapping spans) and `token_budget`. The first unit may use `evidence_max_tokens` and later units `evidence_target_tokens`. Linked sibling pieces of a split element are added nearest first, up to `LINKED_EXTRA_UNITS` = 2; a sibling left out is reported as `linked_evidence_missing`. Units are numbered E1..En with doc, source hash, extraction, element IDs, quote and location. `_prepare_compare` renumbers the units of both sides and merges coverage per document.
