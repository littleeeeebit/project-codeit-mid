`_pack_evidence` walks the ranking and admits `EvidenceUnit`s (`E1…`) with the chunk's quote, element IDs and location. It stops at `evidence_max_units` and the token budget (max tokens for the first unit, the target for the rest). It skips overlapping spans and other requirement codes. It also pulls up to 2 linked sibling pieces of a split element so a condition stays with its fact. Every exclusion is recorded with a reason, and every sibling left out as `linked_evidence_missing`.
