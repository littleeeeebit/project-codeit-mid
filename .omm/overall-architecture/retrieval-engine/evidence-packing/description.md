Walks the final ranking and admits EvidenceUnits (E1…En). Each carries doc_id, source_hash, extraction_id, chunk_id, element_ids, the quote (chunk body), location and token count. Admission enforces evidence_max_units, evidence_max_tokens for the first unit and evidence_target_tokens after it, duplicate-span overlap and other_requirement_code. It then adds up to LINKED_EXTRA_UNITS sibling pieces of a split element, recording `linked_evidence_missing` when one is left out. Timings per stage go into timings_ms.
