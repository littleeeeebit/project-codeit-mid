`_pack_evidence` walks `exact + ordered` and packs evidence units up to the evidence token limits. It adds up to `LINKED_EXTRA_UNITS` sibling pieces of a split element, so a condition stays with its fact, and otherwise reports `linked_evidence_missing`. The resulting `RetrievalResult` carries the evidence (E1..En), excluded units, candidates per channel, ranking, `timings_ms` per stage, `fallback`, `dense_version` and limitations. The prompt (`_priced`) and the trace (`_ranked_chunks`) both consume it.
