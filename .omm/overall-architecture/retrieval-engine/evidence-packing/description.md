`_pack_evidence` walks the final ranking, with exact matches always first. It skips chunks for other requirement codes, overlapping spans, units past `evidence_max_units`, and units over the token budget (the first unit may use `evidence_max_tokens`, later ones `evidence_target_tokens`). With a picked piece of a split element, it adds up to LINKED_EXTRA_UNITS = 2 sibling pieces, and reports `linked_evidence_missing` for any it leaves out. Each `EvidenceUnit` carries doc_id, source_hash, extraction_id, chunk_id, element_ids, the exact chunk body as the quote, the location and the token count.
