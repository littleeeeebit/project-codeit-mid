`_pack_evidence` walks the ranking and admits chunks as `EvidenceUnit` `E1..En`, each carrying doc_id, source_hash, extraction_id, chunk_id, element_ids, the quote (chunk body), location and token_count. Admission stops at `evidence_max_units` and the token budgets (the first unit against `evidence_max_tokens`, later ones against `evidence_target_tokens`). It skips overlapping spans and other requirement codes, and adds up to `LINKED_EXTRA_UNITS` linked split siblings, nearest first, so a condition stays with its fact. A sibling it cannot add becomes `linked_evidence_missing:`. Every exclusion is returned with its reason for the trace.
