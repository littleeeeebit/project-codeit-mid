The final ranking is exact code matches, then the fused or reranked order. `admit()` packs chunks into `EvidenceUnit`s (`E1…`) until `evidence_max_units` is reached. The first unit may use up to `evidence_max_tokens` and later units up to `evidence_target_tokens`. Chunks are excluded for another requirement code, a duplicate span (`_overlaps`), the token budget or being outside the scope. Up to `LINKED_EXTRA_UNITS` (2) sibling pieces of a split element are packed next to their parent; a sibling that does not fit is reported as `linked_evidence_missing`. The location comes from the element map (page, table rows, section path).
