`_pack_evidence` turns the ranking into `EvidenceUnit`s (E1..En, quote, element_ids, location, token_count) within `evidence_max_units` and the token limits, keeping linked split pieces together. Excluded chunks are kept with reasons. In the service, `_priced` then builds messages (`generation.build_messages`), counts tokens with tiktoken o200k_base, and prices the maximum with `budget.estimate`, adding the query-embedding estimate on a cache miss.
