`_exact_matches`: requirement codes found by `CODE_RE` in the NFC question select rows with that `requirement_key`, detail rows first. They record `code_not_found`, `code_detail_unavailable` or `code_ambiguous` and always rank ahead of every ranker. `_lexical_matches`: `scope_redundant_terms` drops terms present in more than half the scope's chunks, and the selected project's restated name terms for 1–2 document scopes. It never drops codes, digits or negations, and drops only when a kept term still matches. `rank_lexical` then scores BM25 over admitted rows only, returns no zero-score rows, and breaks ties by chunk ID.
