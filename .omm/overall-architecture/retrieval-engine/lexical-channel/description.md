Exact requirement codes (`CODE_RE` matched against `requirement_key`, detail chunks first) always rank ahead and report `code_not_found`, `code_detail_unavailable` or `code_ambiguous`. `scope_redundant_terms` drops terms present in more than half of the scope's chunks (at least 8 rows), and the project's own restated name terms for one or two selected documents. Codes, digits and negations are never dropped, and nothing is dropped unless a kept term still matches. `rank_lexical` scores BM25 only over allowed rows and returns nothing rather than zero-score rows. A name-only question (hybrid modes) clears the lexical list and ranks the named documents by dense similarity (`name_only_question:dense_within_named_documents`).
