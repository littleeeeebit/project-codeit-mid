`_exact_matches` puts chunks whose `requirement_key` equals an asked code (`CODE_RE`) first, detail rows ahead of summaries, and notes `code_not_found`, `code_detail_unavailable` or `code_ambiguous`. `_lexical_matches` drops scope-redundant query terms first: terms in more than half of the scope's chunks, and for one or two selected documents their own restated name terms. Codes, digits and negations are never dropped. It then ranks BM25 over the allowed rows up to `channel_top_k`. K0 uses `WhitespaceAnalyzer` and no dropping.
