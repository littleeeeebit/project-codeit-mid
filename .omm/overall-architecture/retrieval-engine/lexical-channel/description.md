`_exact_matches` finds chunks that contain identifier codes (`CODE_RE`), and those stay ahead of every ranker. `_lexical_matches` and `rank_lexical` score Kiwi BM25 with analyzer `kiwi-bm25-2`, which collapses letter-spaced headings and applies `SPACING_ALIASES`. IDF is computed globally and masked to the scope. `scope_redundant_terms` (policy `scope-redundant-2`) drops query terms that cannot discriminate within the scope, but never codes, digits or negations. The dropped terms appear in the limitations.
