Identifiers come first: `CODE_RE` codes in the question match `requirement_key` rows, detail rows first. Missing, summary-only or ambiguous codes are recorded as limitations. Then `rank_lexical` scores BM25 over the allowed rows, with IDF over the whole index (`idf:global`). Before that, `scope_redundant_terms` drops terms that appear in more than half of the scope's chunks or restate the selected project's name. Codes, digits and negations are never dropped, and the dropped terms are listed as `scope_redundant_terms:`.
