`rank_lexical` runs BM25Okapi over Kiwi tokens (`Analyzer`, which normalizes letter-spaced headings and a short alias list), restricted to `allowed` rows of the scope with global IDF. `scope_redundant_terms` drops query terms that cannot discriminate inside the scope: terms in more than half the scope's chunks, and the selected project's own name terms when the question restates them. Codes, digits and negations are never dropped. K0 (`whitespace_bm25`) uses `WhitespaceAnalyzer` with no dropping, as the baseline.
