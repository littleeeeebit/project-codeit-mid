src/rfp_assistant/retrieval. `service.retrieve` resolves the scope to (DocRef, extraction_id) pairs, or to `corpus_scope` for all documents. For dense modes it obtains a query vector through `dense.query_vector`, then calls `retrieval.retrieve` inside a `retrieve-evidence` tracing step. Modes: `whitespace_bm25` (K0), `kiwi_bm25` (K1), `dense` (D), `hybrid` (H) and `hybrid_rerank` (HR).
