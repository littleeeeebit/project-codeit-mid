`service.retrieve` resolves the scope, which is either (DocRef, extraction) pairs or `corpus_scope` for all documents. It reads `active_serving` and calls `retrieval.retrieve` with the activated mode: K0 `whitespace_bm25`, K1 `kiwi_bm25`, D `dense`, H `hybrid` or HR `hybrid_rerank`. Per the README, the current serving run `H-0fffb2a6ec` is hybrid keyword-first fusion over exact pgvector search: the BM25 top 6 stay in place, weighted RRF orders the rest (k 60), each channel takes 50, and 10 evidence units are packed. The result is a `RetrievalResult` with candidates, ranking, packed `EvidenceUnit`s, exclusions, `limitations` and `fallback`.
