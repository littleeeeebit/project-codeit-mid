`service.retrieve` resolves the scope (one or two documents, or `corpus_scope` for all documents), loads the active `KeywordIndex` and the activated configuration, and calls `retrieval.retrieve(settings, index, analyzer, question, scope, mode, dense, query_vector, reranker)`. It wraps that call in a `retrieve-evidence` tracing step. Modes are K0 `whitespace_bm25`, K1 `kiwi_bm25`, D `dense`, H `hybrid` and HR `hybrid_rerank`. A mode whose dependency is missing falls back visibly (`_available_mode` sets `fallback`, for example `hybrid->kiwi_bm25:query_vector_unavailable`). The result is a `RetrievalResult` with candidates per channel, the ranking, packed `EvidenceUnit`s, exclusions with reasons, limitations and per-stage timings.
