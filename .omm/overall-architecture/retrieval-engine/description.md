retrieval.retrieve is a pure function of (settings, KeywordIndex, analyzer, question, scope pairs, mode, dense set, query vector, reranker). service.retrieve supplies the activated configuration: res.serving() plus run_settings() overrides (embedding model and dimensions, depths, RRF k, evidence limits measured by the activated run). Modes: whitespace_bm25 (K0), kiwi_bm25 (K1), dense (D), hybrid (H), hybrid_rerank (HR). Missing dense/vector/reranker prerequisites fall back to kiwi_bm25 or hybrid and record why in `fallback` and `limitations`.
