`retrieval.retrieve(settings, index, analyzer, question, scope_pairs, mode, dense, query_vector, reranker)` scores only rows of the selected extractions (`scope_rows`). Modes are `whitespace_bm25` (K0), `kiwi_bm25` (K1), `dense` (D), `hybrid` (H: K1+D by RRF) and `hybrid_rerank` (HR). A missing or mismatched dense matrix, query vector or reranker falls back visibly through `fallback` (for example `hybrid->kiwi_bm25:query_vector_unavailable`). Requirement codes in the question (`CODE_RE`) are exact-matched first and stay ahead of every ranker. `service.retrieve` wraps it: it resolves scope, applies the activated mode or a verifier's free override, narrows limits, and obtains a query vector through `dense.query_vector`, paying only when `allow_paid` and a `request_id` exist. It appends `index_outdated:` when `index_compatibility` reports an analyzer or snapshot mismatch, and `<stage>_unavailable` limitations from `stage_errors`.
