Requirement codes found in the question (`CODE_RE`) select every allowed chunk with that `requirement_key`, `requirement_detail` chunks first. They go ahead of every ranker in the final order. Limitations record `code_not_found:`, `code_detail_unavailable:` and `code_ambiguous:`. During packing, a chunk carrying a different code is excluded as `other_requirement_code`, so SFR-0010 does not answer SFR-001.
