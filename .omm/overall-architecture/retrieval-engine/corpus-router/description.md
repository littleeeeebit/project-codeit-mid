The scope is the selected (DocRef, extraction_id) pairs, or for mode corpus every indexed active extraction (corpus_scope, first document per original by CSV order). Only scopes larger than two run route_corpus. It keeps documents whose frozen title terms the question restates (at least 2 terms, at least one in fewer than ROUTE_MAX=4 titles), greedily by IDF weight with ROUTE_SHARE 0.5. It returns no routing when nothing or more than 4 documents match. ROUTE_RULE is recorded in frozen runs. active_serving refuses to serve an activated run whose limits.corpus_route differs.
