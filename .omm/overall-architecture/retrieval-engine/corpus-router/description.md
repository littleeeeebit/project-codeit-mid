Only for scopes larger than two (the all-documents scope from `corpus_scope`: each indexed active extraction once, cited under its first CSV document). `route_corpus` keeps the documents whose own title and institution terms (frozen in the index as `scope-terms.json`) the question restates. It needs at least two terms and at least one term rarer than `ROUTE_MAX=4` titles. Documents are taken best first by IDF weight; each must carry at least `ROUTE_SHARE=0.5` of the top weight on terms not already explained. No match, or more than four matches, means no routing. A routed scope appends `corpus_routed:<doc_ids>`.
