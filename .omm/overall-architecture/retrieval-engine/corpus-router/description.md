When the scope has more than 2 documents (the all-documents scope), `route_corpus` narrows it to the projects the question names, and the trace records `corpus_routed:`. Name-only questions in hybrid modes (`name_only`) drop the lexical channel and rank the named documents by meaning (`name_only_question:dense_within_named_documents`). Generic title words appearing in more than `ROUTE_MAX` titles narrow nothing.
