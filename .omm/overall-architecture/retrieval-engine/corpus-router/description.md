Only the all-documents scope has more than two pairs. For that scope, `route_corpus` keeps the documents whose own frozen title/institution terms the question restates: at least `METADATA_RESTATEMENT_MIN` terms, weighted by IDF across titles, taking documents best-first on terms not already explained, and returning nothing when more than `ROUTE_MAX` match. It records `corpus_routed:<ids>`. A 'name-only' hybrid question ranks the named documents by dense similarity alone (`name_only_question:dense_within_named_documents`). Activation refuses runs measured under a different `corpus_route_record()`.
