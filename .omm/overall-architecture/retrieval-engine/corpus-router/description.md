For the All documents scope (more than 2 scope pairs, built by `corpus_scope`), `route_corpus` (retrieval.py:425) keeps the documents whose frozen title and institution terms (`index.scope_terms`, from scope-terms.json) the question restates at least twice, weighted by IDF and taken greedily on terms not yet explained. If no document matches, or more than ROUTE_MAX=4 do, there is no routing. A routed search adds the limitation `corpus_routed:<doc_ids>`. The rule identity (`ROUTE_RULE`, share, max) is recorded in frozen runs, and a run measured under another rule is not served.
