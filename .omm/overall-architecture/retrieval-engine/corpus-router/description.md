Applies only when the scope has more than two pairs, which means the all-documents scope. `route_corpus` narrows to at most `ROUTE_MAX` (4) documents whose frozen title/institution terms (`scope-terms.json` in the index) the question names. A routed document must carry at least `ROUTE_SHARE` of the best match's name weight, and generic project words such as 사업 or 구축 do not route. A route adds `corpus_routed:<doc_ids>` to limitations. A project-name-only question (`name_only`) clears the lexical channel and ranks the named documents by dense similarity (`name_only_question:dense_within_named_documents`).
