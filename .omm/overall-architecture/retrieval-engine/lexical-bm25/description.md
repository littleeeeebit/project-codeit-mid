The K1 channel: Kiwi analysis with letter-spacing collapse and alias normalization (`normalize_for_analysis`). Inside a one- or two-document scope it drops terms present in more than half of the scope's chunks, and drops the project's own name terms when the question restates them (`scope_redundant_terms`). Codes, digits and negations are never dropped. K0 (`whitespace_bm25`) is the plain baseline. `_name_only` detects a question that only names its project. Lexical results are then cleared, and the named documents' rows are ranked by dense meaning alone.
