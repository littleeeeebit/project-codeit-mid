`rank_lexical` runs BM25Okapi over the allowed rows, using Kiwi tokens persisted in the index (K0 builds a whitespace BM25 once per loaded index). It returns only positive scores, with ties broken by chunk ID. Before ranking, `scope_redundant_terms` drops query terms present in more than half of the scope's chunks (when the scope has at least 8 rows) and, for one or two selected documents, the project's restated title terms. Codes, digits and negations are never dropped, and nothing is dropped unless a kept term still matches. A question that only names its documents (`name_only`) clears the lexical list and ranks those documents by meaning in hybrid modes.
