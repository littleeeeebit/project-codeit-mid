Packing walks the final ranking (exact matches first) and admits chunks as EvidenceUnits numbered E1..En. It stops at `evidence_max_units`, uses `evidence_max_tokens` for the first unit and `evidence_target_tokens` after it, and skips overlapping spans (`duplicate_span`) and chunks for another requirement code (`other_requirement_code`). For split elements it pulls up to LINKED_EXTRA_UNITS=2 nearest sibling pieces so that a condition stays with its fact, and otherwise records `linked_evidence_missing`. Every rejected candidate is listed in `excluded` with its reason. In the service, `_with_carried` appends the previous answer's cited units that the fresh retrieval lacks, renumbered after it, and drops any unit whose chunk has left the serving index.
