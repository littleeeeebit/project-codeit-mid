`dense.query_vector` caches by normalized text, model and dimensions. A local model embeds on the GPU for free. An OpenAI miss is embedded only when `allow_paid` and a `request_id` are set, through `metered_embed` in the same ledger under the request's dispatch guard. Gemini uses its own key and cap. `PgDenseIndex.load` verifies the set's ready state, its identity against the source keyword manifest, row order, and the checksum of every vector, and refuses mixed model, dimensions or policy. `search` runs exact cosine SQL, or HNSW with `hnsw_ef_search` when `dense_search == "hnsw"`, over the allowed rows. Hybrid without any lexical or exact match skips dense entirely (`no_lexical_match:dense_not_used`).
