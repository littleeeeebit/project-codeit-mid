`PgDenseIndex.load` verifies an `embedding_sets` row (state `ready`, config and base-manifest hash), the row count and order, and the model, dimensions, policy and extraction of every row, plus each vector checksum, against the keyword index's chunk order. Any mismatch raises `DenseError`, and serving falls back. `search` runs `EXACT_SQL` (cosine over `embedding_set_rows ⋈ embedding_payloads`, restricted to `row_order = ANY(allowed)`, ties broken by chunk ID), or `HNSW_SQL` with transaction-local `hnsw.iterative_scan=strict_order` when the run chose `dense_search: hnsw`.
