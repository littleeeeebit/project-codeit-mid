`retrieval.retrieve` → `_candidates` (RRF / keyword-first fusion) → optional `_rerank` → `_pack_evidence`. Exact identifier matches always stay ahead of every ranker. Evidence units are packed within `evidence_target_tokens` / `evidence_max_tokens` while keeping linked split pieces together. The result carries the ranking, candidates per channel, per-stage timings and limitations. `service.retrieve` traces it as the `retrieve-evidence` span.
