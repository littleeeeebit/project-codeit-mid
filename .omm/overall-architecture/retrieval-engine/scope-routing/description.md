`_routed_scope`: a scope larger than 2 (the All documents corpus from `corpus_scope`) goes through `route_corpus`. It keeps documents whose frozen title/institution terms the question restates (IDF-weighted, at least 2 terms, at most `ROUTE_MAX` documents) and records `corpus_routed:`. Missing extractions add `scope_not_indexed:`, and an unreviewed index adds `index_includes_unreviewed_sources`. `_name_only` detects a question that only names the project; `_named_rows` then ranks that project's rows by meaning (dense) with the limitation `name_only_question:dense_within_named_documents`.
