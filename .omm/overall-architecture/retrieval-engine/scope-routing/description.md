`_routed_scope` narrows the all-documents scope (any scope with more than 2 pairs) to the documents the question names, using `route_corpus`: rule `greedy-rare-term-1`, at most 4 documents, each with at least half the best document's name weight. Generic title words narrow nothing. `scope_rows` maps scope pairs (DocRef plus active extraction) to the allowed chunk rows, and nothing outside them can be scored. For a question that only names the project, `_name_only` and `_named_rows` replace the lexical results with the named documents' rows, ranked by meaning.
