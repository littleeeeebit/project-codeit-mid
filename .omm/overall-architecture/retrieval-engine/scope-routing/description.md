`_routed_scope` handles a scope larger than two documents, which only the all-documents scope can be. `route_corpus` keeps documents whose own title and institution terms the question restates: at least two terms, IDF-weighted, best match first, explaining terms greedily, with `ROUTE_SHARE` and `ROUTE_MAX` bounds. With no match, or too many matches, it does not route. `scope_rows` restricts scoring to rows of the admitted extractions. `_name_only` handles a question that only names the project by serving its overview passages (`_named_rows`).
