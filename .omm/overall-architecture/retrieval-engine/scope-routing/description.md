`_routed_scope` handles all-documents questions only (scope > 2). There, `route_corpus` keeps the documents whose frozen title or institution terms the question restates: at least two terms, IDF-weighted, greedy over terms not yet explained, at most `ROUTE_MAX` = 4 documents. Generic title words alone do not narrow the scope. `_exact_matches` puts rows whose `requirement_key` equals an asked code (`CODE_RE`) first, detail rows first. `_name_only` detects questions that only name the project. For those, lexical results are discarded and dense ranking runs within the named documents (`name_only_question:dense_within_named_documents`). Every decision is appended to `limitations`.
