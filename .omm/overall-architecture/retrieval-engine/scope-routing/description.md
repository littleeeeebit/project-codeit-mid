The scope is one or two selected (DocRef, extraction) pairs, or `corpus_scope`: every indexed active extraction, cited under the first CSV document for that original. `_routed_scope` narrows an all-documents question to the projects it names (`route_corpus`, noted `corpus_routed:`) and notes `index_includes_unreviewed_sources` and `scope_not_indexed:`. `scope_rows` gives the allowed rows; the service checks it before paying for a query embedding.
