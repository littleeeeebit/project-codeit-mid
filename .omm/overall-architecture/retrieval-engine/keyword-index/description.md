`build_keyword_index` chunks every parsed source allowed by the review policy (`auto_verified`, `sample_checked` and `reviewed`, plus `unreviewed` and `auto_flagged` with `--include-unreviewed`) through `chunking.build_profile`. It tokenizes with the Kiwi `Analyzer` and freezes title and institution terms into `scope-terms.json`. It writes `chunks.jsonl`, `tokens.jsonl`, `requirements.jsonl` and `manifest.json` into `.runtime/indexes/<version>` through a temp-directory rename, then inserts the `indexes`, `chunks` and `requirements` rows and optionally sets `app_settings.active_index`. The version is the hash of the config. `KeywordIndex.load` verifies the manifest hash and every listed file hash, checks token and chunk row alignment, and builds `BM25Okapi` and the element map.
