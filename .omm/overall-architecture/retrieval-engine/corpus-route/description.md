Only for all-documents scopes (more than two pairs). `route_corpus` keeps the documents whose own title and institution terms the question restates: at least two terms, IDF-weighted, greedy, each document at least `ROUTE_SHARE` 0.5 of the best match, and at most `ROUTE_MAX` 4 documents. The routing then adds `corpus_routed:` to `limitations`. A question that only names a project (`name_only_question`) drops lexical ranking and ranks the named documents by dense similarity.
