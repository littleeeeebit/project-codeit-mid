`_routed_scope`: when the scope has more than two documents (only the all-documents scope does), `route_corpus` narrows it to the documents whose frozen title or institution terms (`scope-terms.json`) the question names, and records `corpus_routed:`. The step also notes `index_includes_unreviewed_sources` and `scope_not_indexed:*`. `_exact_matches` maps requirement codes found by CODE_RE to rows whose `requirement_key` matches, detail rows first, and records `code_not_found`, `code_detail_unavailable` or `code_ambiguous`. Exact matches stay ahead of every ranker in the final ranking.
