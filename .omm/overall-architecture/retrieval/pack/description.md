`_pack_evidence` walks the ranking and admits chunks as `EvidenceUnit`s E1..En. Each unit carries the doc_id, source_hash, extraction_id, chunk_id, element_ids, the quote (the chunk body), its location and its token count. A chunk is excluded, with its reason recorded, for `other_requirement_code`, `unit_limit` (evidence_max_units), `duplicate_span` or `token_budget`. The first unit may use `evidence_max_tokens`; later units must fit `evidence_target_tokens`. Linked pieces of a split element are added nearest-first, up to LINKED_EXTRA_UNITS; a piece left out is reported as `linked_evidence_missing:<chunk>:<reason>`.
