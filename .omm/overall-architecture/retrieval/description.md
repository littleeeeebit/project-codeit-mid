Entry point is `service.retrieve`. It authorizes the caller, resolves the scope (or the whole corpus via `corpus_scope`), loads `res.index()` and `res.serving()`, and calls `retire_inactive`. A verifier may switch to a free mode. `run_settings(cfg)` makes serving use the activated run's embedding, depths, RRF constant and evidence limits, whatever the process config says. `_narrow_limits` may only lower limits. In a dense mode, the dense index must match the run's model and dimensions, otherwise the stage is marked unavailable and no query vector is paid for. Then `retrieval.retrieve` runs the pipeline shown. The service appends limitations: `index_outdated:*`, `activated_run_stale:corpus_route` and `<stage>_unavailable`. The result (`RetrievalResult`) carries the mode, any fallback, every channel's candidates, the evidence units, the exclusions, per-stage timings in milliseconds and the index and dense versions.
