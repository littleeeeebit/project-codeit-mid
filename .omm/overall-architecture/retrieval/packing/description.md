Packing walks the ranking and admits chunks as EvidenceUnit E1..En. It stops at evidence_max_units, and it uses evidence_max_tokens for the first unit and evidence_target_tokens after that. It skips duplicate spans and out-of-scope rows, then adds linked split siblings up to LINKED_EXTRA_UNITS and reports any it left out as linked_evidence_missing. The result is a RetrievalResult with per-channel candidates, excluded chunks with reasons, ranking, timings_ms and limitations.
