`postgres.open_db(target)` opens psycopg connections and `require_server` refuses any server other than PostgreSQL 18.6. Pooled connections also refuse pgvector other than 0.8.6. `tx(conn, immediate=True)` gives the write transaction every state transition uses. `require_imported_database` refuses startup without a validated `migration_import`/`migration_validation` marker and unchanged artifact hashes. A failed validation or open recovery fence (`recovery_blocked`) closes `paid_admission`.
