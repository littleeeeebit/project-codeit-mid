PostgreSQL 18.6 with pgvector 0.8.6 holds everything: `documents`, `sources`, `extractions`, `elements`, `extraction_inputs`, `indexes`, `chunks`, `requirements`, `requests`, `attempts`, `adjustments`, `budget_settings`, `verifier_runs`, `gold_candidates` and its review log, `activations`, app settings (`store.get_app_setting`: `active_run`, `active_index`), `embedding_payloads/sets/set_rows`, `external_attempts`, `database_control`, `migration_import/validation`, and `application_mutex`. Access goes through `postgres.open_db(target)`, which checks a connection out of the bounded `psycopg_pool` (max 8, 5 s timeout) that `lifecycle(target)` created. A query never implicitly creates a pool. `bind_sql` translates qmark SQL and refuses SQLite dialect (`PRAGMA`, `INSERT OR`, `REPLACE INTO`). Each connection sets `lock_timeout 5s` and `statement_timeout 60s`. Large artifacts (extraction JSONL, index files, runs, compare tables, drafts) live under `RFP_DATA_DIR` (`.runtime/`); the database records their paths and hashes, and `host_path` remaps those paths through `RFP_PATH_MAP` after a cross-host restore.
