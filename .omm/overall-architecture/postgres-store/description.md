postgres.py wraps a psycopg pool per Target (`lifecycle` / `open_db`). `require_server` refuses anything other than PostgreSQL 18.6 and pgvector 0.8.6. `tx(conn, immediate=True)` serializes every writing transaction by taking `SELECT ... FROM application_mutex WHERE id=1 FOR UPDATE`. The schema (store.SCHEMA version 6 plus vector_store.SCHEMA and models.EXTERNAL_SCHEMA) covers sources, documents, extractions, elements, indexes, chunks, requirements, reviews, fidelity_checks, app_settings (active_index, active_run), requests, attempts, adjustments, budget_settings, gold_candidates, gold_reviews, audit_events, corrections, verifier_runs, eval_estimates, activations, embedding_payloads, embedding_sets and embedding_set_rows. `require_imported_database` blocks startup unless the `migration_import` and `migration_validation` markers are complete and every referenced artifact file (originals, extractions, index manifests and their files) still hashes as recorded. `host_path` maps recorded Windows paths onto Linux through RFP_PATH_MAP.
