`drafting._generate` has gpt-6-luna draft source-bound questions for the chosen slots. Each call is reserved and settled in `gold_eval` within the consented maximum. Output goes to `<drafts>/<run_id>/output/` (`candidates.jsonl`, `invalid.json`, `receipt.json`). `submit_drafts` calls `evaluation.assign_families`, then `gold.submit(..., 'dev', drafting.DRAFTER)` as one batch. Agents can also submit JSONL through `cli gold submit` (procedure in `.wiki/gold-drafting.md`).
