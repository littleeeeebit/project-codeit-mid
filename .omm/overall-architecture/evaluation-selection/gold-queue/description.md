`gold.decide` writes approved rows to the dataset file (`.runtime/datasets/dev.jsonl`) and rejected rows to the rejection wiki. Both are rendered from the database inside the deciding transaction and guarded by `expected_sha`. A note is required. `service.gold_decide` refuses approval by whoever started the drafting run. Sealed `test` candidates are refused on the web; they are reviewed only through the owner's CLI. `gold.second_review` resolves disputed rows. `validate-gold` rejects unreviewed or self-approved rows, missing quotes and cross-split families.
