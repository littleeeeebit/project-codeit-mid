Everything reaches the database through `store.open_db(settings.db_path)` and `store.tx(conn, immediate)`. `Settings.db_path` returns a `postgres.Target` (DSN taken from `RFP_DATABASE_DSN` in the environment) when `database_backend='postgresql'`, and `<data>/rfp.sqlite3` otherwise. Bulk artifacts live under `RFP_DATA_DIR` (`.runtime`) and are referenced from the database by path and hash.
