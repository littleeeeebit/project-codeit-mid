The paid gateway is the only path to the provider. Callers inside a request use `service._metered_chat`. Embedding builds, OCR fallback, drafting, judges and stage-review workers use `budget.reserve` and `settle` directly, or through their own wrappers. Every call reserves its worst-case cost before dispatch (with every input token priced at the cache-write rate, per the README) and settles exactly once from the reported usage. An outcome that is not known stays `unknown` until the owner reconciles it.
