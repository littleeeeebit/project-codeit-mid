postgres.GatewayOwner holds pg_try_advisory_lock(GATEWAY_LOCK) on its own autocommit connection. borrow_owner lets several Resources in one process share it, counting users. check() confirms the lock is still granted and that recovery and validation are ready. OpenAITransport._check_owner calls it before every provider call and turns a failure into ProviderError(pre_execution=True). owner_guard runs inside reserve and mark_dispatching. Once ownership is lost the owner never reconnects.
