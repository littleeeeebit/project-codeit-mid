`service._metered_chat` is used by `_rewrite` (stage `query_rewrite`, at most 1,500 output tokens) and by `_generate` (stage `generation`, `generation_max_output_tokens`, streaming into `res.partials`). The steps are: `budget.reserve`. Then `res.paid_refusal()`: with no key, the reservation is released as `provider_unavailable`. Then `budget.mark_dispatching` with `_dispatch_guard`. Then `_call_and_settle`. The outcomes map as follows. A refused admission becomes `budget_blocked`, or `clarification_required` for `above_consented_maximum`. A guard that reports cancellation or interruption raises `_Stop`. A ledger refusal becomes `budget_blocked`. A provider error before execution is released; any other provider error or transport exception is marked unknown; a missing usage report is marked unknown; a settlement failure is marked unknown and the request fails with `technical_error`.
