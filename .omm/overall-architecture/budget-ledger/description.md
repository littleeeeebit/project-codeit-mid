src/rfp_assistant/budget.py is the only spending authority. `reserve` atomically admits an attempt at its maximum cost: every input token priced at the cache-write rate, plus the maximum output. It refuses when paid generation is off (`paid_enabled`), when PostgreSQL paid admission is closed, when the ledger is frozen, for an unknown rate or purpose, above a consented ceiling, or when the cap or the purpose envelope (interactive, embedding, gold_eval, judge_eval) would be exceeded. Pending reservations count against the cap. Reconciliation, adjustments, paid on/off, envelopes and the shared cap are owner operations (CLI, plus `set_limit` from 설정). See state-transitions/attempt-record for the attempt lifecycle.
