`budget.py` keeps one shared ledger in the `budget_settings` row (cap, allowance, purpose envelopes, rates, paid flag, `frozen_reason`, history), the `attempts`, `adjustments` and `audit_events` tables, and `ledger_revision`.

`reserve` checks the following in one immediate transaction: PostgreSQL ownership (`owner_guard`), PostgreSQL `database_control.paid_admission`, `paid_enabled`, `frozen_reason`, a known rate, a known purpose, the consented ceiling, cap headroom (cap − settled − adjustments − open reservations) and envelope headroom. It inserts a `reserved` attempt priced by `max_cost`, which bills every input token at the cache-write rate plus the full output allowance. Envelopes split the cap 1:3:12 across `embedding`, `gold_eval` and `interactive`. Operational database errors fail closed as `ledger_unavailable`.

`settle` is exactly-once. A settled cost above its reservation sets `frozen_reason`, which stops all new paid work until the owner inspects it. Owner commands (`settle`, `reconcile`, `adjust`, `paid`, `set-limit`, `set-envelopes`, `register-embedding-rate`) are append-only and keyed for idempotency. `snapshot` drives `GET /api/budget` with pacing and cap warnings (`visible_warnings` shows only the highest cap level).
