`budget.py` is one shared ledger in the `attempts`, `adjustments` and `budget_settings` tables. `reserve()` is atomic admission inside `tx(immediate=True)`. It checks the gateway owner (`require_owner`, `owner_guard`), `paid_enabled`, `database_control.paid_admission`, `frozen_reason`, a known rate, the purpose envelope, the consented `ceiling_micro_usd` and the cap (spent + pending). It then inserts a `reserved` attempt priced at `max_cost`, with every input token at the cache-write rate. `mark_dispatching()` repeats the stop guard and ownership check in the same transaction and moves the attempt to `dispatching`, or releases it. `settle()` runs exactly once from reported usage; a settlement above the reservation freezes the ledger. `mark_unknown` keeps a timeout as pending cost. `reconcile`, `add_adjustment`, `settle_from_evidence` and `set_paid_enabled` are owner CLI actions with an audit event. `recover()` on a fresh owner turns `dispatching` into `unknown` and `reserved` into `released`, including in the Gemini `external_attempts` ledger. Every write bumps `app_settings.ledger_revision`.
