`run generation` and `run ocr` let each candidate price its own work without a key and stop at `estimate.json` in state `needs_approval`. `approve` records the person in the run and the audit log, refusing expired or over-budget estimates. `resume` re-checks inputs, worker, rates, refs and the remaining budget, then hands each candidate `OPENAI_API_KEY`. Idempotency keys are `<run-id>:<candidate>:<question>:<n>`, and the ledger names every billed item, so a crashed pass never pays twice. `reprice` re-estimates what is left at current rates.
