gold.py is the review queue (`gold_candidates`, `gold_reviews`, the rejection wiki, `datasets/dev.jsonl`). drafting.py drafts source-bound questions with gpt-6-luna under the `gold_eval` envelope. evaluation.py validates gold-2 rows and runs frozen retrieval runs. answers.py runs phase-4 answer evaluation of at most two finalists through the service's own answer path. sealed.py freezes a release candidate and runs the sealed test once. release.py and postgres_backup.py handle backup, staged `restore-check` and `release-report` (ready, limited or blocked). judges.py compares a Luna judge with a Jev judge on mutated held-out sets.
