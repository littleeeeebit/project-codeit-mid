`answers.plan_run` prices every remaining attempt into `eval_estimates`. `run_answers` answers each development row through `service.answer` on a `PinnedResources`, which serves the recorded run's retrieval configuration, charges `gold_eval`, and borrows the owner's transport. Finished rows and rows with unknown billing are never re-sent. Scoring keeps denominators. `embedding-comparison` adds McNemar and Holm statistics into `answer-embedding` tables.
