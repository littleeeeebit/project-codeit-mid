`gold.submit` loads a batch into `gold_candidates` (pending). `gold.decide` runs in one transaction. The first decision wins; `expected_sha` must equal the stored row; the drafter, the drafting model and (in the service) whoever started the run cannot approve; a gold-2 approval needs `original_inspected`; a rejection needs categories. It writes `gold_reviews`, then rewrites the dataset JSONL (approve) or the rejection wiki page (reject). `second_review` handles disputed rows. Sealed rows are refused outside the owner CLI.
