These modules decide what serves and how good it is. Gold datasets are drafted by gpt-6-luna and approved by people. Frozen retrieval runs and axis-matrix comparison tables come next. A person activates a row. Paid answer runs, the Luna and Jev judge comparison, and a sealed run then feed a release report (`ready`, `limited` or `blocked`). The internals of `compare.py`, `judges.py`, `sealed.py` and `release.py` are summarized from their entry points and the README, not traced line by line.
