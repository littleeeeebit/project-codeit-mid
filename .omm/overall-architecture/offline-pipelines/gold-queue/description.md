Drafted questions enter `gold_candidates` as pending through `gold.submit`, from the CLI `gold submit` or from `submit_drafts` on a finished drafting run. `gold.decide` approves a row into `datasets/<dataset>.jsonl` or rejects it into the rejection wiki. It re-renders the dataset file and the wiki pages inside the deciding transaction. `second_review` handles disputed approved rows. Sealed `test` candidates are reviewed only through the owner CLI (`gold show/decide/second-review`).
