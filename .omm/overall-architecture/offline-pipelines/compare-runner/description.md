`compare.run_matrix` and the matrix `run` expand one of `MATRICES` (lexical, chunking, embedding, reranker) into rows. Each row varies one axis and keeps the serving run's other limits. Rows are scored on three populations (dev on its own documents, dev over the whole corpus, needles over the whole corpus) and written as activatable runs under `.runtime/runs/`, plus `.runtime/compare/tables/<matrix>.json|md`. Cells are cached. A paid corpus build stops as `needs_approval` until `compare --approve`.
