`record_review` and `import_reviews` append human reviews (`reviews` rows: reviewer, status, element locations, findings) bound to the active extraction. `confirm_fidelity` (route `/api/verify/fidelity/{hash}/confirm`) records `sample_checked` after a person has checked every reported place. `recover_source` registers an approved PDF conversion of a quarantined original as a new, unreviewed revision. `resolve-metadata` records canonical values, which filters, ranking and the next `build-keyword` use.
