`ocr.run` (CLI `ocr`, under a file `ProcessLock`) reads HWP embedded pictures (WMF and EMF rendered to bitmaps) and PDF raster images with PaddleOCR-VL on CUDA (`LocalOCR`). `fallback_reasons` (looping, low confidence, length far from what the ink suggests) flag regions. `RemoteReader` re-reads only the flagged ones with gpt-5-mini through `budget.reserve` in the `ocr` envelope (member `ocr`, stage `ocr:<region>`). `--ledger-dsn-env` lets it use the shared ledger over the tunnel. Results are cached per region under `.runtime/ocr/<hash>/`, so reruns resume. The next `ingest` merges them, and fidelity skips OCR text.
