`assign_revision` binds the extraction ID to the actual output: identical output keeps its revision and its review status, while different output under the same fingerprint gets a content-hashed revision beside the old one. Old artifacts and element rows stay for pinned gold rows and issued citations. The `elements.jsonl` artifact under `.runtime/extracted/<hash>/<fp>/` and the `elements` rows are written; a new revision resets `review_status` to `unreviewed`. `diagnose` flags `short_output`, `no_tables`, `thin_tail`, `blank_pages` and others, but never marks a source reviewed.
