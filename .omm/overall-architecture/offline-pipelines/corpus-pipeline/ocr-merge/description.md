`ocr.merge(settings, source_hash, raw, rendering)` inserts cached OCR text (PaddleOCR-VL on the GPU, with gemini-3.5-flash-lite only for flagged regions under the $0.50 cap in `.runtime/ocr/gemini-ledger.jsonl`) as `image_text` elements next to the surrounding print text and suffixes the parser fingerprint. These elements are chunked apart, cited as OCR, and skipped by the fidelity check.
