`ingestion.ingest` processes every unique original once. In `ingest_source`, the original's hash must match its recorded value, and an unchanged `input_key` (original, parser revision, native print, OCR cache, converter) reuses the recorded outcome. Otherwise `_parse_source` runs `parse_hwp` (pyhwp `hwp5proc`, with the Hancom print as a fallback) or `parse_pdf` (PyMuPDF) and merges OCR text. A parse failure sets the source to `parse_status='quarantined'` and `review_status='needs_recovery'`. A successful parse gets a revision from `assign_revision`, which is bound to the actual output. Different output under the same fingerprint gets a new content-addressed extraction ID, and the old artifact stays for pinned citations and gold rows. A new revision resets review to `unreviewed`. Element rows go to `elements`, and the artifact goes to `.runtime/extracted/<hash>/<fp>/elements.jsonl`.
