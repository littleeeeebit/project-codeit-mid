`fidelity.verify_source` compares the pyhwp extraction against Hancom Viewer's printed PDF in both directions: 6-character shingles, ruled-table cells, and exact matches for runs of 3+ digits. It records `auto_verified` or `auto_flagged` with element, cell and page findings in `fidelity_checks`. On 검증 a person can confirm with `confirm_fidelity`, which writes `sample_checked`. An automatic verdict never overwrites a human review.
