`fidelity run` has Hancom Viewer print each HWP to PDF and compares the two text layers with 6-character shingles and exact digit runs. The verdict is `auto_verified` or `auto_flagged`, and a human status (`sample_checked`) is never overwritten. `ocr` (corpus/ocr.py) reads image regions with PaddleOCR-VL on the GPU. It sends only regions that fail the fallback test to Gemini, under that model's own $0.50 cap and its `external_attempts` ledger. The next ingest merges the results as `image_text` elements.
