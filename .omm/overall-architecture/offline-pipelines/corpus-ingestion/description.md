`import_manifest` reads `data_list.csv` and hashes the originals into `sources`/`documents`, recording duplicates and conflicts. `ingest_source` reparses a source only when its `input_key` changed (original, parser revision, native print, OCR cache, converter) or with `--force`. HWP goes through `hwp5proc` XML (`walk_hwp`), falling back to the Hancom print's text layer. PDF goes through PyMuPDF. OCR regions are merged as `image_text` elements. A failure sets `parse_status = 'quarantined'`, `review_status = 'needs_recovery'` with a reason. Success writes an output-bound extraction revision with its elements. A new revision resets review to `unreviewed`, and the old artifact stays for pinned gold rows and issued citations.
