`fidelity run` has Hancom Viewer print each HWP to PDF as an independent witness. It compares both directions with 6-character shingles and exact runs of 3+ digits, and sets `auto_verified` or `auto_flagged` without overwriting a human status. Verifiers confirm reported spots on the 원문 대조 tab (`confirm_fidelity`). src/rfp_assistant/ocr.py reads raster regions with PaddleOCR-VL on the GPU. It re-reads only flagged regions with Gemini under its own $0.50 cap, and caches per region.
