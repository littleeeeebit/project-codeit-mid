`evaluation.evaluate_retrieval` writes frozen retrieval-only runs (K0, K1, D, H, HR) to `.runtime/runs/<run_id>/`. A run is bound to the dataset hash, the evaluated population, the index manifest, the analyzer, the dense version and the limits. `compare.py` runs the declared axis matrices (`MATRICES`: lexical, chunking, embedding, reranker) over three populations. It caches cells, stops paid rows at a priced estimate until `compare --approve`, and writes `.runtime/compare/tables/<matrix>.json` and `.md`, which 검증 → 실험 비교 displays. `answers.py` and `sealed.py` handle paid answer evaluation, the freeze-release step and the single sealed run. `release.write_release_report` decides ready, limited or blocked.
