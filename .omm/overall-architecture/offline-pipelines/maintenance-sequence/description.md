`maintenance.run` executes STEPS in order: backup, restore_check (into a scratch database), ingest, fidelity, keyword, embedding, regression and report. Each step reuses unchanged inputs, and a paid embedding stops at its estimate. A failed step stops the sequence and names itself, and a reparse that cost the rebuilt rows any question ends the run `unverified`. It activates nothing. It starts from the CLI `maintain` or from POST /api/verify/maintenance/start (one per process), and GET /api/verify/maintenance reports its state file.
