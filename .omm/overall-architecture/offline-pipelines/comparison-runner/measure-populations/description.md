`measure` runs retrieval for each population and scores it against source spans (hit@k, recall, complete coverage, nDCG@5, MRR, packed coverage, critical, code and scope failures, Wilson intervals). `write_dev_run` writes an activatable run under `.runtime/runs/<run_id>/` (`config.json`, `traces.jsonl`, `scores.json`, `report.md`). HR rows also measure warm latency alone and under `LOAD_USERS` concurrent questions, and record a gate (nDCG@5 +0.03, no new critical failure, at most +1 s p95) as a column, not a refusal. A load failure, OOM or crash becomes a failed row with its reason after `free_gpu()`.
