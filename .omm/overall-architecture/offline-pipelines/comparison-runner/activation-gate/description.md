`evaluation.activate_decision` (called by the CLI `activate-run` and the 검증 activate button through `service.activate_experiment`) runs `decision_errors` -> `run_errors`. The checks: complete scores, the current `EVAL_VERSION`, the current corpus-route rule, an unchanged evaluated population, a recorded reranker concurrency bound for HR, a verified keyword index manifest and compatibility, a loadable dense set, a non-OpenAI embedding matching its pinned revision and prefixes, and a non-empty `decided_by`. It then sets `active_run` and `active_index` and appends to `activations` (with the previous configuration) in one transaction.
