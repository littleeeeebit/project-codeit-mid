`ensure_dense` and `paid_estimate` stop an uncached OpenAI or Gemini corpus or query build as a `needs_approval` row with a priced estimate under `.runtime/compare/estimates/`. `compare --approve <id> --approved-by <name>` (`approve_estimate`) records the approval; only an approval of the estimate covering exactly the missing questions lets them be sent. OpenAI spending uses the shared ledger, and Gemini uses `compare-cap` and `external_attempts`.
