Members sign in with their JupyterHub account through OAuth (owner decision 2026-10-06, replacing the earlier no-login decision). `api.py` gates every `/api` route on a session for an allowlisted hub username, and `auth.visitor(name)` gives that member every capability; the name is what requests, reviews and audit rows record. The capability checks (`_authorize`) stay so each public function states its role, and CLI/tests can still pass narrower principals.
