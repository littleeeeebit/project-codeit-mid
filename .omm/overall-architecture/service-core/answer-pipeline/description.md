`_execute` opens one Langfuse trace, seeded by the request ID, and runs `_execute_traced`. The free modes `metadata` and `inventory` finish inline from CSV facts and the requirement inventory. The paid modes `single`, `compare` and `corpus` go through `_paid_answer`, which resolves the scope, rewrites a follow-up, retrieves and prices, generates, validates and finishes (details under request-lifecycle). Each exit path goes through `done()`, then `_finish`, which writes the result, the trace and the billing state rolled up from the ledger. Exceptions map to statuses: `_Stop` becomes cancelled or interrupted, `ServiceError` becomes `clarification_required`, and anything else becomes `technical_error`.
