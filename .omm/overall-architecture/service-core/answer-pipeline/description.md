From `submit_answer` to `_finish`, which `request-lifecycle` details. Free modes `metadata` (`_metadata_answer`) and `inventory` (`_inventory_answer`, `requirement_inventory`) run inline without the executor. Paid modes `single`, `compare` (balanced two-document retrieval via `_prepare_compare`) and `corpus` (all documents) go through `_paid_answer`. A frozen verifier run (`_frozen_run_for`) reuses exactly the evidence of a recorded trace and may not reserve above its consented estimate (`ceiling`).
