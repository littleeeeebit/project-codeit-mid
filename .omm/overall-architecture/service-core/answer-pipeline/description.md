`_execute` opens a Langfuse run seeded by request_id and calls `_execute_traced`. That dispatches free modes (`metadata` → `_metadata_answer`, `inventory` → `_inventory_answer`) inline, and paid modes (`single`, `compare`, `corpus`) to `_paid_answer`. Every exit goes through `done` → `_finish`, which derives the overall billing state from the ledger (`_billing`, precedence unknown > pending > reconciled > settled > released), keeps an already cancelled or interrupted status, stores trace and result JSON, and drops the partial. `_execute_traced` maps `_Stop` to cancelled/interrupted, `ServiceError` to `clarification_required` and failed, and any other exception to `technical_error` and failed. The full sequence is under `request-lifecycle`.
