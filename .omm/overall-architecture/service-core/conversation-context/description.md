`_conversation` follows the `previous_request_id` chain back for up to `CONVERSATION_TURNS = 6` turns. Every turn must belong to the same member, be terminal, and have the identical `scope_json`; otherwise the request is refused before it is queued. Each turn contributes `{question: standalone_question or original, answer: _answer_text(result)}`, truncated to 6000 characters for the latest turn and 1200 for older ones. The latest turn's cited `EvidenceUnit`s (up to `CARRIED_EVIDENCE = 8`) are carried forward. `_with_carried` appends them after the fresh retrieval, renumbered `E{n+1}...`, and drops any unit whose chunk has left the serving index (its quote could no longer be checked). A verifier-run request cannot continue a conversation.
