`_conversation` walks `previous_request_id` back through up to 6 turns. It refuses the request before anything is queued if an earlier turn belongs to another member, is not yet terminal, or used a different scope. From each turn it builds `{question, answer}` with `_answer_text`, allowing 6000 characters for the latest answer and 1200 for older ones, and carries up to 8 evidence units the previous answer cited. `_rewrite` makes a paid `query_rewrite` call through `_metered_chat`, with `REWRITE_PROMPT_VERSION` and at most 1500 output tokens. Its validated standalone question replaces the user's question for retrieval and generation and is stored as `standalone_question`. `_with_carried` appends the carried units after fresh retrieval, renumbers them, and drops any whose chunk left the serving index.
