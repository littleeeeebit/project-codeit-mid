`verifier_trace` freezes a retrieval-only run (question, scope, as-of date, mode, effective limits, evidence and estimate) into `verifier_runs`. `run_generation_block` explains why a paid answer cannot be generated now: an unknown rate, or serving changed since the freeze. `generate_from_run` submits an `AnswerRequest` with `verifier_run_id`. `_frozen_run_for` and `_frozen_prep` then reuse exactly the frozen evidence, with no new retrieval or query embedding, and `_metered_chat` passes `ceiling=frozen estimate`, so a reservation above the consented maximum is refused with `above_consented_maximum`.
