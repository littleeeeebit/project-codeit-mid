`generation.validate_answer(response, evidence, allowed_doc_ids, stored_quotes, required_doc_ids=...)` checks the strict-JSON payload against the evidence units actually supplied. Unknown evidence IDs, scope mismatches, quote mismatches against `_stored_quote` (re-read from the stored extraction), and claims, conflicts or summaries without evidence raise `TechnicalError`. In compare mode, every represented document must be answered. `_validated` records `citation_valid` 1.0/0.0 as a Langfuse score. `partial_answer` parses the unvalidated streamed JSON for the SSE route. The full validator body was not read.
