`service.bind_request(res, member_id)` reads `res.member_keys[member]` and `res.key_sources[session]` and sets three contextvars: the key session, the model and the billing scope. `RequestRunner.submit` wraps each job in `contextvars.copy_context().run`, so a background answer keeps the key and model the member had when they asked, even if they change them later. `Resources.set_api_key` keeps a key only in process memory, inside the transport's per-session client. A restart therefore needs every member to enter their key again.
