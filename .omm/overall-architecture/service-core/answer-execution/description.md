`_execute` opens one Langfuse trace (`tracing.run`, seeded by `request_id`, tagged with the mode) around `_execute_traced`. That dispatches metadata (`_metadata_answer`, typed CSV facts) and inventory (`_inventory_answer`, the requirement list from the index) for free, and everything else through `_paid_answer`. Every exit, including `_Stop` (cancelled or interrupted), `ServiceError` (clarification_required) and unexpected exceptions (technical_error), goes through `done` → `_finish`. `_finish` rolls the request's attempts up into `billing_state` (precedence unknown > pending > reconciled > settled > released), keeps a status a shutdown or cancel already set, writes `trace_json` and `result_json`, and drops the streamed partial.
