`_execute` opens one Langfuse trace per request (`tracing.run`, seeded by `request_id`) and routes by mode. `metadata` and `inventory` are free and deterministic (`_metadata_answer`, `_inventory_answer`). `single`, `compare` and `corpus` go to `_paid_answer`. Exceptions map to outcomes: `_Stop` gives cancelled or interrupted, `ServiceError` gives `clarification_required`/failed, and anything else gives `technical_error`/failed. Every exit goes through `_finish`, so a request never stays `running`. The full step sequence is under `request-lifecycle`.
