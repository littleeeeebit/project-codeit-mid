`_execute` opens one Langfuse trace (`tracing.run('answer-question', seed=request_id)`). `_execute_traced` dispatches to `_metadata_answer`, `_inventory_answer` or `_paid_answer`, and maps exceptions to outcomes: `_Stop` → cancelled or interrupted, `ServiceError` → `clarification_required`/failed, anything else → `technical_error`/failed. Every exit goes through `done` → `_finish`. That derives `billing_state` from the request's attempts (unknown > pending > reconciled > settled > released), stores `trace_json` and `result_json`, and keeps a status already set to interrupted or cancelled. It maps completed + `cancel_requested` to cancelled and pops `res.partials[request_id]`. See request-lifecycle for the paid stages.
