Each long job runs on a single daemon thread started under `res._runner_lock` after the update fence and closing checks pass, and is appended to `res._jobs`. `start_drafting` runs `drafting._generate` with gpt-6-luna against the `gold_eval` envelope, capped at the consented maximum. Its output goes to `.runtime/.../drafts/<run>/output`, and `submit_drafts` hands the valid drafts to `gold.submit` for the review queue. `start_answer_evaluation` and `start_judges` run evaluations from priced estimates. `start_maintenance` runs `storage/maintenance.run`: backup, restore check, ingest, fidelity, keyword build, embedding, regression and report. That sequence stops at a paid embedding estimate and never activates anything. Each job passes a `guard` / `closing` callback so a closing service stops before its next dispatch.
