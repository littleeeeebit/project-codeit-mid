Observed: maintenance and drafting are not covered by `_refuse_closed_or_busy`, so maintenance, drafting and one evaluation or judge run can execute at the same time on the same transport and ledger. Each still reserves through the ledger, so the cap holds. Separately, `maintenance.step_restore_check` sets and restores `os.environ['RFP_RESTORE_DATABASE_DSN']` from its thread, which is process-global state. I found no other reader that would race with it, but it was not exercised.
