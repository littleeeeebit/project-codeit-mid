`run_queued` claims `queued -> running` atomically (`AND cancel_requested = 0`), rebuilds the `AnswerRequest` and `Principal` from `request_json`, and calls `_execute`. `_execute` opens one Langfuse trace with `tracing.run(..., seed=request_id)` and calls `_execute_traced`. That dispatches metadata to `_metadata_answer`, inventory to `_inventory_answer` and everything else to `_paid_answer`. Exceptions map to outcomes: `_Stop` gives cancelled or interrupted, `ServiceError` gives `clarification_required` with a failed request, and any other exception gives `technical_error`. Every exit goes through `_finish`. It aggregates attempt states into `billing_state` (unknown ranks above pending, which ranks above settled), keeps an already interrupted or cancelled status, turns a cancel-requested completion into cancelled, stores `trace_json` and `result_json`, and drops `res.partials[request_id]`.
