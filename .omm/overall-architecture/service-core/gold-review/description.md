`submit_drafts` sends a finished drafting run's `candidates.jsonl` to `gold.submit` as one batch drafted by `drafting.DRAFTER` (after `evaluation.assign_families`). `gold_decide` requires a note for both outcomes, and it refuses approval by the member who requested the drafting run (`_drafting_meta(...).requested_by`). It then calls `gold.decide`, which, according to the gold.py docstring and its `write_jsonl_atomic`/`write_text_atomic` calls, re-renders the dataset JSONL and the rejection-wiki pages from the database inside the deciding transaction. Sealed `test` candidates are reviewed only through the owner CLI.
