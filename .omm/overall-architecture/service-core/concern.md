Any signed-in member holds the `verifier` capability. As a result, `request_status`, `get_request`/`open_evidence` and `list_requests(all_members=True)` let every allowed member read other members' requests by ID, and `set_budget_limit` lets any member change the shared cap. `cancel_request` remains owner-only. This matches the README's 'every allowed member gets all pages', but it means the owner/verifier checks are effectively 'signed in'.
