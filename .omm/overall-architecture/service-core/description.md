src/rfp_assistant/service/service.py is the authenticated entry point for every screen and CLI behaviour. `_authorize` checks capabilities; every signed-in member is a `visitor` holding all of them. The module owns the process-wide `Resources`, the paid request pipeline, and the background jobs: drafting, answer evaluation (service/answers.py), judges (evaluation/judges.py) and maintenance. Paid admissions of every kind are serialized against shutdown and the update fence through `Resources._runner_lock`.
