`src/rfp_assistant/service/service.py` (about 3,100 lines) is the single behavioural layer behind both entry points. It holds the process-wide `Resources` owner, the bounded `RequestRunner`, the ask and answer pipeline, free answers, verifier traces, and the starters for paid background jobs. Helpers sit beside it: `answers.py` (answer runs), `drafting.py`, `ops.py` (fake-provider load check), `auth.py` and `update.py`. Every paid admission takes `Resources._runner_lock` and checks the update fence.
