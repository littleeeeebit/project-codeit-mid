`_conversation` walks `previous_request_id` back through at most 6 turns. Every earlier turn must belong to the same member, be finished, and have the identical scope; otherwise the call is refused before queuing. It renders each earlier answer as the screen showed it (`_answer_text`, 6000 characters for the latest turn and 1200 for older ones). It carries up to 8 cited evidence units from the previous answer. For a follow-up, `_rewrite` makes a paid `query_rewrite` call through `_metered_chat` with `REWRITE_PROMPT` and validates one standalone question. Retrieval and the answer then use that question; it is recorded as `standalone_question`. `_with_carried` appends the carried units, renumbered, when the fresh retrieval lacks them.
