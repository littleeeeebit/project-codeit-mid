`_paid_answer` answers with `ingestion_unavailable` when no selected document is parsed and indexed. Otherwise it continues in order. A `_checkpoint` runs first. For a follow-up, `_rewrite` makes a paid `query_rewrite` call that turns the conversation into a standalone question (`generation.validate_rewrite`), followed by another checkpoint. `_prepare_paid` then does retrieval and pricing: `prepare_answer` for single and corpus, `_prepare_compare` for one subquery per document, or `_frozen_prep` for a verifier run. A frozen run whose new estimate exceeds its consented estimate is refused. No evidence gives `insufficient_evidence` with no paid generation. Otherwise a final checkpoint runs, then `_generate` calls `_metered_chat` with stage generation and streams into `res.partials`. `_validated` runs `generation.validate_answer` against the evidence IDs, the allowed documents and the stored chunk quotes (`_stored_quote`), and scores `citation_valid` in the trace. A validation failure is `technical_error`; the call is billed and never retried.
