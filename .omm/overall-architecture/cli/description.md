`cli.py` (python -m rfp_assistant.cli) shares the service and pipeline modules with the server. Its command groups: corpus (`init`, `manifest`, `ingest`, `import-reviews`, `recover-source`, `identity`, `resolve-metadata`, `fidelity`, `ocr`, `review`); index and vectors (`build-keyword`, `plan-embeddings`, `build-dense`); selection (`evaluate-retrieval`, `trial-reranker`, `compare`, `compare-runs`, `draft-activation`, `activate-run`); phase 4 (`validate-gold`, `freeze-dataset`, `plan-run`, `run-answers`, `latency-run`, `export-review` and `import-review`, `freeze-release`, `backup`, `restore-check`, `release-report`); budget, owner only with `--actor` and a reason (`configure-budget`, `budget-status`, `unresolved`, `settle`, `reconcile`, `adjust`, `paid`, `audit`, `set-envelopes`, `set-limit`); gold (`gold submit/infer/check/repin/decide`); operations (`maintain`, `check`, `load-check`); and `stage-review run/approve/resume/rescore/reprice`.
