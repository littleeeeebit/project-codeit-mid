`TurnView` polls `GET /api/requests/{id}?generation_id&target` every 1000 ms through `usePoll`, until the status leaves queued/running. `usePoll` drops late results when its key changes. When `attachable` is false (cancelled, or the generation or target no longer matches), the turn is 'revoked': streaming stops and the outcome is shown only as history. A finished answer renders through `AnswerBody` only when `attachable` is true. Citation markers are numbered in cited order, and the latest turn opens its first citation in the pane. The 요청 취소 button posts `/cancel`, then reloads the poll.
