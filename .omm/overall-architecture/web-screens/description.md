Next.js app in `web/src`, built as a static export that `api.create_app` mounts at `/` after every `/api/*` route. In development, `npm run dev` on port 8510 forwards `/api/*` to `RFP_API_URL` (default 127.0.0.1:8511). Every call goes through the `openapi-fetch` client in `web/src/lib/api.ts`, typed from `web/src/lib/api-schema.d.ts`. That schema is generated from the FastAPI OpenAPI by `tools/openapi.py`, and `tests/test_api.py` fails while it is stale. The pages are 질문하기 (`/`), 검증 (`/verify`), 데이터셋 만들기 (`/dataset`) and Settings (`/settings`). The screens hold no business state: they poll read-only status routes and render what the service returns.
