A Next.js static export in web/ that the API serves itself. Pages: 질문하기 (web/src/app/page.tsx → components/ask/ask-page.tsx), 검증 (app/verify → components/verify/*), 데이터셋 만들기 (app/dataset → components/dataset/*) and 설정 (app/settings/page.tsx). Every call goes through the openapi-fetch client in web/src/lib/api.ts, which is typed from web/openapi.json, generated by tools/openapi.py. That client has two middlewares. `expired` redirects any 401 outside `/api/auth/` to `/api/auth/login`. `sameAccount` attaches `X-BidMate-Account` (set by `bindAccount` after `GET /api/auth/me` in components/sign-in.tsx) and reloads the page on a 409 that carries `X-BidMate-Account-Changed`. Screens hold no business rules. They poll read-only endpoints through `usePoll` (web/src/lib/use-poll.ts), which drops late results when its key changes, and the Ask page reads the SSE answer stream through `useAnswerStream`.
