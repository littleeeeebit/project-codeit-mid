`UpdateBanner` polls `GET /api/update/status` every 15 s. It renders only when the watch is `configured` and either main is ahead, an update is in progress, or a failed result is less than 24 h old. The 업데이트 button is disabled while `paid_work.open`. The confirm dialog warns that the restart signs everyone out and wipes the API keys entered on 설정. After `POST /api/update` succeeds, `useUpdateWait` polls the status with raw `fetch` every 2 s. It reloads the page on a 401 or 409 (new process), on recovery after a failure, or on `succeeded`. It shows the outcome for `up_to_date`, `failed`, `rolled_back`, `rollback_failed` or `interrupted`, and gives up after 15 minutes.
