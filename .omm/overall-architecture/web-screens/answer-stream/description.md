`useAnswerStream` opens `GET /api/requests/{id}/stream?generation_id=` with `fetch`, so the stream can be aborted, and parses the SSE frames by hand. A `data:` frame replaces the provisional text (summary and claims parsed from partial JSON). `data: null` withdraws it after a cancellation or a lost generation. `event: done` calls `onDone`, and the turn then reloads its status to get the validated outcome. Free modes and revoked turns never open a stream. Stream errors are swallowed because the status poll still delivers the outcome.
