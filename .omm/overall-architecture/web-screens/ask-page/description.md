`AskPage` holds the conversation as `turns`. Each turn keeps its ownership record `{request_id, generation_id, target, member}` from `POST /api/ask`, whose body carries `scope`, `question`, `mode` and `previous_request_id` (the last turn). Each `TurnView` polls `GET /api/requests/{id}?generation_id&target` every 1000 ms until the status leaves `queued`/`running`. It renders the validated `view.result` only when `attachable` is true. The cancel button posts to `/cancel`. `release()` runs on a new document selection, a scope change, the 새 대화 button or unmount. It posts to `/abandon` with `keepalive`, and it invalidates an in-flight POST, whose late ownership record is then abandoned too. Citation markers open `EvidenceDetail`, which reads `/api/requests/{id}/evidence/{eid}`. The composer labels a follow-up as two paid calls (rewrite plus answer).
