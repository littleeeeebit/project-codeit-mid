`createClient<paths>` from `openapi-fetch` with two middlewares. `expired` sends the browser to `/api/auth/login` on any 401 outside `/api/auth/`. `sameAccount` adds `X-BidMate-Account` (the account bound by `bindAccount` after `/api/auth/me`) to every call, and reloads the page on a 409 that carries `X-BidMate-Account-Changed: 1`. That reload is how a sign-in in another tab is detected, since the tabs share one session cookie. `errorText` shows the service's Korean `detail`.
