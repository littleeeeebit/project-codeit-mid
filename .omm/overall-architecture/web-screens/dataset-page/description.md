`dataset-page.tsx`, `draft.tsx` and `review.tsx` drive gold drafting and review. The calls are `/api/drafting/documents`, `/elements`, `/slots`, `/plan`, `/start`, `/runs` and `/runs/{id}/submit`, then `/api/gold/pending`, `/api/gold/{candidate_id}` and `/decide`. The approval rule (the requester cannot approve their own drafts, and every decision needs a note) is enforced server-side in `service.gold_decide`.
