The 데이터셋 만들기 page. `draft.tsx` picks development-family documents and elements (`/api/drafting/documents`, `/elements`), builds slots (`/api/drafting/slots`), prices them (`/api/drafting/plan`) and starts paid gpt-6-luna drafting with a consented maximum (`/api/drafting/start`). It then submits a finished run's valid drafts to the review queue (`/api/drafting/runs/{id}/submit`). `review.tsx` shows a candidate next to its source spans (`GET /api/gold/{id}`) and posts an approve or reject decision with `expected_sha`, a note and categories (`POST /api/gold/{id}/decide`).
