`dataset-page.tsx`, `draft.tsx` and `review.tsx`. A verifier picks development documents and passages (`GET /api/drafting/documents` and `.../elements`) and builds slots (`POST /api/drafting/slots`). They get a free maximum (`POST /api/drafting/plan`), then start paid gpt-6-luna drafting with that consented maximum (`POST /api/drafting/start`). They watch `GET /api/drafting/runs` and submit valid drafts to the queue (`POST /api/drafting/runs/{id}/submit`). Reviewers open `GET /api/gold/{candidate_id}` and decide with `POST .../decide`. The decision carries `expected_sha`, the row version shown, so a stale view cannot decide.
