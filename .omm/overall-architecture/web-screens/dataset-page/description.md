Gold dataset authoring (dataset-page.tsx, draft.tsx, review.tsx). A verifier picks development documents and elements (`/api/drafting/documents`, `/api/drafting/documents/{doc_id}/elements`) and builds slots (`POST /api/drafting/slots`). `POST /api/drafting/plan` prices them. `POST /api/drafting/start` starts gpt-6-luna drafting with a consented maximum. `POST /api/drafting/runs/{run_id}/submit` sends valid drafts to the review queue. review.tsx shows a pending candidate (`GET /api/gold/{candidate_id}`) beside its source passages and posts a decision (`POST /api/gold/{candidate_id}/decide`).
