데이터셋 만들기. `draft.tsx` picks development documents and elements (`/api/drafting/documents`, `/elements`), builds slots (`POST /api/drafting/slots`), prices them (`POST /plan`), and starts paid drafting with the consented maximum (`POST /start`). It then sends a finished run's valid drafts to the queue (`POST /runs/{run_id}/submit`). `review.tsx` loads one candidate with its source spans (`GET /api/gold/{id}`) and decides it (`POST /api/gold/{id}/decide`) with `expected_sha`, a note, reject categories and `original_inspected`. `dataset-page.tsx` lists `/api/gold/pending` and `/api/drafting/runs`.
