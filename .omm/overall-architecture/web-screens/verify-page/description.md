The 검증 screen (`components/verify/*`) is the verifier console. `trace.tsx` runs frozen retrieval-only runs (`POST /api/verify/traces`) and can generate a paid answer from one (`/generate`). The other views are `evaluation.tsx` (plan/start a dev answer evaluation), `judges.tsx` (judge comparison), `experiments.tsx` (comparison tables and `POST /api/verify/experiments/activate`), `fidelity.tsx` (HWP fidelity findings, rendered pages, confirm), `maintenance.tsx` (start the maintenance sequence) and `records.tsx` (corrections and history).
