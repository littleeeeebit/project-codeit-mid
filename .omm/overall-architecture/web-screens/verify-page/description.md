The verification tabs, each in `web/src/components/verify/`. Traces (`trace.tsx`): `POST /api/verify/traces`, then `GET /api/verify/traces/{id}`, a paid `POST .../generate`, and `GET /api/verify/compare`. Evaluation (`evaluation.tsx`): plan and start. Judges (`judges.tsx`): progress, plan, start, results and disagreements. Experiments (`experiments.tsx`): tables, failed questions per row, and `POST /api/verify/experiments/activate`. Maintenance (`maintenance.tsx`). Fidelity (`fidelity.tsx`): confirm and page PNGs. Gold second review (`gold.tsx`). History and records (`records.tsx`). Every paid start first shows a free estimate and sends the `estimate_id` the person consented to.
