The verification screens (`web/src/components/verify/*`) cover traces, evaluation, judges, experiments, fidelity, gold review, maintenance and records. They call the `/api/verify/*` routes, which reach `service.verifier_trace`, `start_answer_evaluation`, `start_judges`, `start_maintenance`, `experiments` / `activate_experiment`, `fidelity_overview` / `confirm_fidelity` and the correction and history readers. Paid starts require a prior plan or estimate call whose estimate ID the person consents to.
