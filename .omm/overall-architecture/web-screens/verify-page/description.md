Verifier workspace. It loads /api/verify/overview, /fidelity, /ingestion and /traces. Sub-panels: trace.tsx runs free frozen retrieval traces (POST /api/verify/traces, then service.verifier_trace) and can generate a paid answer from one (POST /traces/{run_id}/generate, then service.generate_from_run_id). evaluation.tsx plans and starts development answer evaluation. judges.tsx plans and starts the Luna/Jev judge comparison and reads its results. gold.tsx records second reviews. fidelity.tsx confirms a fidelity check as sample_checked. records.tsx reads /api/verify/history and /api/requests.
