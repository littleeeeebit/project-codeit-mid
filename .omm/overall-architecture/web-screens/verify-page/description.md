The 검증 tabs. `verify-page.tsx` loads `/api/verify/overview`, `/fidelity`, `/ingestion` and `/traces`. Each tab component calls its own routes: `trace.tsx` runs frozen retrieval traces (`POST /api/verify/traces`), compares two of them and generates a paid answer from one (`/traces/{run_id}/generate`). `experiments.tsx` reads comparison tables and calls `POST /api/verify/experiments/activate`, which switches serving. `evaluation.tsx` and `judges.tsx` follow a plan-then-start pattern: they price first, then start a background job against a stored estimate ID. `maintenance.tsx` starts and polls the maintenance sequence. `gold.tsx` and `fidelity.tsx` record second reviews and fidelity confirmations.
