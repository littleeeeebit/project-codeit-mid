The 데이터셋 만들기 page (dataset-page.tsx) lists pending candidates (`/api/gold/pending`) and drafting runs. draft.tsx picks development documents and elements, builds slots, prices them (`/api/drafting/plan`), starts a paid drafting run with the consented maximum (`/api/drafting/start`) and submits valid drafts to the queue. review.tsx shows a candidate beside its source passages and posts `/api/gold/{id}/decide` with a mandatory note.
