resolve() pins each ref to a full commit. checkout() adds a detached worktree under .runtime/review/worktrees/; for the working tree it copies changed and untracked files and records changed_sha256. read_variant() validates an optional review-variant.json, and candidate_config() applies it over the frozen activation (mode, embedding, reranker, limits, dense_version, chunk profile, or generation reasoning_effort and max_output_tokens). A candidate needing CUDA on a host without it is marked refused. The worktree is always removed in `finally`.
