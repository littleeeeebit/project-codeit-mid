`stage_review.run` resolves the refs (`.` means HEAD plus uncommitted and untracked files, hashed into `changed_sha256`). It creates `<data>/review/runs/<stage>-<UTC>-<hex6>/` and freezes the inputs. For the retriever: `activation.json`, `questions.json` (dev population on its own documents plus the needle set over the corpus), `rows.json` and `documents.json`. For chunking: `documents.json.gz` of serving-index elements. Generation and OCR are delegated to `stage_review_paid.freeze`. `--inputs-from` copies another run's inputs. It copies `stage_review_worker.py` into the run as `worker.py`. For each ref it calls `checkout`, `read_variant` (`review-variant.json`), `candidate_config`, a refusal when a GPU is needed on a host without CUDA, and `execute`, and always removes the worktree. Free stages are scored immediately (`score_retriever`, `score_chunking`) into `view.json`. Paid stages write `run.json` with `state: needs_approval` and no `view.json`, so the app does not list them yet.
