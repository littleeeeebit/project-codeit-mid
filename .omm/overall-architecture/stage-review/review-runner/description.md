`stage_review.run` resolves the refs (`.` means the working tree, including uncommitted files), freezes the inputs (`freeze`: serving activation, index, vectors, dev and needle questions; or the served elements for chunking) or copies them with `--inputs-from`, and copies the worker script into the run folder. For each candidate it checks out a worktree, reads an optional `review-variant.json`, and refuses with a reason any GPU-needing candidate on a host without CUDA. It runs `execute`, and always removes the worktree. Free stages are scored at once (`score_retriever`/`score_chunking`). Paid stages render OCR images, write `run.json` with `state=needs_approval` and stop unpriced. `reprice` then prices them on a fresh ledger connection, so a dropped connection after an hour of local reads costs only a re-price.
