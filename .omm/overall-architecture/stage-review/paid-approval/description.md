`approve` records the approver in the run and the audit log. It refuses estimates older than 24 h or ones that no longer fit the envelope. `resume` requires approval, `recheck`s inputs, worker, rate card, refs and remaining budget, refuses a ref that moved or a working tree that changed since pricing, and only then injects the key (`approved_env`). Idempotency keys `<run-id>:<candidate>:<question>:<n>` and the ledger let a rerun skip billed items. A billed answer whose output was lost is shown lost and is never repriced. A run stays `stopped` until every approved candidate completes. `--ledger-dsn-env` points the ledger at codeit's database through a tunnel (`on_ledger`).
