Sign-in settings come only from the process environment (`BIDMATE_HUB_URL`, `BIDMATE_OAUTH_CLIENT_ID`, `BIDMATE_OAUTH_CLIENT_SECRET`, `BIDMATE_OAUTH_REDIRECT_URI`, `BIDMATE_ALLOWED_USERS`). If any is missing, every /api call returns 503. Setting `BIDMATE_LOCAL_MEMBER` together with the hub settings is also refused. When the VM's public IP changes, BIDMATE_HUB_URL, BIDMATE_OAUTH_REDIRECT_URI and the hub's oauth_redirect_uri must be updated together (runbook 3.1).
