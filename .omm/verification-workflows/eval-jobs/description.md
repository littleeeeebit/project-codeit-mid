Development answer evaluation: POST /api/verify/evaluation/plan (`answers.plan_run('answer-finalists', 'dev')`) then /start with the estimate id. Sealed runs are refused from the UI. Judges: /api/verify/judges/plan, then /start. Maintenance: /api/verify/maintenance/start. One answer-evaluation or judge job runs at a time (`_EVAL_LOCK`), and maintenance has its own single slot. Answer evaluation runs through the service's own answer path with the finalist's retrieval configuration pinned, charged to `gold_eval` (per its docstring and the README; answers.py was not read).
