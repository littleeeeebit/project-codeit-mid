`/api/gold/pending` lists development candidates only. `/api/gold/{id}` → `candidate_review` returns the candidate with its cited spans cut from the source elements. `/api/gold/{id}/decide` → `service.gold_decide` → `gold.decide`, which applies the independence rules: the drafter, the person who started drafting, or the drafting model cannot approve. `/api/gold/{id}/second-review` → `gold.second_review`. `gold check` (CLI) verifies that the dataset files and wiki pages equal their database rendering.
