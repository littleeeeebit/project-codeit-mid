`plan_drafting` returns a free maximum. `start_drafting` refuses if the maximum grew past the consented amount or does not fit, then runs `drafting._generate` in a thread, writing to `.runtime/.../drafts/<run_id>/output` (`candidates.jsonl`, `invalid.json`, `receipt.json`). `submit_drafts` sends a completed run's valid rows to `gold.submit` as one batch with `drafted_by = drafting.DRAFTER`, and refuses a second submission. The internals of `drafting.py` were not read in detail; the description relies on the `service.py` call sites and docstrings.
