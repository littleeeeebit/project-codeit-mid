How development gold questions are produced without a person reading whole documents. The model drafts source-bound questions under a consented maximum charged to `gold_eval`. Drafts enter the review queue as a batch attributed to the model. A different person approves or rejects each one with a note while viewing the draft next to its original passages. Approved rows become the datasets that frozen runs and answer evaluation score. Rejections feed a rejection wiki with inferred lessons for later drafting.
