Approved rows are rendered from the database into the dataset JSONL (`dataset_path`: `.runtime/datasets/dev.jsonl`, `dev-pilot`, or sealed `.runtime/sealed/test.jsonl`). Rejections are rendered as wiki pages under the rejection directory, with an index. `validate-gold` and `freeze-dataset` read these files for evaluation; the validator rejects unreviewed or self-approved rows, missing quotes, families that cross splits, and unverified negatives.
