review.tsx loads one candidate (GET /api/gold/{candidate_id} → `service.candidate_review`, with its original passages, spans, PDF pages and metadata) and the rejection categories. It posts the decision with the row sha it saw, the categories, a note, `original_inspected` and `disputed` (POST /api/gold/{candidate_id}/decide). The verify gold tab handles second review (POST /api/gold/{candidate_id}/second-review).
