With `app_settings.active_run` absent, the keyword default serves over `active_index`. `activate_decision` replaces the activation atomically and appends to `activations`. At read time, `active_serving` may degrade a stored activation: a changed corpus route, an unknown embedding model, or a stale reranker policy. Per request, `retrieve` may also fall back: the dense set fails verification, no query vector is available, or the reranker errors. Each degradation is recorded in limitations or `fallback`.
