`gold_candidates.status`: `pending` (from `gold.submit`) moves to `approved` or `rejected` through `gold.decide`, where the first decision wins. `decide` requires the `row_sha256` the reviewer saw. A drafter or drafting model cannot approve its own row. Gold-2 approvals must state `original_inspected`. A rejection needs categories (plus a note for 'other'). An approval rewrites the dataset JSONL from database rows; a rejection writes a rejection wiki page. `second_review` on an approved gold row appends agree or disagree from a third person; a disagreement keeps the row out of valid gold. Sealed (`test`) candidates are refused outside the owner CLI (`_sealed_refusal`).
