`gold_candidates.status` moves pending → approved or rejected. The first decision wins and must match the row's `row_sha256` as the reviewer saw it (gold.decide, gold.py:484). The drafter, and for LLM drafts the drafting model, cannot approve. Gold-2 approvals require `original_inspected`. Rejections need at least one category, and a note for 'other'. Approval rewrites the dataset JSONL from the database; rejection writes a rejection-wiki page. `second_review` (gold.py:536) appends an agree or disagree verdict from a third person; a disagreement keeps the row out of valid gold. Sealed (`test`) candidates are refused through the web and decided only through the owner CLI (`gold show/decide/second-review`).
