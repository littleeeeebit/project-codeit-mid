Only the process holding `pg_try_advisory_lock(GATEWAY_LOCK)` may dispatch. Acquisition in `Resources._own` runs recovery: `recover_requests` (queued/running → interrupted) and `budget.recover` (dispatching → unknown, reserved → released; the same for Gemini `external_attempts`). Ownership is checked again at reserve (`require_owner` + `owner_guard`), at dispatch (`owner_guard`) and inside the transport (`owner_check`). Loss is permanent for that process, and further dispatch needs a restart. `Resources.close` releases the lock only after workers stop and the transport closes.
