`gold_candidates.status` goes from pending (`gold.submit`) to approved or rejected (`gold.decide`). A decision needs a note, and the reviewer must differ from the drafter and from whoever started the drafting run. Approved rows marked disputed wait for `second_review`. `gold repin` moves pending rows to a new extraction, and `pending_invalid` rows fail the current checks. Approved rows are re-rendered into the dataset JSONL, and retrieval runs score only reviewed development rows.
