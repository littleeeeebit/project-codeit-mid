`gold_candidates.status` is pending → approved | rejected, and the first decision wins (`gold.decide`). Decide checks the `expected_sha` the reviewer saw, a required note, and that the drafter and the drafting model never approve their own row. For gold-2 rows the reviewer must state `original_inspected`. The service additionally refuses approval by whoever started the drafting run (`requested_by` in `drafts/<run>/request.json`). Approval rewrites the dataset projection (`datasets/<name>.jsonl`). Rejection writes a rejection-wiki page that needs an inferred cause (`gold infer`) before any further drafting (`drafting.lessons` raises otherwise). `disputed` approvals wait for `second_review`; a disagreement keeps the row out of valid gold. Sealed (`test`) candidates are refused everywhere except the owner CLI (`include_sealed`). `gold_reviews` logs every decision.
