`gold_candidates.status` moves from `pending` to `approved` or `rejected` through `gold.decide`. The first decision wins, guarded by `expected_sha` (the row version the reviewer saw). The drafter and the drafting model cannot approve their own rows. A gold approval requires `original_inspected`. A rejection requires categories (and a note for `other`). Approval rewrites the dataset JSONL from the database; rejection writes a rejection wiki page. `second_review` appends an agree or disagree record, and a disagreement keeps the row out of valid gold. Sealed rows are reviewed only through the owner CLI.
