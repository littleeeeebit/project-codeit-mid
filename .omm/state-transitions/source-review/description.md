`sources.review_status` values: `unreviewed` (every new extraction revision), `auto_verified` or `auto_flagged` (fidelity verdict), `sample_checked` or `reviewed` (a person: `confirm_fidelity`, `import-reviews`, `review record`), and `needs_recovery` (quarantined). `extraction_review_status` replays reviews and fidelity verdicts; a person's positive review outranks an automatic verdict. `build_keyword_index --reviewed-only` admits only auto_verified, sample_checked and reviewed sources. The operating index also admits unreviewed and auto_flagged sources and labels them (`index_includes_unreviewed_sources`, `source_unreviewed` warnings).
