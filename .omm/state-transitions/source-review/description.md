The `sources.parse_status` and `review_status` columns. A parse failure becomes `quarantined` with `needs_recovery`. A parse that produces a new extraction revision resets review to `unreviewed`; an identical revision keeps it. `fidelity.verify_source` sets `auto_verified` or `auto_flagged` only when no human status (`HUMAN_STATUSES`) is present. Human statuses (`sample_checked`, `reviewed`) come from `record_review`, `import_reviews` or `confirm_fidelity`, and are never overwritten automatically. `recover_source` turns a quarantined original into a new `unreviewed` revision from an approved PDF conversion. `build_keyword_index --reviewed-only` admits `auto_verified`, `sample_checked` and `reviewed`. The operating index also admits `unreviewed` and `auto_flagged`, and labels them `index_includes_unreviewed_sources`.
