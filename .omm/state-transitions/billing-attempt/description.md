The `attempts.state` machine documented at the top of src/rfp_assistant/gateway/budget.py. `reserved → dispatching` (`mark_dispatching`) or `reserved → released` (guard refusal, missing provider, restart recovery). `dispatching → settled` (`settle` with usage), `dispatching → unknown` (`mark_unknown` on timeout, connection loss, missing usage or settlement failure; `recover` on restart), or `dispatching → released` only with `confirmed_pre_execution=True` after a 4xx. `unknown → settled` (`settle_from_evidence`) or `unknown → reconciled` (`reconcile` against a provider total over an interval). `reconciled → settled` inserts a compensating negative adjustment. Open states (`reserved`, `dispatching`, `unknown`) count against the cap at their reservation.
