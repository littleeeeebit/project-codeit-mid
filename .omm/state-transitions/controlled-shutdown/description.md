`systemctl restart bidmate`, from the updater or an operator, sends SIGTERM. Uvicorn stops, and `Resources.close()` runs through the threading atexit hook: it stops accepting work, waits for workers, marks rows interrupted, joins jobs and releases the gateway lock. Because `open_paid_work` refused the update while anything was open, a self-update restart should find no paid work. Free requests are not fenced and may be cut off.
