There are three conditions: the process holds the advisory lock (owner), another `Resources` in the process shares it (borrowed), or no owner exists. A ledger-only `Resources(dispatch=False)` takes no ownership and refuses paid stages; its docstring describes it as the budget-administration owner beside the serving app. Ownership becomes `lost` when the lock disappears or the session errors. It never recovers in-process; restart and reconcile unknown billing. Every admission and dispatch transaction re-checks it with `owner_guard`.
