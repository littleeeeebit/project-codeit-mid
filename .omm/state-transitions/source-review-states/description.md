`sources.parse_status` is parsed or quarantined (`ingest_source` sets quarantined with `review_status='needs_recovery'` and a reason_code). `review_status` takes the values unreviewed, auto_verified, auto_flagged, sample_checked and reviewed. A new extraction revision resets it to `unreviewed`, because parsed does not mean faithful. `fidelity run` sets an automatic verdict, but `record_review` from `confirm_fidelity` or `import-reviews` records a human `sample_checked`/`reviewed` that an automatic status never overwrites (per README). `build_keyword_index` admits auto_verified, sample_checked and reviewed sources, plus unreviewed and auto_flagged with `--include-unreviewed` (the operating index). `open_evidence` attaches `source_unreviewed`, `source_auto_flagged` or `source_needs_recovery` warnings.
