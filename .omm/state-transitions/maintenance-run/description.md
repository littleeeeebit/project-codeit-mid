`maintenance.run` state: overall `running` → `complete`, `unverified` (every step ran, but re-parsing cost pinned questions: `needs_evidence_review`), `failed` (first failing step, with reason and traceback), or `needs_approval` (a paid embedding stopped at its estimate). Steps are `pending`, `running`, `done`, `reused`, `skipped`, `failed` or `needs_approval`. `service.maintenance_status` reports `interrupted` when the stored state says running but no live thread exists. Reports go to `.runtime/maintenance/reports/<run_id>.json|md`.
