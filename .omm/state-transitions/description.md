The lifecycle of a `requests` row (`requests.status`). The answer's domain status (`result.status`: `answered`, `insufficient_evidence`, `budget_blocked`, `clarification_required`, `technical_error`, `ingestion_unavailable`, …) is separate from the request status. `budget_blocked` and `insufficient_evidence` end as `completed`, while a `ServiceError` or a provider or validation error ends as `failed`. The terminal states are completed, failed, cancelled and interrupted, and every one of them may still have open billing (see `billing-states`).
