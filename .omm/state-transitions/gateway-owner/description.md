`GatewayOwner` takes `pg_try_advisory_lock(GATEWAY_LOCK)` on a dedicated autocommit connection. Within one process, other `Resources` objects (for example `PinnedResources` in evaluation, or ledger-only owners) share it through `borrow_owner`, and it is released with the last user. `check()` and `owner_guard()` confirm the lock is still granted and that recovery and validation are ready. A lost session sets `lost=True` permanently: there is no reconnect, and a new owner must recover. Taking ownership triggers `recover_requests` and `budget.recover`.
