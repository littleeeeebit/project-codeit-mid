`UpdateWatch.fenced()` is true while `accepting` (between the paid-work check and the marker write) or `in_progress()` (the marker exists, or `result.json` says running for under 30 minutes). `RequestRunner.try_admit`, `_start_job`, `start_drafting` and `start_maintenance` all check it under `_runner_lock`. `request_update` refuses while `open_paid_work` reports any admitted slot, active row, live job, or open or unknown attempt. A restart starts a new process that stays fenced while the result still says running.
