`UpdateWatch.fenced()` is true when `accepting` is set or `in_progress()` is true. `in_progress()` means the marker file exists, or `result.json` says `running` and is less than 30 minutes old. `request_update` raises `accepting` under `_runner_lock` before `open_paid_work` counts work. Work admitted earlier is counted and makes the update refuse, and work arriving later is refused by the fence. The fence comes down when the updater ends without a restart. A restarted process stays fenced while `result.json` still says running.
