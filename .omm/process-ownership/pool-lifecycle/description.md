postgres.lifecycle is entered by Resources (store.database_lifecycle) or by cli.main. It probes the DSN once with require_server (PostgreSQL 18.6), opens a bounded ConnectionPool whose configure step requires pgvector 0.8.6 and sets lock_timeout 5 s and statement_timeout 60 s, and reference-counts users. open_db raises if no owner entered the lifecycle or the DSN changed while owned.
