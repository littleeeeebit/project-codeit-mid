`stage_review.run` resolves the base ref and each `--cand` ref ('.' means the working tree) and creates `<stage>-<UTC>-<hex6>`. It freezes the inputs, or copies them with `--inputs-from`, and copies `stage_review_worker.py` into the run as `worker.py`. For each candidate it reads `review-variant.json` and builds `candidate_config` from the frozen activation plus the variant; it refuses unknown keys and models outside the registry. One shared scorer grades every candidate (`score_retriever`: nDCG@5 and needle top-5; `score_chunking`: sizes and table titles). A candidate that fails stays in the run with its reason and never costs the others their results.
