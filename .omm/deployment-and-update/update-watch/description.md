`UpdateWatch.from_env` reads `BIDMATE_UPDATE_REQUEST` and `BIDMATE_UPDATE_STATE_DIR`. Without both, the watch is unconfigured and no update is offered. The API lifespan starts and stops its poll thread. `last_result()` reads the updater's `result.json`, and a `running` result older than 30 minutes counts as cut off. `update_status` combines the check, `open_paid_work`, `in_progress()` and the last result for the banner.
