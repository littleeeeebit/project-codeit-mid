`maintenance.run` is reached from `cli maintain --actor` or `POST /api/verify/maintenance/start` (`service.start_maintenance`, one daemon thread, one per process, using the server's transport). It runs `STEPS` in order, writes `.runtime/maintenance/state.json` after each step, and ends with a report under `maintenance/reports/<run_id>.json|md`. A step raising `StepFailed` stops the run as `failed`. A paid embedding or regression row raises `NeedsApproval` and stops at its estimate. `closing()` stops it when the service shuts down, and the status endpoint reports a `running` state without a live thread as `interrupted`. It never activates anything: the report compares the serving config before and after and records provider calls made.
