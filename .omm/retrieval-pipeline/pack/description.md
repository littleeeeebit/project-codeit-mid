`_pack_evidence` walks the final ranking, with exact matches first. It admits a chunk unless the chunk is for another asked requirement code, the unit limit (`evidence_max_units`, 10 when serving) is reached, it overlaps an already picked span, or it would exceed the token budget (`evidence_max_tokens` for the first unit, `evidence_target_tokens` after). It then pulls the linked sibling pieces of split elements or rows, nearest first and up to `LINKED_EXTRA_UNITS`, and records `linked_evidence_missing:` when a piece does not fit. It returns the evidence units, the exclusions with reasons, and the tokens used.
