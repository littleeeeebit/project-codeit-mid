Only for scopes larger than two documents, which in practice means corpus mode. `route_corpus` keeps the documents whose frozen title and institution terms the question restates: at least two terms, weighted by IDF, with each document weighed greedily only on terms not already explained by a better match. Generic words found in four or more titles cannot qualify a document on their own. Routing is skipped when nothing, or more than `ROUTE_MAX` documents, match. The rule is recorded as `corpus_route_record()`, and an activated run that measured another rule stops serving.
