# Daily collaboration log for 9/30–10/9 (입찰메이트 / BidMate) drafted from the wiki

The user asked for a daily collaboration log (협업일지) covering every day from 9/30 to today, written from the wiki. Each day needed six columns: work or collaboration content, progress %, basis for the progress figure, insights or problems found, difficulties, and goals or to-dos. The assistant produced one entry per day from 9/30 to 10/9. Its progress percentages are its own estimate, and the user had not responded to the draft when the conversation ended.

## Facts

- The project is 입찰메이트 (BidMate), an internal RAG Q&A system built on 100 real RFPs; 96 of the 100 originals are HWP (unverified)
- No document in the wiki records a progress percentage; the log's percentages (15% on 9/30 rising to 84% on 10/9) are the assistant's estimate, counting the four build phases as 60% and Phase 5 plus the final report and presentation as 40% (unverified)
- Commit and merge dates in KST sometimes fall a day later than decision-record dates, which use UTC (unverified)
- Six people share a $20 API budget for more than three weeks, which is why billing uses reserve-then-settle (unverified)
- The owner rejected Streamlit; the frontend is FastAPI + Next.js, with the pages 질문하기, 검증, 데이터셋 만들기 and 실험 비교 (unverified)
- Kiwi morpheme-based BM25 beat standard BM25: nDCG@5 0.95 vs 0.67 (unverified)
- pgvector indexes are limited to 2,000 dimensions, so 3-large's 3,072-dimension embeddings were cut to 1,536 (unverified)
- `keyword_first` fusion, which keeps BM25's top 6 results in place, reached nDCG@5 0.9405; exact search was kept because HNSW recall@20 stayed below 0.99 even at ef_search 400 (unverified)
- SQLite was removed; storage is PostgreSQL only, and the pilot lives in a separate PostgreSQL archive DB (PR #16) (unverified)
- Local Langfuse runs on 127.0.0.2 because sharing 127.0.0.1 with another Langfuse broke login: browser cookies ignore ports (unverified)
- The output token limit was raised from 2,000 to 4,000 because reasoning tokens used up the limit (unverified)
- The service is deployed on the team VM `codeit`, with JupyterHub-account login on :8501 limited to 6 members (PR #22, PR #25) (unverified)
- The team key returns 429 for 3-large and can reach only gpt-5-mini and gpt-5-nano (unverified)
- Comparison run `E-9b785d7cd167` covered 12 configurations × 35 questions and cost $1.93 against a $3 cap; KURE-v1 and BGE-m3-ko each scored 28/35 vs 24/35 for keyword-only, with p=0.34, which is not significant (unverified)
- Serving was switched to `H-5b2565f606`, the BGE-m3-ko hybrid configuration chosen by the owner; gpt-5-mini + BGE-m3-ko became the defaults (PR #29) (unverified)
- Verification flows had failed at `import psycopg` because they launched base Anaconda Python (unverified)
- A mis-quoted cleanup command deleted `/srv/bidmate` on codeit; the DB was untouched, and 98 originals, 624 extraction files and 30 index manifests were restored (unverified)
- The automatic fidelity check flagged 58 documents for review (unverified)
